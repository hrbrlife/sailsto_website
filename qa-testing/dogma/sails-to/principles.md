## What Sails.to IS

**Compliant Sovereign Tokenized and Classic Securities Issuance and OTC Trading Platform.**

That sentence is the canonical identity. Every word matters:
- **Compliant** — not renegade, not DeFi, not permissionless
- **Sovereign** — you control your data, your AI, your infrastructure
- **Tokenized and Classic** — not just crypto tokens; real securities too
- **Securities** — bonds, shares, RWA — regulated financial instruments
- **Issuance** — the primary market: creating and distributing securities
- **OTC Trading** — the secondary market: broker-mediated, not exchange-traded
- **Platform** — infrastructure, not a service provider doing it for you

### What Sails.to IS NOT

- NOT a broker-dealer, investment adviser, or fiduciary
- NOT a custodian of investor funds or securities
- NOT a party to any transaction — the issuer is the self-issuer
- Sails.to is a **Technology Infrastructure Provider** that provides templated
  IT and legal wrapper infrastructure to issuers, working together with a
  **licensed trust company** for compliance oversight and trust services
- No ability to buy or sell anything on sails.to directly
- Investors access the platform ONLY via regulated licensed brokers

## The Core Truths of Sails.to

These are non-negotiable. Every page must reinforce (never contradict) them.

### 1. Securities that cross the line
CrossSecurities can be held on-chain as tokens OR via traditional bank custody
as ISIN-numbered instruments — and crossed between forms at will. This is THE
core differentiator. "Cross the line between TradFi and DeFi." The word
"Classic" matters — these are real securities, not just tokens.

### 2. NOT an exchange — a broker-mediated OTC network
Investors trade WITH their broker. Brokers trade with each other. The broker
is always the counterparty of record. This is how institutional securities
markets work. It is legally critical — exchanges require different licenses.

### 3. Compliant, not renegade
This is NOT DeFi. Not permissionless. Not trustless. Sails.to is compliant
market infrastructure built on blockchain rails. Every participant has a
credential. Every transaction has a counterparty. Every action is auditable.

### 4. Sovereign infrastructure
Each broker, issuer, and trust company controls their own stack.
Compliance is self-hosted, not outsourced. Your data stays on your server.
The platform provides infrastructure; you run the compliance. Sovereignty
means full control over your data, your AI, and your Web3 presence.

### 5. DAO LLC for near-instant legal structure
Wyoming Series LLC DAO or Marshall Islands DAO LLC — legal entities that
map cleanly to on-chain governance. One Series = one issuance = one security.
Near-instant setup, not months of legal drafting. The issuing SPV is a
Series of a Wyoming DAO LLC controlled by the issuer — Sails.to is just
the technology provider.

#### DAO LLC Compartment Structure
Each issuance DAO LLC has four interconnected Series compartments:
- **Issuer Series** — the issuing entity, controlled by the issuer
- **Locked CrossSecurities Series** — holds locked/escrowed tokens
- **Cash Accumulation Series** — accumulates cash due to bondholders
- **Guarantee Series** — holds 3% of issuance under trust for the bond,
  used for legal defense expenses or proceedings against the issuer

All guarantees are under trust and administered by a licensed trust company.
Disputes are resolved via arbitration (mandatory in all PPMs).

### 6. Soulbound credentials — identity, not tokens
KYC/AML credentials are non-transferable, revocable, renewable NFTs.
They represent trust assertions, not universal identities. Different brokers
may issue different credentials to the same investor.

### 7. Clearstream + ISIN integration
CrossConversion bridges on-chain and traditional settlement. Clearstream XS
provides global custody. ISIN numbers make tokens bankable. Optional Vienna
MTF listing for exchange-traded liquidity. 0.75% conversion fee.

### 8. Solana blockchain
Currently built on Solana. The credential hierarchy and fee model work
consistently on-chain.

### 9. For professionals, not retail
Sails.to serves professional investors, accredited investors, qualified
purchasers, and institutional participants. This is not a retail platform.
Every use of "anyone" or "everyone" should be qualified.

### 10. The Broker Hop is by design
When an investor buys: Investor → their Broker → source Broker → Issuer.
On-chain settlement shows Broker A → Broker B → Client. The client never
transacts directly with the market. This preserves exemption integrity.
Each broker operates their own regulated instance via `sails.broker.tld`
white-label platform. Brokers have a **duty of care** — checking suitability
of investment products and whether clients are QIC'd (Qualified Investor
Certified). US broker for US investors, Mauritius broker for Mauritius
investors — jurisdiction-matched.

## Red Flags — Always Flag These

- Calling sails.to a "DEX" or "exchange"
- Implying retail/public access to securities
- Using "trustless" or "permissionless"
- Overpromising liquidity or returns
- Hiding the broker-mediation model
- Making Melusina OS too prominent (sails.to is its own product — minimize Melusina references)
- Stating specific app counts (the library is growing)
- Claiming any product is "live" or publicly available (all products are in private testing)
- Referring to TON blockchain (Solana only)
