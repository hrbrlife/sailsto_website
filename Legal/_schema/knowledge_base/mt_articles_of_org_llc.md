For a Professional Limited Liability Company, at least half of the managers must be qualified persons with respect to the limited
liability company. (35-8-1303, MCA)

ANNUAL REPORTS

Annual reports must be filed with the Secretary of State prior to April 15 each year beginning the year following the organization and
each year thereafter. Each year the Secretary of State will mail a notice that the Annual Report is due to the Limited Liability
Company’s registered agent.
