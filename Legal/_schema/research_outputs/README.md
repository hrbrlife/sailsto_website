## Classification

These are **SECONDARY SOURCES** - pre-processed research memos. They carry reduced evidentiary weight compared to:
- Primary statutes (USC, CFR, MCA)
- Official regulator guidance (FinCEN, state regulators)
- Case law from official reporters
- Official government forms and instructions

## Weight Scale

| Source Type | Weight | Use In Audit |

| Primary statute/regulation | 1.0 | Direct citation required |

| **LLM Research Output** | **0.5** | **Cross-reference required** |

## Usage

These outputs support but do not replace primary sources in `knowledge_base/`.

Before citing in audit findings:
1. Verify URLs are accessible and accurate
2. Cross-reference claims to primary KB sources
3. Check generation date (re-validate if >6 months old)
