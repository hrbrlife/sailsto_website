## ARTICLE 3: SERIES ASSETS AND LIABILITY SHIELD

**3.1 Separate Assets:**

The Series shall maintain completely separate assets from:
- Company generally
- All other Issuer Series

Series assets include:
- Investor subscription proceeds
- Interest, dividends, or distributions on Series investments
- Use of proceeds assets (equipment, inventory, receivables, IP)
- Series bank accounts and cryptocurrency wallets
- Physical securities certificates and ISIN records

**3.2 Series Liabilities:**

Series liabilities include:
- Securities principal and interest owed to investors
- Service provider fees (payment processors, custodians, transfer agents)
- Platform fees owed to Company per Pricing Schedule [DOC:C3]
- Regulatory fees (SEC filings, Blue Sky notices, ISIN numbering)
- Legal fees for Series-specific matters
- Taxes allocable to Series

**3.3 Liability Shield:**

ASSETS OF THIS SERIES ARE NOT LIABLE FOR:
- Debts of Company generally
- Debts of any other Issuer Series
- Personal debts of Series Manager
- Personal debts of Company Members

ASSETS OF COMPANY AND OTHER SERIES ARE NOT LIABLE FOR:
- Debts of this Series
- Securities principal and interest owed by this Series
- Obligations arising from this Series' operations

**3.4 Separateness Requirements:**

To maintain liability shield, Series shall:

(a) **Separate Accounting:** Maintain separate QuickBooks or accounting system;
(b) **Separate Bank Accounts:** No commingling with Company or other Series funds;
(c) **Separate Contracts:** All contracts clearly identify Series I-XXX as party;
(d) **Separate Records:** Maintain separate files, correspondence, and documentation;
(e) **Separate Tax Returns:** File separate Form 1065 (if multi-member) or Schedule C (if single-member);
(f) **Separate Filings:** File separate Form D with SEC and state Blue Sky notices;
(g) **Identify Series on Documents:** All documents, checks, invoices, agreements state "Sails DAO LLC - Issuer Series I-XXX";
(h) **No Guarantees:** Series does not guarantee Company or other Series obligations;
(i) **Independent Decision-Making:** Series Manager makes Series-specific decisions independently.

## ARTICLE 5: SECURITIES OFFERING PROCESS

**5.1 Private Placement Memorandum (PPM):**

Series shall prepare PPM containing:

(a) **Offering Summary:** Securities type, terms, amount, timeline;
(b) **Use of Proceeds:** Detailed breakdown of fund usage;
(c) **Risk Factors:** Material risks specific to offering and Series;
(d) **Series Business:** Description of Series operations and management;
(e) **Financial Projections:** Pro forma financials (if applicable);
(6) **Tax Considerations:** Federal and state tax implications;
(g) **Securities Terms:** Full terms and conditions of securities;
(h) **Investor Suitability:** Accredited investor requirements;
(i) **Transfer Restrictions:** Rule 144 holding periods and restrictions;
(j) **Subscription Procedures:** How to invest, minimum amounts, payment methods.

PPM reviewed by:
- Company General Counsel
- Company CCO
- External securities counsel (recommended)

**5.2 Form D Filing:**

Series shall file Form D with SEC:

(a) **Timing:** Within 15 days of first sale of securities;
(b) **Content:** Issuer info, offering terms, securities type, exemption claimed;
(c) **Filing Method:** Electronic filing via EDGAR;
(d) **Amendments:** File amendments within 30 days of material changes or annually;
(e) **State Filings:** File state Form D in states where offering conducted.

**5.3 Blue Sky Compliance:**

Series shall comply with state securities laws:

(a) **Notice Filing States:** File notice + consent to service + fee in states where offering conducted and state requires notice filing;

(b) **Merit Review States:** Comply with substantive review requirements or claim federal preemption under NSMIA;

(c) **Exemption Claims:** Document basis for claimed exemptions (federal preemption, intrastate, etc.);

(d) **State Form Ds:** File state Form D in states requiring separate filing;

(e) **Ongoing Compliance:** File annual renewals or amendments as required by states.

States and filing requirements documented in Blue Sky Compliance Table [DOC:N3].

**5.4 Investor Verification:**

All investors must complete:

(a) **Accredited Investor Verification (if Rule 506(c) or required):**
   - Method 1: Tax returns (past 2 years) + reasonable belief of current year
   - Method 2: W-2s, 1099s, bank statements showing income
   - Method 3: CPA letter certifying accredited status
   - Method 4: Third-party verification service (AngelList, VerifyInvestor.com)

(b) **Subscription Agreement:** Investor signs Subscription Agreement [DOC:F2] containing:
   - Investor representations and warranties
   - Accredited investor self-certification
   - Investment risk acknowledgments
   - Transfer restriction acceptances

(c) **OFAC Screening:** Payment processor screens investor against SDN list in real-time;

(d) **KYC/AML:** Collection of government-issued ID, proof of address, verification of identity.

**5.5 Payment Processing (Agent-of-Payee):**

Investor subscription payments processed via agent-of-payee model:

```
FLOW:
1. Investor completes subscription agreement
2. Investor wires/ACH funds to Payment Processor
   (Payment Processor acting as agent of Series I-XXX)
3. Payment Processor screens investor against OFAC SDN list
4. Payment Processor holds funds in escrow pending closing
5. Upon offering close:
   a. Payment Processor releases funds to Series bank account
   b. Payment Processor remits fees per Pricing Schedule to Company
   c. Transfer Agent records securities ownership
   d. Physical certificate printed with ISIN and delivered to investor
      (or held by Qualified Custodian)
```

**Payment Processor Partners:**
- Stripe Connect (ACH, wire, credit card)
- [Other processors per Company agreements]

**Payment Processor Agreement:** Per Partner Payment Processor Agreement [DOC:N1] containing:
- Agency relationship (processor = agent of Series)
- OFAC screening (real-time SDN list checks)
- Fee structure (cost + [15-40]% markup per volume tier)
- International data protection (GDPR SCCs if applicable)

**5.6 Securities Issuance:**

Upon payment receipt and clearing:

(a) **Transfer Agent Records:** Transfer Agent (Company or third-party) records:
   - Investor name and contact information
   - Securities ownership (number of shares/principal amount)
   - Certificate number and issue date

(b) **Physical Certificate:** Physical securities certificate printed containing:

(c) **Custody Options:**
   - **Investor Custody:** Certificate mailed to investor's address

   - **Electronic Entry:** Certificate held in electronic form by transfer agent (book entry)

## ARTICLE 11: TERM AND DISSOLUTION

**11.1 Term:**

Series continues in existence until:

(a) Maturity date of securities (if debt securities);
(b) Complete liquidation and distribution (if equity securities);
(c) Series Manager determination that Series purpose achieved;
(d) Investor vote to dissolve (if required by securities terms);
(e) Company Member vote to dissolve Series;
(f) Judicial dissolution;
(g) Administrative dissolution for non-compliance.

**11.2 Dissolution Process:**

Upon dissolution, Series shall:

(a) **Cease Business:** Stop conducting new business (continue winding up activities);

(b) **Notify Stakeholders:**
   - Written notice to all investors
   - Notice to SEC (Form D amendment)
   - Notice to state securities regulators
   - Notice to service providers

(c) **Liquidate Assets:**
   - Sell or distribute Series assets
   - Collect receivables
   - Settle litigation

(d) **Pay Obligations:**
   - Priority 1: Series creditors and service providers
   - Priority 2: Securities principal and accrued interest/dividends
   - Priority 3: Remaining assets per securities terms

(e) **Final Reporting:**
   - Final financial statements to investors
   - Final tax returns
   - Transfer records to archive

(f) **File Dissolution Documents:** (if required by Wyoming law)

**11.3 Distribution Upon Dissolution:**

Assets distributed in priority:

1. **Creditors:** Payment processor, custodian, transfer agent, other vendors
2. **Securities Principal:** Return of investor principal (debt securities)
3. **Accrued Interest/Dividends:** Unpaid interest or dividends
4. **Liquidation Preference:** Preferred shareholders (if applicable)
5. **Pro Rata Distribution:** Remaining assets to equity holders pro rata

**11.4 Post-Dissolution:**

After dissolution:
- Series may prosecute/defend lawsuits to completion
- Records maintained for 5 years minimum per regulation
- Series Manager continues until all winding up activities complete
