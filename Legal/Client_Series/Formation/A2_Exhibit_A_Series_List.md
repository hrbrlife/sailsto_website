## SECTION 1: SERIES CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

1.1. The [TERM:Company] uses a standardized classification system for all series. Each series is identified by a prefix letter and number that indicates its functional category.

1.2. Series with the prefix T followed by a number (such as [TERM:Series_T1], [TERM:Series_T2], [TERM:Series_T3], or [TERM:Series_T4]) are Technical and Treasury series. These series handle operational infrastructure including payment services, custody, expenses, and technology.

1.3. Series with the prefix B followed by a number (such as [TERM:Series_B1] or [TERM:Series_B2]) are Brand and Intellectual Property series. These series hold and manage intellectual property assets including trademarks, patents, and copyrights.

1.4. Series with the prefix P followed by a number (such as [TERM:Series_P1], [TERM:Series_P2], or [TERM:Series_P3]) are Platform Services series. These series provide internal services including compliance, finance, and consulting.

1.5. Series with the prefix S followed by a number (such as [TERM:Series_S1]) are Securities series. These series handle capital raising and investor relations activities.

1.6. Series with the prefix C followed by a three-digit number (such as C-001, C-002, or C-003) are [TERM:ClientSeries] Client Operations series. These series are established for independent client MSBs operating their own money services businesses using [TERM:Company] infrastructure. Each [TERM:ClientSeries] maintains its own EIN and FinCEN registration. Client series are added by Articles of Amendment as clients are onboarded.

## SECTION 4: GENERAL PROVISIONS

4.1. All series are manager-managed by CCASH MONEY SERVICES (US) SERIES LLC or its designated officers acting on behalf of the Company.

4.2. Each series maintains separate assets, liabilities, and records as required by Montana Code Annotated Section 35-8-304 to preserve liability separation between series.

4.3. The Operating Agreement provisions for each series are set forth in Exhibit B, which is filed together with these Articles as required by statute.

4.4. Additional series may be added at any time by filing Articles of Amendment with the Montana Secretary of State and paying the applicable filing fee for each new series.
