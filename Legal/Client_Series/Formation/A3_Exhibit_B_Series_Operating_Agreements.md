## SECTION 2: STANDARD PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL SERIES

### 2.1. Liability Separation

2.1.1. Pursuant to Montana Code Annotated Section 35-8-304(4), the debts, liabilities, and obligations of each series are enforceable only against the assets of that particular series.

2.1.2. The debts, liabilities, and obligations of any series are not enforceable against the assets of any other series.

2.1.3. The debts, liabilities, and obligations of any series are not enforceable against the assets of the [TERM:Company] generally.

2.1.4. The debts, liabilities, and obligations of any series are not enforceable against the personal assets of any [TERM:Member] or [TERM:Manager].

2.1.5. Each series maintains separate and distinct records and accounts to preserve liability separation.
[DOC:B4]

### 2.2. Management Structure

2.2.1. All series are manager-managed. The [TERM:Manager] of the [TERM:Company], which is [TERM:Company] CCASH MONEY SERVICES (US) SERIES LLC or its designee, has exclusive authority over operations of each series.

2.2.2. [TERM:Member] who are not the [TERM:Manager] have no authority to manage any series or to bind any series to any contract, obligation, or liability.

### 2.3. Binding Authority

2.3.1. No [TERM:Member] or person other than the [TERM:Manager] may bind the [TERM:Company] or any [TERM:Series] to any contract, obligation, or liability.

2.3.2. Any action that would violate law, the [TERM:Company]'s compliance policies, or the [DOC:B1] Master Operating Agreement is void and has no effect.

### 2.4. Transfer Restrictions

2.4.1. Transfer of any membership interest in any series requires prior written approval from the [TERM:Manager].

2.4.2. Transfer of any membership interest in any series requires completion of a compliance review by the [TERM:Company].

2.4.3. Transfer of any membership interest in any series requires completion of know-your-customer verification of the proposed transferee.
[DOC:B5]

### 2.5. Dispute Resolution

2.5.1. Disputes between any [TERM:Series] and any other [TERM:Series], between any [TERM:Series] and the [TERM:Company], or between any [TERM:Series] and the [TERM:Manager], shall be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association ("AAA") under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, seated in Montana and governed by Montana law.

2.5.2. The Federal Arbitration Act, 9 U.S.C. §§ 1-16, shall govern the interpretation and enforcement of this arbitration provision. Questions of arbitrability shall be decided by the arbitrator.

2.5.3. Parties may mutually elect in writing to use AI-assisted arbitration procedures where offered by AAA. If either party declines or the dispute is not eligible, traditional AAA arbitration with human arbitrators applies.

2.5.4. All disputes shall be arbitrated on an individual basis. No party may bring or participate in class, collective, or representative actions.

2.5.5. The arbitrator's award shall be final and binding, subject only to the limited grounds for review under the Federal Arbitration Act.

### 2.6. AI-Enabled Operations

2.6.1. All [TERM:Series] consent to AI-enabled operations as described in the [DOC:B1] Master Operating Agreement. The [TERM:Company] uses AI systems for compliance, support, security, and operational functions.

2.6.2. [TERM:Member] of each [TERM:Series] consent to AI-enabled processing of their information and transactions as a condition of membership.
