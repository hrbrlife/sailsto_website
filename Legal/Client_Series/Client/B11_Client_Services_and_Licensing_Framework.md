## SECTION 14B: AGENT NETWORKS PROHIBITION

### 14B.1. Prohibition on Agents and Sub-Representatives

14B.1.1. **No Agent Authorization.** [TERM:AgentProhibition] A [TERM:ClientSeries] is NOT authorized to appoint, engage, or utilize agents, sub-licensees, franchisees, or other sub-representatives to conduct money transmission or other regulated financial services activities on behalf of the [TERM:ClientSeries]. [OBL:AGENT-PROHIBITION-001]

14B.1.2. **Prohibited Activities.** Without limiting the foregoing, a [TERM:ClientSeries] shall not:

   (a) Authorize any third party to accept customer funds, process transactions, or perform customer identification on behalf of the [TERM:ClientSeries];

   (b) Enable another entity to operate as an MSB using the [TERM:ClientSeries]'s registration, licenses, or infrastructure;

   (c) Enter into any "nested" or pass-through arrangement whereby another entity provides money services to end-users under the [TERM:ClientSeries]'s regulatory umbrella;

   (d) Sublicense or delegate its MSB activities to any third party; or

   (e) Use the [TERM:Company]'s infrastructure to provide services to third parties who themselves provide money services to their own customers (multi-level or cascading relationships).

14B.1.3. **Rationale.** The prohibition on agent networks exists because:

   (a) Agent relationships create complex compliance obligations (registration, supervision, reporting) that would undermine the [TERM:Company]'s compliance program;

   (b) FinCEN and state regulators require MSBs to exercise direct control over operations and may impose principal liability for agent misconduct;

   (c) The [TERM:Company]'s infrastructure, banking relationships, and risk management are designed for direct client relationships, not cascading agent structures; and

   (d) Permitting agent networks would materially increase regulatory, operational, and reputational risk to the [TERM:Company] and other [TERM:ClientSeries].

### 14B.2. Consequences of Violation

14B.2.1. **Material Breach.** Any attempt to establish an agent network or engage in the prohibited activities described above shall constitute a material breach of the [DOC:F1] Client Services Agreement, entitling the [TERM:Company] to immediate termination without cure period.

14B.2.2. **Indemnification.** Client shall indemnify the [TERM:Company] for any fines, penalties, remediation costs, or regulatory actions arising from Client's unauthorized use of agents or sub-representatives.

### 14B.3. Permitted Third-Party Relationships

14B.3.1. **Vendors and Service Providers.** Notwithstanding the prohibition above, a [TERM:ClientSeries] may engage third-party vendors and service providers for non-regulated support functions (e.g., marketing, customer support, software development), provided such vendors do not perform regulated financial services activities.

14B.3.2. **Company Approval Required.** Any third-party arrangement that involves access to [TERM:EndCustomer] customer data, transaction processing, or compliance functions requires prior written approval from the [TERM:Company].
