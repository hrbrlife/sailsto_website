## SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1. This Directory identifies approved Partner MSBs [TERM:PartnerMSB] through which Client Series [TERM:ClientSeries] may route interstate money transmission activities as described in the Approved States Schedule [DOC:N3].

1.2. Partner MSB routing enables Client Series to serve customers in states where the Client Series does not hold its own money transmitter license, by utilizing the Partner MSB's existing state licenses.

1.3. Client Series may not independently contract with money transmitters for routing. All Partner MSB relationships must be established through the Company [TERM:Company] per [OBL:PARTNER-RESTRICT-001].

---

## SECTION 2: PARTNER MSB REQUIREMENTS

2.1. **Licensing Requirements.** Each Partner MSB must hold valid money transmitter licenses in the states for which it provides coverage, maintain current FinCEN [TERM:FinCEN] MSB registration, and pass the Company's due diligence review per [DOC:B12].

2.2. **Contractual Requirements.** The Company must have an executed Partner MSB Services Agreement [DOC:N1] with each Partner MSB that includes licensing/notice commitments, compliance cooperation, liability allocation, and termination for regulatory action.

2.3. **Ongoing Monitoring.** The Company [TERM:CCO] shall monitor Partner MSB license status, regulatory actions, and compliance performance on at least a quarterly basis.

2.4. **CCO Succession for Partner Oversight.** In the event of [TERM:CCO] vacancy:
   (a) Partner MSB monitoring duties shall transfer to the interim BSA/AML Officer within seventy-two (72) hours per [DOC:B3] Section 6.6;
   (b) Quarterly Partner reviews shall continue without interruption;
   (c) Partner MSBs shall be notified of the change in compliance oversight contact within five (5) business days;
   (d) No new Partner MSB agreements shall be executed until a permanent or qualified interim CCO is in place.

---

## SECTION 2A: DUE DILIGENCE AND MONITORING

2A.1. **Due Diligence Standard.** Partner MSB due diligence and ongoing monitoring is conducted per [DOC:B12] Partner Due Diligence Standard.

2A.2. **Partner Obligations.** Partner MSBs are bound by [DOC:N1] Partner MSB Agreement, which requires:
   (a) Maintenance of licenses in good standing;
   (b) Notification of material changes within 5 business days;
   (c) Compliance with applicable law; and
   (d) SAR confidentiality.

2A.3. **Our Responsibility vs. Theirs.** We verify partners are licensed and in good standing. Their regulators verify their compliance programs. We document our due diligence; we do not audit their internal operations.

## SECTION 5: ROUTING PROCEDURES

5.1. **Transaction Routing.** When a Client Series initiates a transaction requiring Partner MSB routing, the Platform [TERM:Series_T4] shall automatically route through the appropriate Partner MSB based on the destination state and transaction type.

5.2. **Fee Pass-Through.** Partner MSB fees are passed through to Client Series as described in the Pricing and Routing Schedule [DOC:C3] Section 4.7.

5.3. **Compliance Data Sharing.** Transaction data necessary for Partner MSB compliance (KYC, source of funds, etc.) shall be shared pursuant to the Partner MSB agreement and Privacy Policy [DOC:B8].

---

## SECTION 6: UPDATES AND AMENDMENTS

6.1. This Directory shall be updated within five (5) business days of:
   - Execution of a new Partner MSB agreement
   - Termination or suspension of a Partner MSB relationship
   - Material change in Partner MSB license coverage
   - Regulatory action affecting a Partner MSB

6.2. Client Series shall be notified of material changes affecting their routing options within the same timeframe.

6.3. The Company reserves the right to add, remove, or modify Partner MSB relationships at any time, subject to notice requirements in the Client Services Agreement [DOC:F1].
