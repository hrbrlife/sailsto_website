## RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, Sails DAO LLC, a Wyoming decentralized autonomous organization limited liability company, acting by and through its protected series designated as [SERIES NAME] ("[SERIES DESIGNATION]") (the "**Series**"), desires to engage a qualified custodian to hold and safeguard the assets raised through securities offerings;

**WHEREAS**, [CUSTODIAN NAME], a [ENTITY TYPE] organized under the laws of [STATE/COUNTRY] (the "**Custodian**"), is a qualified custodian as defined under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 and applicable regulations;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 2: APPOINTMENT AND STATUS

**2.1 Appointment**

Series hereby appoints Custodian as its qualified custodian, and Custodian accepts such appointment, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

**2.2 Qualified Custodian Status**

Custodian represents and warrants that it is a "qualified custodian" within the meaning of Rule 206(4)-2 under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940, by virtue of being:

☐ A bank as defined in Section 202(a)(2) of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940  
☐ A savings association as defined in Section 3(b)(1) of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act  
☐ A broker-dealer registered under Section 15 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934  
☐ A futures commission merchant registered under Section 4f of the Commodity Exchange Act  
☐ A foreign financial institution that customarily holds financial assets for customers

**2.3 Regulatory Compliance**

Custodian shall maintain its status as a qualified custodian throughout the term of this Agreement. Custodian shall promptly notify Series if its status as a qualified custodian changes or is threatened.

**2.4 Series Structure Acknowledgment**

Custodian acknowledges and agrees:

**(a)** Series is a protected series of Company formed under Wyoming Statute § 17-31-114;

**(b)** Assets held for Series are assets of Series only and not assets of Company or any other series;

**(c)** Custodian shall not assert any claim against Assets based on obligations of Company or any other series;

**(d)** Custodian shall segregate Series Assets from assets of Company and any other series;

**(e)** Account records shall clearly identify Assets as belonging to Series.

## ARTICLE 3: CUSTODIAL SERVICES

**3.1 Establishment of Account**

Custodian shall establish and maintain one or more custodial accounts in the name of Series (or as otherwise directed by Series in compliance with applicable law) to hold Offering Proceeds and other Assets.

**Account Registration:**
- Account Name: Sails DAO LLC - [Series Designation]
- Tax ID: [SERIES EIN]

**3.2 Receipt of Assets**

**(a) Offering Proceeds:**

Custodian shall receive and hold Offering Proceeds deposited by:
- Wire transfer from Company's payment processor [DOC:N1]
- ACH transfer from Company's payment processor
- Direct wire from investors (if applicable)

**(b) Securities:**

Custodian shall receive Securities:
- In book-entry form through DTC or other depository
- In certificated form (if applicable)
- In electronic form through approved systems

**(c) Other Assets:**

Custodian shall receive other assets as agreed by the parties.

**3.3 Safekeeping**

Custodian shall:

**(a)** Hold all Assets in the Account;

**(b)** Maintain Assets in a manner that clearly identifies them as belonging to Series;

**(c)** Not commingle Assets with Custodian's own assets or assets of other customers (except omnibus accounts at sub-custodians where industry practice);

**(d)** Hold Securities:
- In Custodian's name, as agent for Series;
- In the name of a nominee of Custodian or sub-custodian;
- In book-entry form at a securities depository (DTC, Fedwire);

**(e)** Maintain appropriate insurance coverage for custodied assets;

**(f)** Exercise reasonable care in performing custodial functions.

**3.4 Cash Management**

**(a) Investment of Cash:**

Cash held in the Account may be invested in:
- Money market funds
- Bank deposits
- Treasury securities
- Other investments as directed by Series

**(b) Interest:**

Interest or earnings on cash balances shall be credited to the Account.

**(c) Uninvested Cash:**

Custodian may hold uninvested cash as bank deposits, subject to FDIC insurance limits.

**3.5 Disbursements**

Custodian shall disburse Assets from the Account only upon Proper Instruction:

**(a) Payment Types:**
- Disbursements to Series (operational expenses)
- Distributions to investors
- Payment of fees (including Custodian fees)
- Transfer to other accounts
- Other payments as instructed

**(b) Disbursement Procedures:**
- Written Instruction required (electronic acceptable)
- Dual authorization for disbursements over $[THRESHOLD] (see Schedule A)
- Custodian may rely on Proper Instruction without further inquiry

**(c) Rejection of Instructions:**

Custodian may reject Instructions that:
- Are not Proper Instructions
- Would violate applicable law
- Would violate this Agreement
- Present reasonable risk of fraud

Custodian shall promptly notify Series of rejected Instructions.

**3.6 Settlement of Transactions**

**(a) Securities Transactions:**

Custodian shall settle securities transactions in accordance with market practice:
- Receive securities against payment
- Deliver securities against payment
- Free delivery/receipt as instructed

**(b) Failed Transactions:**

Custodian shall notify Series of failed settlements and take reasonable steps to complete settlement.

**3.7 Corporate Actions**

**(a) Notification:**

Custodian shall notify Series of corporate actions affecting Securities:
- Dividends
- Stock splits
- Rights offerings
- Tender offers
- Mergers
- Proxy solicitations

**(b) Action:**

Custodian shall act upon corporate actions as instructed by Series. In absence of Instructions, Custodian may take no action (except collecting and crediting dividends in ordinary course).

**(c) Proxy Voting:**

Custodian shall forward proxy materials to Series. Series is responsible for voting decisions.

## ARTICLE 5: REPORTING AND RECORDS

**5.1 Account Statements**

Custodian shall provide Series with:

**(a) Monthly Statements:**
- Opening and closing balances
- All transactions during the period
- Holdings as of statement date
- Interest and earnings credited

**(b) Daily Transaction Reports:**
- All transactions processed
- Pending transactions

**(c) Annual Tax Reporting:**
- 1099s and other required tax forms
- Cost basis reporting

**5.2 Confirmation of Transactions**

Custodian shall provide confirmation of each transaction within 1 Business Day of execution.

**5.3 Online Access**

Custodian shall provide Series with online access to:
- Account balances (real-time)
- Transaction history
- Holdings
- Statements and reports

**5.4 Recordkeeping**

Custodian shall maintain records of:
- All account transactions
- All Instructions received
- All statements and reports
- Account opening documentation

Records shall be maintained for at least 7 years or as required by law, whichever is longer.

**5.5 Audit Rights**

Series (and its auditors) may:
- Audit Account records upon reasonable notice
- Request confirmations and certifications
- Examine Custodian's controls (SOC 1/SOC 2 reports)

## ARTICLE 8: DUTIES AND STANDARD OF CARE

Custodian shall:

**(a)** Perform its duties with the care that a professional custodian would exercise;

**(b)** Maintain and enforce reasonable procedures to safeguard Assets;

**8.2 Limitation of Duties**

Custodian is not responsible for:

**(a)** Valuation of Securities (unless separately agreed);

**(b)** Investment decisions or advice;

**(f)** Any matter beyond Custodian's control.

**8.3 Reliance on Others**

**(a)** Information and Instructions from Authorized Persons;

**(b)** Legal counsel;

**(c)** Accountants and auditors;

**(d)** Sub-custodians and depositories.

## ARTICLE 9: LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Limitation of Liability**

**(a) Custodian Liability:**

Custodian shall be liable for direct losses resulting from:
- Custodian's negligence or willful misconduct
- Custodian's failure to use reasonable care
- Custodian's breach of this Agreement

**(b) Exclusion of Consequential Damages:**

NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, REGARDLESS OF THE FORM OF ACTION OR THEORY OF LIABILITY.

**(c) Limitation Amount:**

Custodian's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed:
- For operational failures: Annual fees paid to Custodian
- For loss of Assets due to Custodian negligence: Value of lost Assets

**9.2 Force Majeure**

Custodian shall not be liable for delays or failures caused by:
- Acts of God
- Government actions
- War or terrorism
- Strikes or labor disputes
- System failures beyond reasonable control
- Third-party failures (depositories, utilities, communications)

**9.3 Indemnification by Series**

Series shall indemnify and hold harmless Custodian from claims, losses, and expenses arising from:

**(a)** Series' breach of this Agreement;

**(b)** Custodian acting on Proper Instructions;

**(c)** False or misleading information provided by Series;

**(d)** Series' violation of applicable law.

**9.4 Indemnification by Custodian**

Custodian shall indemnify and hold harmless Series from claims, losses, and expenses arising from:

**(a)** Custodian's negligence or willful misconduct;

**(b)** Custodian's breach of this Agreement;

**(c)** Custodian's violation of applicable law.

**9.5 Indemnification Procedures**

**(a)** Prompt written notice of claim;

**(b)** Cooperation in defense;

**(c)** Indemnifying party controls defense (with consent to settlement);

**(d)** Indemnified party may participate at own expense.

## ARTICLE 12: TERM AND TERMINATION

**12.1 Term**

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until terminated as provided herein.

**12.2 Termination**

**(a) Termination by Either Party:**
- Upon 60 days' written notice

**(b) Termination for Cause:**
- Upon material breach (with 30 days to cure)
- Immediately upon insolvency
- Immediately upon regulatory action affecting ability to serve as custodian

**(c) Termination by Series:**
- Immediately if Custodian ceases to be qualified custodian

**12.3 Effect of Termination**

Upon termination:

**(a)** Series shall provide Instructions for transfer of Assets;

**(b)** Custodian shall transfer Assets to successor custodian as instructed;

**(c)** If no Instructions within 60 days, Custodian may liquidate and hold cash;

**(d)** Termination does not affect accrued rights or obligations;

**(e)** Confidentiality provisions survive termination.

**12.4 Cooperation**

Custodian shall cooperate with transition to successor custodian, including:
- Providing records and information
- Executing necessary documents
- Completing pending transactions
