## ARTICLE 2: APPOINTMENT AND SCOPE

**2.1 Appointment**

Series hereby appoints Transfer Agent as its exclusive transfer agent and registrar for the Securities, and Transfer Agent accepts such appointment, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

**2.2 Scope of Services**

Transfer Agent shall provide the following services:

**(a) Register Maintenance:**
- Establish and maintain the official Register of Security Holders
- Record all initial issuances of Securities
- Record all Transfers of Securities
- Maintain accurate holder information

**(b) Transfer Processing:**
- Process Transfer requests
- Verify Transfer documentation
- Update Register upon completed Transfers
- Issue new Certificates or Book-Entry confirmations

**(c) Registrar Services:**
- Issue Securities (Certificates or Book-Entry)
- Cancel Securities upon Transfer or redemption
- Maintain Certificate inventory and control

**(d) Security Holder Services:**
- Respond to Security Holder inquiries
- Provide ownership confirmations
- Process address changes
- Maintain contact information

**(e) Distribution Services:**
- Calculate Distribution amounts per holder
- Provide Distribution payment file
- Process Distribution payments (if elected)
- Issue tax reporting information

**(f) Reporting:**
- Provide Security Holder lists
- Provide transaction reports
- Provide regulatory reports
- Provide tax reporting support

**(g) Regulatory Compliance:**
- OFAC screening
- Transfer restrictions monitoring
- Legend enforcement

**2.3 Series Structure Acknowledgment**

Transfer Agent acknowledges and agrees:

**(a)** Series is a protected series of Company formed under Wyoming Statute § 17-31-114;

**(b)** Securities are securities of Series only, not securities of Company;

**(c)** Transfer Agent services relate only to Securities of Series;

**(d)** Records and accounts shall clearly identify Securities as belonging to Series.

## ARTICLE 5: TRANSFER PROCESSING

**5.1 Transfer Requests**

Transfers shall be processed only upon:

**(a) Transferor Request:**
- Signed transfer instruction from Registered Holder
- Medallion signature guarantee (if required)
- Transfer form completion

**(b) Supporting Documentation:**
- Evidence of transferee accredited investor status (if required)
- Opinion of counsel (if required by Series)
- Issuer consent (if required by Operating Agreement)

**5.2 Transfer Requirements**

Before processing a Transfer, Transfer Agent shall verify:

**(a) Proper Documentation:**
- Transfer request properly completed
- Signature(s) verified
- Required supporting documents received

**(b) Transfer Restrictions:**
- Transfer complies with Securities Act restrictions
- Transfer complies with Operating Agreement restrictions
- Holding period met (if applicable)
- Series consent obtained (if required)

**(c) OFAC Screening:**
- Screen transferee against OFAC lists
- Document screening results
- Reject if positive match

**5.3 Transfer Procedures**

**(a) Book-Entry Transfers:**
1. Receive and verify Transfer request
2. Verify OFAC clearance
3. Obtain Series approval (if required)
4. Debit transferor's position
5. Credit transferee's position
6. Issue confirmation to transferee
7. Notify transferor of completion

**(b) Certificate Transfers:**
1. Receive and verify Transfer request
2. Receive original Certificate(s)
3. Verify signatures (medallion guarantee if required)
4. Verify OFAC clearance
5. Obtain Series approval (if required)
6. Cancel old Certificate(s)
7. Issue new Certificate(s)
8. Mail new Certificate(s) to transferee

**5.4 Rejected Transfers**

Transfer Agent shall reject Transfers that:

**(a)** Lack required documentation;

**(b)** Have unverified signatures;

**(c)** Violate Transfer restrictions;

**(d)** Fail OFAC screening;

**(e)** Are not approved by Series (where required);

**(f)** Are otherwise improper.

Transfer Agent shall notify transferor of rejection and reason.

**5.5 Partial Transfers**

Transfer Agent may process partial Transfers where:

**(a)** Transferor holds more Securities than being transferred;

**(b)** Transfer otherwise complies with requirements;

**(c)** New Certificate or Book-Entry issued for remaining Securities.

**5.6 Transfer Taxes**

Any transfer taxes shall be paid by transferor or transferee as agreed. Transfer Agent may require payment before processing.

**6.1 Distribution Processing**

Upon Series' Instruction, Transfer Agent shall assist with Distributions:

**(a) Distribution Calculation:**
- Prepare Schedule of Security Holders as of record date
- Calculate pro rata Distribution amounts
- Verify total Distribution

**(b) Distribution Reporting:**
- Provide Distribution payment file to Series
- Provide wire/ACH instructions for each payee

**(c) Distribution Payment (if elected):**
- If Distribution payment service elected, Transfer Agent shall:
  - Receive funds from Series
  - Disburse to Security Holders per payment instructions
  - Process returned items
  - Report on payment status

**6.2 Payment Methods**

Distributions may be paid by:

**(a)** Direct deposit (ACH)  
**(b)** Wire transfer (for amounts over $[THRESHOLD])  
**(c)** Check (if electronic payment not available)

**6.3 Unclaimed Distributions**

**(a) Returned Payments:**
Transfer Agent shall notify Series of returned payments and attempt to locate Security Holder.

**(b) Escheatment:**
Unclaimed Distributions shall be escheated per applicable state law. Transfer Agent shall coordinate escheatment reporting.

**6.4 Tax Reporting**

**(a) Information Collection:**
Transfer Agent shall maintain W-9 (or W-8) for each Security Holder.

**(b) Tax Form Preparation:**
Transfer Agent shall prepare or assist with:
- Form 1099-DIV (dividends)
- Form 1099-INT (interest)

- Schedule K-1 support data

**(c) Tax Withholding:**
Transfer Agent shall apply backup withholding if W-9 not received.

## ARTICLE 8: OFAC COMPLIANCE

**8.1 Screening**

Per [DOC:N1 Article 4] and OFAC regulations:

**(a) Initial Screening:**
Transfer Agent shall screen all transferees against:
- OFAC SDN List
- OFAC Consolidated Sanctions List
- Other applicable sanctions lists

**(b) Ongoing Screening:**
Transfer Agent shall rescreen Security Holders:
- At least annually
- Upon OFAC list updates
- Upon address/name changes

**8.2 Match Procedures**

**(a) Potential Match:**
Investigate and resolve within 2 Business Days. Document resolution.

**(b) Confirmed Match:**
- Do NOT process Transfer
- Do NOT make Distribution
- Notify Series and legal counsel immediately
- Block applicable Securities/funds
- Report to OFAC as required

**8.3 Documentation**

Maintain records of:
- All screening performed
- Match investigations and resolutions
- Blocking reports
- OFAC correspondence
