## RECITALS

A. The Company is a Montana Series Limited Liability Company [TERM:Series LLC] that operates as a registered Money Services Business [TERM:MSB] providing operational infrastructure, technology platform [TERM:Platform], and compliance services to its Client Series [TERM:ClientSeries].

B. Partner is a licensed money transmitter holding valid licenses in one or more U.S. states as set forth in Schedule A (Licensed States).

C. The Company seeks to enable its Client Series to serve End Customers [TERM:End Customer] in states where neither the Company nor the Client Series holds a money transmitter license, by routing transactions through Partner's licensed infrastructure.

D. Partner desires to provide transaction routing services to the Company and its Client Series in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

E. This Agreement incorporates and is subject to the Partner MSB Directory [DOC:N4], the Approved States Schedule [DOC:N3], and all Company policies referenced herein.

---

## ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS

1.1. **"Applicable Law"** means all federal, state, and local laws, rules, and regulations applicable to the activities contemplated by this Agreement, including without limitation the Bank Secrecy Act [TERM:BSA/AML], state money transmitter laws, and FinCEN [TERM:FinCEN] regulations.

1.2. **"BSA/AML Program"** means the Bank Secrecy Act and Anti-Money Laundering compliance program maintained by each party in accordance with 31 CFR Part 1022.

1.3. **"Covered States"** means the U.S. states listed in Schedule A for which Partner holds valid money transmitter licenses and agrees to provide services under this Agreement.

1.4. **"End Customer"** [TERM:End Customer] means any customer of a Client Series whose transaction is routed through Partner pursuant to this Agreement.

1.5. **"Licensed States"** means all U.S. states and territories in which Partner holds a valid money transmitter license or equivalent authorization.

1.6. **"Routed Transaction"** means a money transmission transaction originated by a Client Series and processed through Partner's licensed infrastructure pursuant to this Agreement.

1.7. **"Transaction Data"** means all data relating to Routed Transactions, including customer identification information, transaction amounts, source and destination details, and compliance documentation.

---

## ARTICLE 2: TERM AND EFFECTIVENESS

2.1. **Initial Term.** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of two (2) years ("Initial Term").

2.2. **Renewal.** Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods ("Renewal Terms") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2.3. **Early Termination.** Either party may terminate this Agreement:
   (a) For cause upon thirty (30) days' written notice following a material breach that remains uncured;
   (b) Immediately upon regulatory action affecting either party's ability to perform;
   (c) For convenience upon one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice.

---

## ARTICLE 3: SERVICES AND TERRITORY

3.1. **Routing Services.** Partner agrees to process Routed Transactions for the Company and its Client Series in the Covered States listed in Schedule A.

3.2. **License Maintenance.** Partner shall maintain in good standing all money transmitter licenses required to provide services in the Covered States throughout the term of this Agreement.

3.3. **Territory Exclusivity.** This Agreement is non-exclusive. The Company may contract with other Partner MSBs for routing services in the same or different states.

3.4. **Coverage Updates.** Partner may expand Covered States upon written notice to the Company and execution of an updated Schedule A. Partner shall provide at least sixty (60) days' notice before ceasing coverage in any Covered State.

3.5. **Authorized Delegate Status.** [TERM:AuthorizedDelegate] To the extent required by Applicable Law, Partner acknowledges and agrees that it may be acting as an authorized delegate, agent, or similar designation of the Company or its Client Series with respect to Routed Transactions. Partner shall:
   (a) Comply with all authorized delegate requirements under applicable state money transmitter laws;
   (b) Conspicuously display or disclose its status as an authorized delegate where required by law;
   (c) Not conduct money transmission activities outside the scope of this Agreement using its authorized delegate status;
   (d) Provide the Company with copies of any authorized delegate filings or registrations made with state regulators; and
   (e) Notify the Company within five (5) business days of any regulatory inquiry concerning its authorized delegate status.

3.6. **Transaction Limits.** Partner may establish reasonable transaction limits and volume caps as set forth in Schedule B. Any changes to limits shall require thirty (30) days' advance notice.

---

## ARTICLE 4: COMPLIANCE AND REGULATORY OBLIGATIONS

4.1. **Partner Regulatory Status.** Partner represents and warrants that it:
   (a) Is properly licensed or authorized to conduct money transmission in all Covered States;
   (b) Maintains current FinCEN MSB registration;
   (c) Complies with all applicable federal and state laws governing its money transmission activities; and
   (d) Maintains a BSA/AML compliance program as required by applicable law.

4.2. **Ongoing Compliance.** Partner shall maintain in good standing all licenses, registrations, and authorizations required for its operations under this Agreement throughout the term.

4.3. **Material Change Notification.** Partner shall notify Company within five (5) business days of:
   (a) Any change in license status (suspension, revocation, restriction, or surrender);
   (b) Any regulatory enforcement action, consent order, or formal investigation;
   (c) Any material change in ownership, control, or key compliance personnel; or
   (d) Any change that materially affects Partner's ability to perform under this Agreement.

4.4. **Verification Rights.** Company may request, and Partner shall provide within ten (10) business days, evidence of current licensing and registration status in any Covered State.

4.5. **SAR Confidentiality.** Partner acknowledges that SAR filings and information revealing SAR existence are confidential under federal law. Partner shall not disclose SAR-related information to Company or any non-regulatory party.

4.6. **Independent Compliance.** Each party is responsible for its own regulatory compliance. This Agreement does not make either party responsible for the other's compliance program, internal controls, or regulatory obligations. Company's due diligence is conducted per [DOC:B12].

### Bankability Requirements

4.7. **Travel Rule Compliance.** For Routed Transactions of $3,000 or more, Partner shall:
   (a) Accept and retain originator information transmitted by Company (name, address, account number or unique identifier);
   (b) Transmit required originator and beneficiary information to the next financial institution in the payment chain; and
   (c) Make Travel Rule records available upon Company or regulatory request.
   [31 CFR §1010.410(f)]

4.8. **Sanctions Screening.** Partner shall:
   (a) Screen all Routed Transactions against OFAC sanctions lists prior to processing;
   (b) Block or reject transactions involving sanctioned parties; and
   (c) Notify Company promptly (without disclosing SAR-protected information) if a Routed Transaction is blocked for sanctions reasons.
   [31 CFR Part 501]

4.9. **Cooperation with Banking Partners.** Partner acknowledges that Company's banking relationships may require Partner to:
   (a) Respond to reasonable information requests from Company's banking partners concerning Routed Transactions;
   (b) Provide transaction-level data (excluding SAR-protected information) for bank compliance or audit purposes; and
   (c) Participate in compliance calls or reviews if requested by Company's banking partner.
   Partner's refusal to cooperate with reasonable banking partner requests is grounds for suspension or termination.

---

## ARTICLE 5: FEES AND SETTLEMENT

5.1. **Routing Fees.** The Company shall pay Partner the fees set forth in Schedule B for each Routed Transaction.

5.2. **Fee Pass-Through.** Partner fees shall be passed through to Client Series as described in the Pricing and Routing Schedule [DOC:C3].

5.3. **Settlement.** Settlement of Routed Transactions and fees shall occur as specified in Schedule B, but in no event less frequently than weekly.

5.4. **Invoicing.** Partner shall provide monthly invoices with transaction detail sufficient for the Company to allocate fees to individual Client Series.

5.5. **Disputed Amounts.** Either party may dispute invoiced amounts within thirty (30) days of receipt. Undisputed amounts shall be paid pending resolution.

---

## ARTICLE 6: TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1. **Termination Events.** In addition to Section 2.3, this Agreement terminates automatically upon:
   (a) Revocation or suspension of Partner's money transmitter license in any Covered State (as to that state);
   (b) Either party's bankruptcy, insolvency, or assignment for benefit of creditors;
   (c) Either party's loss of FinCEN MSB registration; or
   (d) Material regulatory action prohibiting performance.

6.2. **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination:
   (a) No new Routed Transactions shall be initiated;
   (b) Pending Routed Transactions shall be completed or unwound in an orderly manner;
   (c) Transaction Data shall be preserved as required by law; and
   (d) Outstanding fees shall be settled within thirty (30) days.

6.3. **Wind-Down Period.** Unless termination results from regulatory action requiring immediate cessation, the parties shall cooperate in an orderly wind-down of at least thirty (30) days.

6.4. **Survival.** Sections 4.3, 4.4, 6.2, 7, 8, 9, and 10 shall survive termination.

## ARTICLE 7: LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

7.1. **Mutual Indemnification.** Each party ("Indemnifying Party") shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party and its affiliates, officers, directors, and employees from any claims, damages, or expenses arising from:
   (a) The Indemnifying Party's breach of this Agreement;
   (b) The Indemnifying Party's violation of Applicable Law; or
   (c) The Indemnifying Party's negligence or willful misconduct.

7.2. **Limitation of Liability.** EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY OR INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR CONSEQUENTIAL, INCIDENTAL, INDIRECT, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES. Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed fees paid or payable under this Agreement during the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

7.3. **Loss Allocation.** Losses arising from Routed Transactions shall be allocated as follows:
   (a) Losses from Partner's processing errors: Partner's responsibility;
   (b) Losses from Company or Client Series KYC failures: Company's responsibility;
   (c) Third-party fraud losses: allocated per Schedule B.

---

## ARTICLE 8: CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1. **Confidential Information.** Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other party's non-public business information, customer data, and Transaction Data.

8.2. **Permitted Disclosures.** Confidential information may be disclosed:
   (a) As required by Applicable Law or regulatory request;
   (b) To employees and contractors with a need to know;
   (c) With the prior written consent of the disclosing party.

8.3. **Data Protection.** Each party shall implement and maintain reasonable security measures to protect Confidential Information in accordance with [DOC:B8] and [DOC:B6], including:
   (a) Encryption of data at rest and in transit using industry-standard protocols (TLS 1.2+, AES-256);
   (b) Access controls with role-based permissions and audit logging;
   (c) Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing; and
   (d) Incident response procedures with notification to the other party within 72 hours of any security incident affecting shared data.

---

## ARTICLE 8A: DATA PROTECTION

8A.1. **Applicable Law.** Each party shall comply with applicable data protection and privacy laws governing its processing of personal data under this Agreement.

8A.2. **Company Responsibilities.** Company is responsible for obtaining appropriate consents from End Customers and responding to data subject requests, in accordance with [DOC:B8].

8A.3. **Partner Cooperation.** Partner shall reasonably cooperate with Company in responding to data subject requests affecting Transaction Data, within five (5) business days of Company's written request.

8A.4. **Data Use Limitation.** Partner shall use Transaction Data solely for purposes of providing services under this Agreement and meeting its regulatory obligations.

8A.5. **International Transfers.** Where Transaction Data is transferred internationally, such transfers shall comply with applicable law. For End Customers located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the provisions of [DOC:B8] Part II apply.

## ARTICLE 9: REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

9.1. **Mutual Representations.** Each party represents and warrants that:
   (a) It is duly organized and validly existing under the laws of its jurisdiction;
   (b) It has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement;
   (c) This Agreement constitutes a valid and binding obligation; and
   (d) Its performance will not violate any agreement or Applicable Law.

9.2. **Partner Representations.** Partner additionally represents and warrants that:
   (a) It holds valid money transmitter licenses in all Licensed States;
   (b) It maintains current FinCEN MSB registration;
   (c) It has no pending or threatened regulatory actions that would materially impair performance; and
   (d) Its BSA/AML Program meets regulatory requirements.

9.3. **Company Representations.** Company additionally represents and warrants that:
   (a) It holds valid FinCEN MSB registration;
   (b) Each Client Series maintains its own FinCEN MSB registration [DOC:N5];
   (c) Its KYC/AML procedures meet regulatory requirements; and
   (d) It will provide accurate Transaction Data.

---

## ARTICLE 10: GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1. **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Montana without regard to conflicts of law principles; provided, however, that data protection obligations in Article 8A shall be governed by the applicable privacy laws of the relevant jurisdictions based on data subject location, notwithstanding this choice of law provision.

10.2. **Dispute Resolution.** Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved as follows:
   (a) **Negotiation.** The parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation.
   (b) **Mediation.** If negotiation fails within thirty (30) days, the parties shall submit to non-binding mediation.
   (c) **Arbitration.** If mediation fails, disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration [FLOW:Arbitration] in Montana in accordance with AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules.

10.3. **Equitable Relief.** Either party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction.

11.3. **Amendment.** This Agreement may only be amended by written instrument signed by both parties.

11.4. **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, including all Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter.

11.5. **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.
