## ARTICLE 1: OVERVIEW

**1.1 Purpose**

This guide provides procedures for obtaining International Securities Identification Numbers (ISINs) and CUSIP numbers for securities issued by Series Issuers of Sails DAO LLC.

**1.2 Why Obtain an ISIN/CUSIP**

While not legally required for private placements, ISINs/CUSIPs provide benefits:

**(a) Operational Benefits:**
- Standardized identification across systems
- Easier transfer agent processing
- Compatibility with custodian systems
- Simplified reporting and reconciliation

**(b) Investor Benefits:**
- Professional presentation
- Easier portfolio tracking
- Compatibility with investor reporting systems

**(c) Future Benefits:**
- Required if securities later registered
- Required for trading on secondary markets
- Required for certain institutional investors

| **Scenario** | **Recommendation** |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Small offering (<20 investors) | Optional |
| Institutional investors | Recommended |
| Debt securities | Highly recommended |
| Future secondary market planned | Required |

## ARTICLE 2: UNDERSTANDING ISINs AND CUSIPs

**2.1 CUSIP**

**(a) Definition:**

CUSIP (Committee on Uniform Security Identification Procedures) is a 9-character alphanumeric identifier for securities issued in the United States and Canada.

**(b) Structure:**

```
[6-digit Issuer Number][2-digit Issue Number][Check Digit]
Example: 123456 78 9
         ^^^^^^ ^^ ^
         Issuer Issue Check
```

**(c) Issuer:**

CUSIP Global Services (CGS), operated by S&P Global Market Intelligence on behalf of the American Bankers Association.

**2.2 ISIN**

**(a) Definition:**

ISIN (International Securities Identification Number) is a 12-character alphanumeric code that uniquely identifies securities globally, per ISO 6166.

**(b) Structure:**

```
[2-letter Country Code][9-character NSIN][Check Digit]
Example: US 123456789 0
         ^^ ^^^^^^^^^ ^
         Country NSIN Check
```

For U.S. securities, the NSIN is the CUSIP.

**(c) Issuer:**

In the United States, CGS issues ISINs along with CUSIPs.

**2.3 Relationship**

- Every U.S. CUSIP has a corresponding ISIN
- ISIN = "US" + CUSIP + Check Digit
- Apply for both simultaneously through CGS

## ARTICLE 4: APPLICATION STEPS

**4.1 Step 1: Create CGS Account**

1. Go to https://www.cusip.com
2. Click "Request Access" or "Register"
3. Complete registration form:
   - Company name
   - Contact information
   - Role (Issuer / Agent)
4. Verify email address
5. Complete account setup

**4.2 Step 2: New Issue Request**

1. Log in to CGS portal
2. Select "Request New Identifier"
3. Choose security type
4. Complete application form with information from Article 3.4

**4.3 Step 3: Provide Supporting Documents**

Upload or be prepared to provide:

**(a) Required:**
- Articles of Organization / Series Designation
- Operating Agreement excerpts (security terms)
- Board/Manager resolution authorizing securities
- Evidence of offering exemption (Form D if filed)

**(b) May Be Requested:**
- Private Placement Memorandum
- Subscription Agreement
- Legal opinion
- Additional security description

**4.4 Step 4: Pay Fees**

**(a) CGS Fee Schedule (approximate - verify current fees):**

| **Service** | **Fee** |
|-------------|---------|
| New CUSIP (equity) | $200-$300 |
| New CUSIP (debt) | $200-$300 |
| ISIN (with CUSIP) | Included |
| Additional issue (same issuer) | $100-$200 |
| Rush processing | Additional $200-$500 |

**(b) Payment:**
- Credit card (most common)
- Invoice (for established accounts)

**4.5 Step 5: Review and Submit**

1. Review all information for accuracy
2. Verify security description matches offering documents
3. Submit application
4. Receive confirmation number

**4.6 Step 6: Receive CUSIP/ISIN**

**(a) Timeline:**
- Standard processing: 1-3 business days
- Rush processing: Same day or next day

**(b) Notification:**
- Email notification with CUSIP and ISIN
- Access through CGS portal
- Letter confirmation (if requested)

## ARTICLE 5: SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS FOR SERIES LLCs

**5.1 Series as Separate Issuer**

Each Series of Sails DAO LLC is a separate issuer for CUSIP purposes:

- Apply for separate CUSIP for each Series
- Series Designation is the "issuer" name
- Series EIN used for tax identification

**5.2 Issuer Name Format**

Recommended format:
```
Sails DAO LLC - Series I-001
or
Sails DAO LLC Series I-001
or
[Series Name] (I-001 of Sails DAO LLC)
```

**5.3 Multiple Securities Per Series**

If a Series issues multiple classes or tranches:
- Separate CUSIP for each class/tranche
- Same issuer number, different issue numbers
- Example:
  - Series I-001 Class A Units: XXXXXX 10 X
  - Series I-001 Class B Units: XXXXXX 11 X
  - Series I-001 Convertible Notes: XXXXXX 20 X

**5.4 Documentation for Protected Series**

CGS may request additional documentation to confirm:
- Series is validly formed
- Series has authority to issue securities
- Series is separate from parent LLC

Provide:
- Wyoming SOS confirmation
- Series Designation document
- Series Operating Agreement excerpt
- Legal memo on protected series (if requested)

## ARTICLE 10: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Is a CUSIP/ISIN legally required for private placements?**

A: No. There is no legal requirement to obtain a CUSIP or ISIN for private placement securities. However, they are recommended for operational benefits and required for certain secondary market activities.

**Q: How long does it take to get a CUSIP/ISIN?**

A: Standard processing is 1-3 business days. Rush processing can provide same-day or next-day issuance for an additional fee.

**Q: Can I get a CUSIP/ISIN for membership interests in an LLC?**

A: Yes. LLC membership interests are eligible for CUSIP/ISIN identification, classified as equity securities.

**Q: Does each series need its own CUSIP?**

A: Yes. Each Series is a separate issuer and requires its own CUSIP for its securities. If a Series issues multiple classes, each class needs a separate CUSIP.

**Q: What if we issue additional securities later?**

A: If you issue additional securities of the same class/terms, they typically use the same CUSIP. If terms are materially different (different interest rate, maturity, etc.), a new CUSIP is needed.

**Q: Can we apply before the offering closes?**

A: Yes. You can apply for a CUSIP as soon as the security terms are finalized, even before the first sale. This is recommended to have the CUSIP ready for investor confirmations.

**Q: What if information changes after CUSIP is issued?**

A: Notify CGS of material changes. Most changes can be updated without a new CUSIP. New CUSIP is needed only for material changes to security terms.

**Q: Who owns the CUSIP number?**

A: CUSIP numbers are assigned by CGS. The issuer has the right to use the number for its securities. Numbers can be retired but not transferred.
