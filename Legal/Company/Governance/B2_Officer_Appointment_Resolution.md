## SECTION 3: OFFICER RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1. RESOLVED, that each [TERM:Officers] shall have the responsibilities set forth in the [DOC:B1] Master Operating Agreement and further described in this Section.

### 3.2. Chief Executive Officer Responsibilities

3.2.1. The [TERM:CEO] is responsible for overall management and strategic direction of the [TERM:Company].

3.2.2. The [TERM:CEO] represents the [TERM:Company] to regulatory authorities and government agencies.

3.2.3. The [TERM:CEO] manages external affairs and stakeholder relations.

3.2.4. The [TERM:CEO] supervises and coordinates the activities of other [TERM:Officers].

3.2.5. The [TERM:CEO] has final decision authority on operational matters, subject to [TERM:Manager] oversight.

### 3.3. Chief Technology Officer Responsibilities

3.3.1. The [TERM:CTO] is responsible for platform technology development and maintenance.

3.3.2. The [TERM:CTO] manages information security and cybersecurity programs.
[DOC:B6]

3.3.3. The [TERM:CTO] oversees infrastructure management and disaster recovery planning.

3.3.4. The [TERM:CTO] manages technology vendor relationships.

3.3.5. The [TERM:CTO] ensures technical compliance with industry standards and best practices.

### 3.4. Chief Marketing Officer Responsibilities

3.4.1. The [TERM:CMO] is responsible for brand management and protection.

3.4.2. The [TERM:CMO] manages client acquisition and relationship management programs.

3.4.3. The [TERM:CMO] leads business development and partnership initiatives.

3.4.4. The [TERM:CMO] develops and executes marketing strategy.

3.4.5. The [TERM:CMO] manages public relations and communications.

### 3.5. Chief Financial Officer Responsibilities

3.5.1. The [TERM:CFO] is responsible for treasury and cash management.
[DOC:B10]

3.5.2. The [TERM:CFO] manages financial accounting and reporting.

3.5.3. The [TERM:CFO] ensures tax compliance and planning.

3.5.4. The [TERM:CFO] develops budgets and financial forecasts.

3.5.5. The [TERM:CFO] coordinates audits and examinations.

3.5.6. The [TERM:CFO] manages banking relationships jointly with the [TERM:CEO].

### 3.6. Chief Compliance Officer Responsibilities

3.6.1. The [TERM:CCO] serves as the BSA/AML Officer with authority over the BSA/AML program, customer identification program, customer due diligence and enhanced due diligence, sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, and suspicious activity reporting.
[DOC:B5]

3.6.2. The [TERM:CCO] designs and maintains the BSA/AML Program, including written policies and procedures, independent testing, training, and ongoing monitoring.

3.6.3. The [TERM:CCO] files and certifies required regulatory forms and updates, including FinCEN MSB registration updates and any required state money transmitter license change filings.
[DOC:C1] <!-- ABN Registration -->

3.6.4. The [TERM:CCO] coordinates regulatory examinations, responds to inquiries, and reports material compliance matters to the [TERM:Manager].

3.6.5. The [TERM:CCO] oversees compliance training for [TERM:Officers] and employees and verifies completion.

3.6.6. The [TERM:CCO] maintains the sanctions screening program and ensures all onboarding and transactional activity complies with OFAC and other sanctions regimes.

3.6.7. The [TERM:CCO] may halt or reject transactions or clients, require remediation, and direct holds across any [TERM:Series] when compliance concerns arise.

3.6.8. The [TERM:CCO] ensures [TERM:Company] compliance with applicable anti-money laundering laws and international standards, and monitors regulatory guidance updates for impact on [TERM:Company] operations.

3.6.9. The [TERM:CCO] oversees anti-corruption compliance, including administration of the ethics program and confidential reporting mechanisms.

3.6.10. The [TERM:CCO] manages risk-based information sharing with banking partners, correspondent institutions, and regulators, including determining when higher-risk indicators trigger expedited disclosure.

3.6.11. The [TERM:CCO] ensures data protection compliance under applicable laws and maintains appropriate bases for compliance data processing and cross-border transfers.
[DOC:B8]

3.6.12. The [TERM:CCO] coordinates transparency and disclosure obligations to business partners, including banking partners requiring ongoing compliance information access.
