## RECITALS

**WHEREAS,** Sails DAO LLC (the "**Company**") is a Wyoming decentralized autonomous organization limited liability company formed pursuant to the Wyoming Limited Liability Company Act (W.S. § 17-31-101 et seq.);

**WHEREAS,** the Members desire to operate a securities platform enabling issuers to conduct compliant offerings under Regulation D via independent Issuer Series;

**WHEREAS,** the Company shall implement agent-of-payee payment processing architecture to minimize regulatory costs while maintaining compliance with federal securities laws;

**WHEREAS,** the Members desire to establish governance through smart contracts, token-based voting, and on-chain execution;

**NOW THEREFORE,** the Members hereby adopt this Operating Agreement governing the Company's operations, management, and member relations.

## ARTICLE 7: AGENT-OF-PAYEE PAYMENT PROCESSING

**7.1 Agent-of-Payee Model:**

The Company coordinates agent-of-payee payment processing whereby:

(a) Payment processors act as AGENTS of Issuer Series (not as independent intermediaries);
(b) Processors collect investor subscription payments on behalf of Issuer Series;
(c) Processors verify accredited investor status and perform OFAC screening;
(d) Processors facilitate delivery of physical securities certificates with ISIN numbers;
(e) Processors remit funds to Issuer Series minus processing fees.

**7.2 Legal Rationale:**

Agent-of-payee structure avoids:
- Broker-dealer registration (processor not acting as securities intermediary);
- Money transmitter licensing (delivering securities, not transmitting money);
- Costly regulatory compliance infrastructure (broker-dealer: $500K+/year).

Cost advantage: ~$70K/year total vs. $600K+ for broker-dealer route.

**7.3 Payment Processor Network:**

The Company maintains relationships with payment processors including:
- Stripe Connect
- PayPal Commerce Platform
- [Additional processors]

Requirements for payment processor partners:
- Agreement to act as agent of Issuer Series;
- OFAC SDN list screening integration;
- Accredited investor verification capability;
- API integration with Sails platform;
- Acceptance of cost-plus-markup fee structure;
- Compliance with international data protection (GDPR SCCs if applicable).

**7.4 Payment Processor Agreements:**

Each payment processor executes Partner Payment Processor Agreement [DOC:N1] containing:

(a) **Agency Relationship:** Processor acts as agent of Issuer Series, not principal;
(b) **OFAC Screening:** Real-time screening against SDN list [Preserve N1 §4.8];
(c) **Fee Structure:** Cost-plus-markup (15-40% based on volume) [Preserve N1 Article 5];
(d) **Data Protection:** International data transfers via SCCs [Preserve N1 Article 8A];
(e) **Liability:** Indemnification and limitation of liability;
(f) **Termination:** 60-day notice, wind-down procedures.

**7.5 Payment Flow:**

Standard payment flow:

```
1. Investor visits Series I-XXX offering page
2. Investor completes accredited investor verification
3. Investor signs Subscription Agreement
4. Investor submits payment to Payment Processor (acting as agent of I-XXX)
5. Payment Processor performs OFAC screening
6. Payment Processor holds funds in escrow pending closing
7. Upon closing:
   a. Payment Processor releases funds to Issuer Series
   b. Payment Processor remits fees to Company
   c. Transfer Agent records securities ownership
   d. Physical certificate printed and delivered (or held by Qualified Custodian)
```

**7.6 Securities Certificates:**

Physical securities certificates contain:

- Issuer Series name and designation
- ISIN number (applied via CUSIP Service Bureau)
- Securities type and terms (par value, interest rate, maturity date)
- Investor name and address
- Transfer restrictions legend (Rule 144)
- Series Manager signature
- Certificate number and issue date

Certificates printed on security paper with anti-counterfeiting features.

**7.7 OFAC Compliance:**

ALL investor payments screened against OFAC SDN list in real-time:
- Automatic screening via payment processor API
- Manual review of potential matches
- Blocking of transactions involving sanctioned persons/countries
- SAR filing if suspicious activity detected
- Annual certification by payment processors

[Preserve N1 §4.8 OFAC screening architecture]

**7.8 No Sub-Delegation:**

Payment processors may NOT sub-delegate agent responsibilities to third parties (agent networks prohibited). All processing functions must be performed directly by contracted payment processor.

**13.1 Good Faith Negotiation:**

- Written notice of dispute to other party
- Face-to-face meeting (or video conference)
- Attempt to resolve through compromise

**13.2 Mediation:**

- Conducted via American Arbitration Association (AAA)
- Mediator selected per AAA Commercial Mediation Rules
- Location: Cheyenne, Wyoming (or virtual)
- Cost split equally among disputing parties
- 30-day mediation period

**13.3 Arbitration:**

If mediation fails, binding arbitration:

(a) **AAA Rules:** Per AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules
(b) **Arbitrator:** One arbitrator selected per AAA rules
(c) **Location:** Cheyenne, Wyoming (or virtual)
(d) **Governing Law:** Wyoming law
(e) **Discovery:** Limited discovery per arbitrator discretion
(f) **Award:** Final and binding, enforceable in any court
(g) **Confidentiality:** Proceedings confidential except as required by law

**13.4 Exceptions:**

Disputes not subject to arbitration:
- Injunctive relief (may seek in court)
- Judicial dissolution
- Interpretation of smart contract code (technical arbitration via blockchain dispute resolution)

**13.5 Attorney's Fees:**

Prevailing party in arbitration entitled to reasonable attorney's fees and costs.

**13.6 Smart Contract Disputes:**

Disputes regarding smart contract execution:
- Technical arbitration via [Kleros, Aragon Court, or similar]

- Result executed on-chain

(c) Sale of all Company assets

Upon dissolution:

(a) **Cease Business:** Stop conducting business except as necessary for winding up;
(b) **Liquidate Assets:** Sell or distribute Company assets;
(c) **Pay Debts:** Pay or make provision for Company debts and liabilities;
(d) **Distribute Remaining Assets:** Distribute remaining assets to Members per Section 14.3;
(e) **File Certificate:** File Articles of Dissolution with Wyoming SOS.

**14.3 Distribution Priority:**

Assets distributed in following order:

**14.4 Series Continuation:**

- Continue as independent entity
