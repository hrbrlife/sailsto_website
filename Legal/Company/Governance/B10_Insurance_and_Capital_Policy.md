## SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1. This Policy sets minimum insurance, capital, and cash management guidelines to support licensing, operational resilience, risk management, and prudent stewardship of client funds for the [TERM:Company] and its [TERM:Series].

1.2. The [TERM:Company] manages client funds prudently, maintaining appropriate segregation, liquidity reserves, and operational controls. Client assets are protected and available for client use in accordance with applicable agreements and compliance requirements.

---

## SECTION 2: MANDATORY INSURANCE COVERAGE
[FLOW:Insurance_Coverage]
[OBL:OBL-INS-001]

2.1. The [TERM:Company] shall maintain the following insurance coverages at all times:

2.1.1. Commercial crime insurance or fidelity bond covering theft of funds (fiat and digital assets), fraudulent transfers, employee dishonesty, and cybersecurity breaches, in an amount not less than the greater of: (a) one million dollars; or (b) twenty-five percent of average monthly client assets under custody, reviewed quarterly.

2.1.2. Cyber liability insurance covering data breaches, network security failures, ransomware, business interruption from cyber events, and incident response costs, in an amount not less than five million dollars.

2.1.3. Errors and omissions or professional liability insurance covering claims arising from professional services, in an amount not less than two million dollars.

2.1.4. Directors and officers liability insurance protecting [TERM:Officers] and [TERM:Manager] from claims arising from their service, in an amount not less than two million dollars.

2.1.5. Such surety bonds as required by state money transmitter licensing in each jurisdiction where the [TERM:Company] is licensed.
[OBL:OBL-REG-001] <!-- State MTL bond requirements -->

2.2. Coverage amounts shall be reviewed at least annually by the [TERM:CFO] and [TERM:CCO], and upon: (a) any license application or renewal; (b) material increase in assets under custody; (c) expansion into new jurisdictions; or (d) any security incident or material claim.

2.3. The [TERM:CFO] shall maintain certificates of insurance evidencing current coverage and shall notify the [TERM:Manager] immediately if any required coverage lapses or is materially reduced.
[DOC:B4] <!-- Record keeping for insurance certificates -->

2.4. Insurance policies shall, where available, name [TERM:ClientSeries] as additional insureds or loss payees with respect to claims involving client assets.

## SECTION 4: CLIENT FUND TRUST AND SEGREGATION
[FLOW:Client_Fund_Trust]

4.1. All client fiat funds held by [TERM:Series_T1] and all client digital assets held by [TERM:Series_T2] are held in trust for the benefit of the respective [TERM:ClientSeries] and their [TERM:EndCustomer]. Such funds are not general assets of the [TERM:Company] and shall not be available to satisfy claims of the [TERM:Company]'s general creditors.

4.2. Client funds held by [TERM:Series_T1] on behalf of [TERM:ClientSeries] are segregated from [TERM:Company] operating funds. Client balances are tracked separately in the accounting system and are not available for [TERM:Company] operating purposes.

4.2.1. **Omnibus Accounts and Sub-Ledger Segregation.** Client fiat funds may be held in omnibus (commingled) bank accounts for operational efficiency, provided that:

   (a) Such accounts are titled to reflect a customer benefit / “for benefit of” structure where supported by the banking partner (e.g., “FBO Clients/Customers”), or are otherwise designated as trust/custodial accounts under the applicable agreement;

   (b) The [TERM:Company] maintains a daily-reconciled sub-ledger that records each [TERM:ClientSeries] and [TERM:EndCustomer] beneficial entitlement to the omnibus balance with sufficient granularity to support withdrawals, disputes, and regulatory examination;

   (c) The omnibus balance is not used for [TERM:Company] operating expenses, investments outside the permitted investment policy, or as collateral for [TERM:Company] obligations; and

   (d) The Company maintains procedures to promptly segregate and distribute beneficial balances in the event of a distress event (including Company dissolution, banking partner termination, or Client insolvency). [OBL:OMNIBUS-TRUST-001]

4.2.2. **No Cross-Series Substantive Commingling.** Even where omnibus banking accounts are used, the [TERM:Company] shall maintain separate accounting, reconciliation, and audit trails to preserve liability separation between Series and to prevent any substantive commingling of beneficial ownership.

4.3. Client digital assets held by [TERM:Series_T2] on behalf of [TERM:ClientSeries] are segregated using separate wallet addresses or sub-accounts. Client assets are not commingled with [TERM:Company] assets or assets of other clients except in omnibus arrangements where individual client entitlements are clearly recorded.

4.4. In the event of bankruptcy, dissolution, or liquidation of the [TERM:Company] or any functional [TERM:Series], client funds and assets shall be distributed to the respective [TERM:ClientSeries] and their [TERM:EndCustomer] in priority to claims of the [TERM:Company]'s general creditors, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

4.4.1. **Reconciliation and Proof of Entitlements.** The [TERM:CFO] shall ensure that reconciliation records, sub-ledger reports, and supporting bank/custody statements are retained in accordance with [DOC:B4] and are sufficient to prove beneficial entitlements in an examination, insolvency proceeding, or dispute. [OBL:OMNIBUS-RECON-001]

4.5. The [TERM:Company] maintains liquidity reserves sufficient to meet reasonably anticipated client withdrawal and payment demands. Liquidity is monitored daily by treasury operations and reported weekly to the [TERM:CFO].

4.6. Float and settlement timing are managed to ensure client funds are available when needed. The [TERM:Company] does not extend settlement periods to benefit from float at client expense.

4.7. Client funds are placed only with regulated banking partners and financial institutions meeting the [TERM:Company]'s counterparty risk standards. The [TERM:CCO] and [TERM:CFO] jointly approve banking relationships.

4.8. The [TERM:Company] does not invest client funds in speculative or high-risk instruments. Client funds may be held in demand deposits, money market accounts, or short-term government securities, prioritizing capital preservation and liquidity.

4.9. Interest or yield earned on client funds, if any, is allocated as provided in applicable client agreements. Where agreements are silent, interest accrues to the [TERM:Company] as compensation for treasury management services.

## SECTION 5: INTERNAL FINANCIAL CONTROLS AND SEGREGATION OF DUTIES
[FLOW:Financial_Controls]

5.1. The [TERM:Company] shall implement internal controls designed to prevent fraud, error, and unauthorized transactions, including dual controls, segregation of duties, and independent reconciliation.

5.2. Dual Control Requirements:

5.2.1. Any transfer of [TERM:Company] funds or client funds exceeding ten thousand dollars requires initiation by one authorized [TERM:Officers] and independent approval by a second authorized [TERM:Officers].

5.2.2. Any change to bank account details, authorized signatories, or wire transfer templates requires written approval from at least two [TERM:Officers].

5.2.3. Disbursement of capital or distributions to [TERM:Member] requires documented approval from the [TERM:Manager] and at least one additional [TERM:Officers].

5.3. Segregation of Duties:

5.3.1. No single individual may initiate and approve the same financial transaction.

5.3.2. Individuals responsible for transaction initiation shall not have sole custody of encryption keys, seed phrases, or authentication credentials required to execute transactions.

5.3.3. Access to accounting systems, treasury systems, and banking platforms shall be role-based and segregated such that no single user can create, approve, and reconcile transactions without independent oversight.

5.3.4. Code deployments affecting financial systems, payment processing, or custody functions require approval from at least two authorized personnel, one from operations and one from technology.
[DOC:B6] <!-- IT Security change management -->

5.4. Reconciliation and Monitoring:

5.4.1. Client fund balances shall be reconciled against bank statements, blockchain records, and internal ledgers at least daily.

5.4.2. Any discrepancy exceeding one thousand dollars or 0.1% of account balance (whichever is greater) shall be escalated immediately to the [TERM:CFO] and [TERM:CCO].

5.4.3. Reconciliation functions shall be performed by personnel independent of those initiating or approving transactions.

5.5. Override and Exception Procedures:

5.5.1. In emergency situations where dual control cannot be achieved (e.g., single [TERM:Officers] availability), the [TERM:CEO] may authorize a transaction subject to: (a) documented justification; (b) immediate notification to the [TERM:Manager]; and (c) ratification at the next Officers' meeting.

5.5.2. All override events shall be logged and reviewed quarterly by the [TERM:Manager].

## SECTION 6: LOSS ALLOCATION AND LIMITATION
[FLOW:Loss_Allocation]

6.1. The [TERM:Company] shall use commercially reasonable efforts to safeguard client funds and assets, including maintaining the security controls set forth in the [DOC:B6] IT Security Policy (B6), the insurance coverages required by this Policy, and prudent custody practices.

6.2. In the event of loss of client funds or assets due to theft, cyberattack, fraud, or other security incident, despite commercially reasonable safeguards:

6.2.1. The [TERM:Company] shall promptly notify affected [TERM:ClientSeries] and regulatory authorities as required by law.
[DOC:B5] <!-- SAR filing if applicable -->

6.2.2. The [TERM:Company] shall prioritize using available insurance proceeds to compensate client losses.

6.2.3. The [TERM:Company] may, at its discretion, contribute additional [TERM:Company] capital to remediate client losses beyond insurance coverage.

6.2.4. The [TERM:Company]'s liability for such losses shall be limited to: (a) amounts actually recovered under applicable insurance policies; plus (b) any additional amounts the [TERM:Company] at its discretion decides to contribute; plus (c) amounts recoverable from responsible third parties.

6.3. Nothing in this Section limits the [TERM:Company]'s liability for losses caused by the [TERM:Company]'s gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud.

6.4. [TERM:ClientSeries] acknowledge that certain risks (including sophisticated cyberattacks, zero-day exploits, and force majeure events) may cause losses despite commercially reasonable security measures. The [TERM:Company] does not guarantee against all loss and is not an absolute insurer of client funds.
