## Section 4: Pricing Philosophy

<!-- [FLOW:Pricing] -->

4.1. The [TERM:Company] Company prices services transparently on a cost-plus basis. The client pays the [TERM:Company] Company's actual cost for banking, custody, and processing, plus a markup that covers compliance overhead, technology investment, and margin.

4.2. **Volume-Based Pricing:** Larger clients with higher transaction volumes receive lower markups. This rewards growth and aligns incentives—as the client grows, both parties benefit.

4.3. **[TERM:RiskClassification] Risk-Based Pricing:** Higher-risk client profiles or transaction types carry higher markups to reflect the additional compliance burden and risk management required.

4.4. **Fee Components:**
- Transaction fees based on volume and risk tier
- [TERM:PlatformFee] Monthly platform fee for infrastructure access
- [TERM:OnboardingFee] One-time onboarding fee for setup and integration
- [TERM:TrainingFee] One-time training fee for compliance and platform training
- [TERM:ITLicenseFee] IT license fee as a percentage of transaction volume

4.5. Pricing is set forth in the [DOC:C3] Pricing and Routing Schedule, which is attached to the [TERM:ClientServicesAgreement] Client Services Agreement. The Schedule may be updated from time to time with reasonable notice.

## Section 5: Routing Philosophy

<!-- [FLOW:Routing] -->

5.1. The [TERM:Company] Company maintains relationships with multiple [TERM:BankingPartner] banking partners, [TERM:PaymentProcessor] payment processors, and service providers. Transactions are routed to provide clients with the most cost-effective compliant path available.

5.2. **Cheapest Compliant Route:** For each transaction, the [TERM:Company] Company selects the provider that offers the lowest cost while meeting all compliance requirements, considering speed, jurisdiction, and transaction type.

5.3. **Multi-Rail Expansion:** The [TERM:Company] Company continuously expands its network of [TERM:BankingPartner] banking partners and payment rails to improve options, reliability, and pricing for all clients.

5.4. **Fair Allocation:** Provider capacity is allocated fairly among clients. No single client monopolizes access to preferred routes.

5.5. **Transit Optimization:** Where multiple hops are required, the [TERM:Company] Company optimizes the path to minimize costs and delays while maintaining compliance at each step.

9.3. **Fairness:** Pricing is consistent across similar risk profiles and volumes. Provider capacity is allocated fairly. Enforcement is proportionate.
