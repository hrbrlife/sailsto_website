## ARTICLE 4: PURPOSE AND POWERS

**4.1 General Purpose:**

The Company is formed for the following purposes:

(a) **Securities Platform Operation:** To operate a decentralized platform enabling the creation, issuance, registration, and administration of securities offerings under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, including Regulation D offerings under Rules 504, 506(b), and 506(c);

(b) **Issuer Series Management:** To establish and manage independent Issuer Series (designated I-001, I-002, etc.) as separate series under W.S. § 17-31-101 et seq., each constituting a special purpose vehicle for specific securities offerings;

(c) **Platform Services Provision:** To provide technology infrastructure, compliance frameworks, document templates, payment processing coordination, investor relations, transfer agent services, and regulatory filing assistance to Issuer Series;

(d) **Agent-of-Payee Network:** To coordinate with payment processors acting as agents of Issuer Series to collect investor subscription payments and facilitate delivery of physical security certificates with ISIN numbering;

(e) **Regulatory Compliance:** To maintain compliance with applicable federal securities laws (Securities Act of 1933, Securities Exchange Act of 1934), state securities laws (Blue Sky laws), privacy regulations (GLBA, GDPR, CCPA), sanctions requirements (OFAC), and data security standards (FTC Safeguards Rule);

(f) **General Business:** To engage in any lawful business, purpose, or activity for which limited liability companies may be organized under the Wyoming Limited Liability Company Act.

**4.2 DAO Governance:**

This Company is organized as a Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO) pursuant to W.S. § 17-31-106. The Company may utilize:

(a) **Smart Contract Governance:** Algorithmic governance mechanisms executing on blockchain infrastructure;
(b) **Token-Based Voting:** Governance tokens conferring voting rights to token holders;
(c) **On-Chain Execution:** Automated execution of governance decisions via smart contracts;
(d) **Member Proposals:** Submission and voting on proposals by DAO members;
(e) **Treasury Management:** Decentralized control of Company treasury and resources.

The specific governance mechanisms shall be set forth in the Operating Agreement and implemented via smart contracts identified in the Operating Agreement.

**4.3 Powers:**

The Company shall have all powers necessary or convenient to effect any purpose for which it is formed, including but not limited to:

(a) Sue and be sued in its own name;
(b) Purchase, lease, or otherwise acquire real and personal property;
(c) Sell, convey, mortgage, pledge, lease, exchange, and otherwise dispose of property;
(d) Make contracts and guarantees;
(e) Borrow money and issue evidences of indebtedness;
(f) Lend money and acquire securities;
(g) Be a promoter, partner, member, associate, or manager of any entity;
(h) Conduct business in any jurisdiction;
(i) Appoint officers, agents, and employees;
(j) Make donations for charitable, educational, scientific, or public purposes;
(k) Establish compensation and benefit plans;
(l) Purchase and maintain insurance on behalf of the Company and its members;
(m) Cease operations and dissolve.

## ARTICLE 5: MANAGEMENT STRUCTURE

**5.1 Member-Managed:**

The Company shall be managed by its Members unless otherwise provided in the Operating Agreement. Management authority may be exercised through:

(a) Direct member votes on proposals;
(b) Delegated authority to designated managers or officers;
(c) Smart contract execution of pre-approved governance rules;
(d) Multi-signature wallet requirements for treasury transactions.

**5.2 Officers:**

The Company may designate the following officers with authority to bind the Company:

(a) **Chief Executive Officer (CEO):** General management authority
(b) **Chief Compliance Officer (CCO):** Regulatory compliance oversight  
(c) **Chief Technology Officer (CTO):** Platform infrastructure management
(d) **Chief Financial Officer (CFO):** Financial operations and treasury
(e) **General Counsel:** Legal affairs and contract execution

Officers serve at the pleasure of the Members and may be removed by member vote.

**5.3 Signing Authority:**

The following persons have authority to execute documents on behalf of the Company:

(a) Any officer acting within their designated authority;
(b) Any member acting pursuant to a duly adopted member resolution;
(c) Any agent or attorney-in-fact appointed by the Members;
(d) Any smart contract executing automated transactions pursuant to the Operating Agreement.

## ARTICLE 6: SERIES STRUCTURE

**6.1 Series Authorization:**

Pursuant to W.S. § 17-31-101 et seq., the Company is authorized to establish one or more series of members, assets, or membership interests. Each series shall:

(a) Have separate and distinct rights, powers, duties, and obligations;
(b) Have separate and distinct business purposes;
(c) Have the power to sue and be sued in its own name;
(d) Have separate and distinct assets and liabilities;
(e) Be designated by series identifier (I-001, I-002, I-003, etc.).

**6.2 Issuer Series:**

Each series established under this Article shall constitute an "Issuer Series" - a special purpose vehicle created for the purpose of:

(a) Conducting one or more securities offerings under Regulation D;
(b) Issuing debt securities, equity securities, or hybrid securities;
(c) Managing investor relations for specific offering(s);
(d) Maintaining separate books and records;
(e) Filing separate tax returns and regulatory reports.

**6.3 Liability Shield:**

The debts, liabilities, obligations, and expenses incurred, contracted for, or otherwise existing with respect to a particular Issuer Series shall be enforceable against the assets of that series only, and not against the assets of the Company generally or any other series. Neither the Company nor any series shall be liable for the debts, liabilities, obligations, and expenses of any other series.

**6.4 Series Formation:**

New Issuer Series may be established by:

(a) Member vote approving series formation;
(b) Filing Articles of Amendment with Wyoming Secretary of State (if required);
(c) Execution of Series Operating Agreement;
(d) Allocation of initial assets to series;
(e) Designation of series manager(s) or officers.

**6.5 Series Dissolution:**

An Issuer Series may be dissolved independently of the Company upon:

(a) Completion of offering objectives;
(b) Member vote to dissolve series;
(c) Judicial dissolution;
(d) Administrative dissolution for non-compliance.

Assets of dissolved series shall be distributed pursuant to the Series Operating Agreement and applicable law.

## ARTICLE 7: CAPITAL STRUCTURE

**7.1 Initial Contributions:**

The Company shall accept capital contributions from founding members in the form of:

(a) Cash or cryptocurrency;
(b) Services rendered;
(c) Intellectual property (platform code, templates, documentation);
(d) Promissory notes or other binding obligations to contribute.

The specific contribution amounts and allocations shall be set forth in the Operating Agreement.

**7.2 Membership Interests:**

Membership interests may be evidenced by:

(a) Units or shares in the Company's Operating Agreement;
(b) Governance tokens on blockchain infrastructure;
(c) Non-fungible tokens (NFTs) representing specific rights;
(d) Membership certificates issued by the Company.

Membership interests may be transferable or non-transferable as provided in the Operating Agreement.

**7.3 Additional Contributions:**

The Company may accept additional capital contributions from existing or new members upon terms approved by member vote. Additional contributions may result in dilution of existing membership interests unless otherwise provided.

**7.4 Distributions:**

Distributions to members shall be made:

(a) Pro rata to membership interests unless otherwise agreed;
(b) Subject to solvency requirements under W.S. § 17-31-405;
(c) In accordance with distribution policies in the Operating Agreement;
(d) Via cryptocurrency transfers, wire transfers, or other payment methods.

## ARTICLE 9: RECORDS AND TRANSPARENCY

**9.1 Books and Records:**

The Company shall maintain the following records at its principal office or electronically:

(a) Current and historical Articles of Organization;
(b) Operating Agreement with all amendments;
(c) Records of member votes and governance decisions;
(d) Financial statements and tax returns (past 5 years);
(e) List of members' names, addresses, and membership interests;
(f) Minutes of member meetings and written consents;
(g) Smart contract code and deployment addresses;
(h) Records of Issuer Series formations and dissolutions.

**9.2 Member Inspection Rights:**

Members have the right to inspect and copy Company records upon reasonable notice. Inspection rights may be exercised:

(a) During normal business hours;
(b) For proper purposes related to member's interest;
(c) Via secure electronic access portals;
(d) Subject to confidentiality obligations.

The Company may deny inspection requests if:
- Purpose is improper (competitive intelligence, harassment);
- Information is privileged or confidential;
- Request is unreasonably burdensome.

**9.3 Public Transparency:**

As a DAO, the Company commits to public transparency regarding:

(a) Smart contract code (open source where appropriate);
(b) On-chain governance decisions and votes;
(c) Treasury transactions via blockchain explorers;
(d) Member proposals and discussion forums;
(e) Platform usage statistics and metrics.

Confidential information (investor PII, trade secrets, legal strategy) shall be protected.

## ARTICLE 11: DISSOLUTION AND WINDING UP

**11.1 Dissolution Events:**

The Company shall dissolve upon:

(a) Member vote to dissolve (supermajority or percentage specified in Operating Agreement);
(b) Entry of judicial decree of dissolution;
(c) Sale, transfer, or disposition of all Company assets;
(d) Unanimous consent of all members;
(e) Administrative dissolution by Wyoming Secretary of State;
(f) Occurrence of event specified in Operating Agreement.

**11.2 Winding Up:**

Upon dissolution, the Company shall:

(a) Cease conducting business except as necessary for winding up;
(b) Notify known claimants of dissolution;
(c) Collect and liquidate assets;
(d) Discharge or make provision for liabilities;
(e) Distribute remaining assets to members pro rata to interests.

**11.3 Priority of Distributions:**

Assets shall be distributed in the following priority:

1. Creditors (secured and unsecured);
2. Return of member capital contributions;
3. Distribution of remaining assets pro rata to membership interests.

**11.4 Series Continuation:**

Dissolution of the Company does not automatically dissolve Issuer Series. Each series may continue as an independent entity or be dissolved separately.

**11.5 Certificate of Dissolution:**

Upon completion of winding up, the Company shall file Articles of Dissolution with Wyoming Secretary of State.

## ARTICLE 12: INDEMNIFICATION

**12.1 Mandatory Indemnification:**

The Company shall indemnify any person who was or is a party to any proceeding by reason of being or having been a member, manager, officer, employee, or agent against judgments, penalties, fines, settlements, and reasonable expenses actually incurred IF:

(a) The person acted in good faith;
(b) The person reasonably believed their conduct was in or not opposed to Company's best interests;
(c) In criminal proceedings, person had no reasonable cause to believe conduct was unlawful.

**12.2 Advancement of Expenses:**

The Company shall advance expenses (attorneys' fees, costs) incurred in defending proceedings in advance of final disposition upon:

(a) Written undertaking to repay if ultimately determined person not entitled to indemnification;
(b) Determination by members or independent counsel that facts known do not preclude indemnification.

**12.3 Insurance:**

The Company may purchase and maintain insurance on behalf of persons entitled to indemnification against any liability asserted against them in their capacity with the Company.

**12.4 DAO Indemnification:**

Smart contract developers, auditors, and infrastructure providers may be indemnified for actions taken in good faith reliance on:

(a) Smart contract code as deployed;
(b) DAO governance decisions;
(c) Technical specifications provided by Company.
