## ARTICLE 4: PURPOSE

4.1. The Company is organized as a Series Limited Liability Company under Montana law. The Company may establish one or more separate series [TERM:Client Series], each with distinct assets, liabilities, and business purposes.

4.2. **Notice of Series Liability Limitation Pursuant to MCA § 35-8-304:** The debts, liabilities, obligations, and expenses incurred, contracted for, or otherwise existing with respect to a particular series shall be enforceable against the assets of that series only, and shall not be enforceable against the assets of the Company generally or any other series. Each series shall be treated as a separate entity for purposes of liability. Persons contracting with, or having claims against, any series are hereby on notice that recourse shall be limited to the assets of that specific series.

4.3. The Company and its series are authorized to engage in the following activities.

4.3.1. Money services business [TERM:MSB] activities including money transmission, currency exchange, and payment services.

4.3.2. Digital asset and cryptocurrency services including trading, conversion, and related financial services.

4.3.3. Custodial and non-custodial wallet operations for both fiat currency and digital assets.

4.3.4. Payment processing and settlement services for domestic and international transactions.

4.3.5. Financial technology platform [TERM:Platform] development and operation, including software and infrastructure.

4.3.6. Operational infrastructure and compliance services [TERM:Services] for affiliated client series, each operating as an independent MSB under their own brand, EIN, and FinCEN registration.

4.3.7. Compliance and risk management services including anti-money laundering [TERM:BSA/AML] and know-your-customer programs.

4.3.8. Technology consulting and professional services for financial technology implementations.

4.3.9. Holding and licensing of intellectual property including trademarks, patents, copyrights, and trade secrets.

4.3.10. Development, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and automated systems for compliance, operations, customer service, security, and other business functions, subject to appropriate human oversight.

4.3.11. Any other lawful business permitted under Montana law for a limited liability company.
