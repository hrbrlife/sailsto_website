## SECTION 1: PURPOSE

1.1. This Standard establishes the Company's due diligence process for evaluating and monitoring Partner MSBs and other third-party business relationships.

1.2. **Philosophy:** We verify that partners are compliant in their jurisdictions. We document our assessment. We decide if the relationship is acceptable. We do not prescribe HOW partners run their compliance programs—but we do verify they can meet bankability requirements we need for our banking relationships.

---

## SECTION 2: INITIAL DUE DILIGENCE

### 2.1. Verification Checklist

Before entering a Partner MSB agreement, the CCO shall verify and document:

| Item | Source | Required |
|------|--------|----------|
| FinCEN MSB Registration | FinCEN MSB Registrant Search | Yes |
| State MTL licenses (covered states) | NMLS Consumer Access or state regulator | Yes |
| License in good standing (no suspension/revocation) | State regulator website | Yes |
| No prohibition on working with partner | OFAC, BIS denied parties, debarment lists | Yes |
| No material enforcement actions | State regulator, FinCEN public records | Yes |
| Corporate status active | Secretary of State | Yes |

### 2.2. Bankability Verification

Before approval, confirm partner can meet bankability requirements per [DOC:N1] Art 4.7-4.9:

| Requirement | Verification Method | Required |
|-------------|---------------------|----------|
| Travel Rule capability | Confirm partner can accept/transmit originator/beneficiary data for $3,000+ transactions | Yes |
| Sanctions screening | Confirm partner screens against OFAC lists | Yes |
| Banking cooperation | Partner agrees to respond to reasonable bank information requests | Yes |

**Note:** We don't audit HOW they do these things—we confirm they CAN and WILL do them.

### 2.3. Risk Assessment

2.3.1. The CCO shall document a brief risk assessment noting:
   (a) Partner's licensing scope and limitations;
   (b) Any gaps against our operational expectations;
   (c) Justification for accepting those gaps (if any); and
   (d) Overall risk rating: **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**.

2.3.2. A "High" risk rating requires Manager approval before proceeding.

### 2.4. Documentation

2.4.1. Due diligence file shall include:
   - Verification checklist (completed and dated)
   - Screenshots/printouts of license verification
   - Bankability confirmation
   - Risk assessment memo (1-2 pages max)
   - Approval signature (CCO; Manager if High risk)

2.4.2. Files retained per [DOC:B4] (5 years from termination of relationship).

## SECTION 3: PARTNER AGREEMENT TERMS

### 3.1. Required Terms (Short, Enforceable)

Partner agreements ([DOC:N1]) shall include these commitments:

| Term | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Partner is licensed/authorized in covered states | Legal authority to operate |
| Partner will maintain licenses in good standing | Ongoing compliance |
| Partner will comply with applicable law | General regulatory obligation |
| Partner will notify us of material changes (license status, enforcement, restrictions) | Early warning |
| Partner will provide evidence of licensing upon request | Verification right |
| We can terminate/suspend if risk is unacceptable | Exit right |
| No SAR disclosure to non-regulatory parties | Federal law compliance |
| **Travel Rule compliance** (accept/transmit originator data for $3k+ transactions) | Bankability |
| **Sanctions screening** (OFAC before processing) | Bankability |
| **Banking partner cooperation** (respond to reasonable info requests) | Bankability |

### 3.2. What NOT to Prescribe

We do not dictate:
- How partner runs their BSA/AML program
- Their internal security controls or certifications
- Their record retention systems
- Their succession planning
- Their insurance coverage levels (beyond what their licenses require)

**Rationale:** These are their regulatory obligations. We verify they are licensed; their regulator verifies their compliance program.

## SECTION 4: ONGOING MONITORING

### 4.1. Quarterly Review

The CCO shall conduct quarterly monitoring including:
   (a) License status check (NMLS, state regulator);
   (b) FinCEN registration status;
   (c) Review of public enforcement actions; and
   (d) Any material notifications received from partner.

### 4.2. Annual Re-Assessment

Annually, update the risk assessment considering:
   - Transaction volume and patterns through partner
   - Any incidents or compliance concerns
   - Changes in partner's licensing or business
   - Changes in regulatory environment

### 4.3. Triggered Review

Immediate review required upon:
   - Partner notification of material change
   - Public enforcement action
   - Customer complaints involving routed transactions
   - Regulatory inquiry concerning partner

## SECTION 5: RISK DECISIONS

### 5.1. Acceptance

5.1.1. If partner meets verification requirements and risk is acceptable, document approval and proceed.

5.1.2. Acceptance does not guarantee partner's ongoing compliance - only that we performed reasonable due diligence.

### 5.2. Conditional Acceptance

5.2.1. If gaps exist but are manageable, document:
   - The specific gap
   - Why it's acceptable for our purposes
   - Any conditions or monitoring enhancements
   - Review date for re-evaluation

### 5.3. Rejection or Termination

5.3.1. If risk is unacceptable, decline or terminate the relationship.

5.3.2. Document the basis for rejection/termination.

5.3.3. No obligation to disclose reasons to partner beyond "business decision."
