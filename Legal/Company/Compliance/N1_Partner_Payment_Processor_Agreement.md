## RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, Company operates Sails, a securities offering platform enabling Series Issuers (as defined herein) to conduct private placement offerings of debt, equity, and convertible securities pursuant to Regulation D of the Securities Act of 1933;

**WHEREAS**, Processor is a payment services provider licensed and authorized to transmit, collect, and remit funds on behalf of principals in connection with lawful commercial transactions;

**WHEREAS**, Company desires to engage Processor to act as AGENT of each Series Issuer for the limited purpose of collecting investor subscription payments, performing OFAC sanctions screening, and remitting collected funds to Series Issuers, thereby enabling securities offerings without requiring Series Issuers or Company to obtain broker-dealer registration or money transmission licenses;

**WHEREAS**, the Parties intend that Processor shall at all times act as AGENT of the Series Issuer (and NOT as principal, broker-dealer, underwriter, or investment adviser), with authority limited to ministerial payment processing functions and no discretion over investment decisions;

**WHEREAS**, this agent-of-payee structure is specifically designed to comply with money transmission licensing exemptions available under state and federal law for agents acting on behalf of payees in commercial transactions;

**2.2 Scope of Services**

Processor shall provide the following services (collectively, "**Services**"):

(a) **Subscription Payment Collection:** Receive and collect Subscription Payments from Investors via:
   - ACH transfer
   - Wire transfer (domestic and international)
   - Credit/debit card (if enabled)
   - Other payment methods mutually agreed in writing

(g) **Compliance Cooperation:** Cooperate with Company and Series Issuer compliance programs and regulatory examinations (see Article 4);

(h) **Record Retention:** Maintain records of all transactions for periods required by Applicable Laws (minimum 5 years).

**2.3 Limitations on Scope**

Processor shall NOT:

(a) Provide investment advice, recommendations, or suitability determinations to Investors;

(b) Solicit investors, market securities, or engage in selling activities;

(c) Exercise discretion over whether to accept or reject investor subscriptions (such decisions made by Series Issuer);

(d) Negotiate terms of Securities offerings;

(e) Handle, custody, or transfer Securities (only cash payments);

(g) Act as broker-dealer, underwriter, investment adviser, or transfer agent;

(h) Commingle Collected Funds with Processor's own funds or funds of other clients (except as permitted by Applicable Laws for omnibus trust accounts with sub-accounting).

This Agreement is non-exclusive. Company may engage other payment processors. Processor may provide services to other clients, provided no conflict of interest exists.

## ARTICLE 3: AGENT-OF-PAYEE RELATIONSHIP

**THIS ARTICLE ESTABLISHES THE LEGAL FOUNDATION FOR THE ENTIRE AGENT-OF-PAYEE STRUCTURE. PRESERVATION OF THIS STRUCTURE IS CRITICAL TO REGULATORY COMPLIANCE.**

**3.1 Agency Appointment**

(a) Each Series Issuer, by utilizing the Platform and Processor's Services, hereby appoints Processor as its authorized AGENT for the sole and limited purpose of:
   - Collecting Subscription Payments from Investors on behalf of Series Issuer;
   - Holding Collected Funds in trust for Series Issuer pending remittance;
   - Remitting Collected Funds to Series Issuer pursuant to Payment Instructions;
   - Performing OFAC Screening on behalf of Series Issuer.

(b) Company, on behalf of each Series Issuer, hereby confirms and ratifies such agency appointment.

(c) Processor hereby accepts such agency appointment and agrees to act as agent of each Series Issuer in accordance with this Agreement.

**3.2 Legal Effect of Agency**

(a) **Payment to Processor = Payment to Series Issuer:** When Investor makes Subscription Payment to Processor, such payment constitutes payment to Series Issuer and extinguishes Investor's payment obligation under Subscription Agreement, subject to clearance and OFAC Screening.

(b) **Series Issuer is Principal:** For purposes of Applicable Laws, Series Issuer is the "payee" or "merchant" in the transaction; Processor is agent of payee/merchant.

(c) **Money Transmission Exemption:** Processor's collection of funds as agent of Series Issuer is intended to qualify for exemption from money transmission licensing under:
   - FinCEN guidance on agent-of-payee (FinCEN-2014-R003 and related guidance);
   - Uniform Money Services Act § 102(13)(G) (agent of payee exemption);
   - State money transmission licensing exemptions for payment processors acting as agents of merchants/payees;
   - Specific state exemptions identified in Schedule B.

(d) **No Principal Liability:** Processor acts solely as agent and shall not be deemed principal, guarantor, or surety for Series Issuer's obligations. Processor's sole obligation is to remit Collected Funds pursuant to this Agreement.

**3.3 Agency Authority and Limitations**

(a) **Express Authority:** Processor has authority to:
   - Accept Subscription Payments in Series Issuer's name;
   - Issue payment receipts on behalf of Series Issuer;
   - Reject payments from OFAC-blocked persons (mandatory);
   - Return payments that fail OFAC Screening or AML verification;
   - Deduct Processor Fees from Collected Funds before remittance.

(b) **No Implied Authority:** Processor has NO authority to:
   - Bind Series Issuer to any agreement, contract, or obligation;
   - Make representations or warranties on behalf of Series Issuer;
   - Accept or reject investor subscriptions (only accept/reject payments);
   - Modify terms of Securities or offerings;
   - Waive any Series Issuer rights;
   - Delegate agency relationship without prior written consent.

(c) **Scope Limitation:** Processor's agency is LIMITED to payment collection and remittance functions described in Section 2.2. Any action beyond this scope requires separate written authorization from Series Issuer.

**3.4 Series Issuer Obligations**

Each Series Issuer agrees to:

(a) Authorize Processor to act as its agent for payment collection;

(b) Provide accurate Payment Instructions;

(c) Indemnify Processor for claims arising from Series Issuer's securities offering activities (excluding Processor's own negligence or misconduct);

(d) Maintain compliance with securities laws governing the offering;

(e) Verify accredited investor status prior to accepting subscriptions (not Processor's responsibility);

(f) Notify Processor of any offering termination or modification affecting payment collection.

**3.5 Disclosure to Investors**

(a) Company shall ensure that PPM [DOC:PPM] and Subscription Agreement [DOC:F2] disclose:
   - Processor acts as agent of Series Issuer for payment collection;
   - Payment to Processor constitutes payment to Series Issuer;
   - Processor performs OFAC Screening on all investors;
   - Processor is not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, or underwriter;
   - Processor makes no representations about Securities or investment merits.

(b) Processor may provide standard disclosure language for inclusion in investor-facing documents.

**3.6 No Broker-Dealer Activities**

(a) **Processor is NOT a Broker-Dealer:** Processor is not registered as a broker-dealer with SEC or any state securities regulator and shall not engage in activities requiring such registration.

(b) **Prohibited Activities:** Processor shall not:
   - Effectuate transactions in securities;
   - Receive transaction-based compensation (commission per sale);
   - Participate in securities offering structuring or negotiations;
   - Provide advice on merits of securities or suitability for investors;
   - Solicit investors or engage in selling efforts;
   - Handle or custody securities (only cash);
   - Act as placement agent or finder.

(c) **Fee Structure:** Processor Fees are FLAT FEES or PERCENTAGE OF PAYMENT VOLUME, not transaction-based commissions tied to securities sales. This fee structure is designed to avoid broker-dealer characterization.

(d) **Referral to Counsel:** If any question arises regarding whether specific activity requires broker-dealer registration, Processor shall refrain from such activity until written confirmation from securities counsel that activity is permissible.

**3.7 Regulatory Filings**

(a) Processor shall NOT be listed as "Sales Compensation Recipient" on Form D filings (Item 12) because Processor does not receive sales commissions or finder's fees.

(b) Processor Fees are operational expenses, not offering-related sales compensation.

## ARTICLE 4: COMPLIANCE AND OFAC SCREENING

**THIS ARTICLE PRESERVES THE CRITICAL OFAC SCREENING REQUIREMENTS FROM ORIGINAL N1 §4.8. PRESERVATION MANDATORY.**

**4.1 Compliance Program**

Processor represents and warrants that it maintains comprehensive compliance program including:

(a) **AML Program:** Written anti-money laundering policies and procedures compliant with Bank Secrecy Act, USA PATRIOT Act, and FinCEN regulations;

(b) **BSA/AML Officer:** Designated compliance officer responsible for AML program implementation and oversight;

(c) **Training:** Annual AML and OFAC training for all personnel involved in payment processing;

(d) **Independent Testing:** Annual independent testing of AML program effectiveness;

(e) **Customer Identification Program (CIP):** Procedures for verifying identity of parties to transactions;

(f) **Transaction Monitoring:** Automated and manual monitoring for suspicious activity;

(g) **Suspicious Activity Reporting:** Procedures for filing SARs with FinCEN when required;

(h) **Recordkeeping:** Maintenance of records as required by Applicable Laws (minimum 5 years).

**4.2 OFAC Screening Program**

**CRITICAL COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENT - OFAC SCREENING MANDATORY FOR ALL INVESTORS**

(a) **Screening Obligation:** Processor shall screen EVERY Investor against OFAC sanctions lists PRIOR to acceptance of Subscription Payment.

(b) **Sanctions Lists:** Processor shall screen against, at minimum:
   - Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) List;
   - Consolidated Sanctions List (Non-SDN Lists);
   - Sectoral Sanctions Identifications (SSI) List;
   - Foreign Sanctions Evaders (FSE) List;
   - Palestinian Legislative Council (NS-PLC) List;
   - Correspondent Account or Payable-Through Account Sanctions (CAPTA) List;
   - Non-SDN Menu-Based Sanctions List (NS-MBS);
   - Other OFAC lists as updated.

(c) **Screening Data Points:** Processor shall screen Investor based on:
   - Full legal name (individual or entity);
   - All known aliases and alternate names;
   - Date of birth (individuals);
   - Country of citizenship/residence;
   - Country of incorporation/formation (entities);
   - Address (residential and business);
   - Government-issued identification numbers (passport, national ID);
   - Beneficial owners (for entities).

(d) **Real-Time Screening:** Screening shall occur in real-time or near-real-time (within 24 hours of payment receipt).

(e) **Screening Technology:** Processor shall use commercially reasonable sanctions screening technology capable of:
   - Fuzzy matching (similar name variations);
   - Phonetic matching;
   - Alias detection;
   - Country risk scoring;
   - Automated list updates (within 24 hours of OFAC list updates).

(f) **Match Resolution:** Upon potential match:
   - Processor shall hold Subscription Payment and NOT remit to Series Issuer;
   - Processor shall conduct enhanced due diligence to confirm or clear match;
   - If match confirmed (true positive), Processor shall:
     - Block funds as required by OFAC regulations;
     - File blocking report with OFAC within 10 business days;
     - Notify Company and Series Issuer immediately (within 24 hours);
     - NOT release funds without OFAC license.
   - If match cleared (false positive), Processor may proceed with payment processing and document clearance rationale.

(g) **Ongoing Monitoring:** Processor shall re-screen Investor database against updated OFAC lists on at least DAILY basis. If existing Investor becomes blocked person:
   - Processor shall immediately notify Company and Series Issuer;
   - Processor shall block any pending payments to/from such Investor;
   - Series Issuer responsible for blocking distributions and taking remedial action.

**4.3 Prohibited Countries and High-Risk Jurisdictions**

(a) **Comprehensive Sanctions Programs:** Processor shall NOT accept payments from Investors located in or citizens of countries subject to comprehensive OFAC sanctions programs:
   - Cuba
   - Iran
   - North Korea
   - Syria
   - Crimea region of Ukraine
   - Donetsk People's Republic
   - Luhansk People's Republic
   - Other countries as designated by OFAC

(b) **High-Risk Jurisdictions:** For Investors from FATF-identified high-risk jurisdictions (grey list and black list), Processor shall apply enhanced due diligence:
   - Additional identity verification;
   - Source of funds inquiry;
   - Purpose of investment inquiry;
   - Senior management approval before processing.

(c) **Country Risk Matrix:** Processor shall maintain country risk matrix categorizing jurisdictions by risk level. Matrix shall be reviewed and updated at least annually.

**4.4 Customer Identification and Verification**

(a) **Identification Data Collection:** Processor shall collect following information for each Investor:
   - **Individuals:**
     - Full legal name;
     - Date of birth;
     - Residential address;
     - Government-issued ID number (passport, driver's license, or national ID);
     - Copy of government-issued photo ID.
   - **Entities:**
     - Full legal name;
     - Principal place of business;
     - Jurisdiction of formation;
     - Formation documents (articles, certificate);
     - Identification of beneficial owners (25%+ ownership);
     - Identification of control persons (officers, directors, managers).

(b) **Verification:** Processor shall verify Investor identity through:
   - Documentary verification (government-issued ID); AND
   - Non-documentary verification (database checks, credit bureau, public records).

(c) **Reliance on Company/Series Issuer:** Processor may rely on identity verification performed by Company or Series Issuer pursuant to accredited investor verification procedures, provided:
   - Company/Series Issuer has written CIP procedures;
   - Company/Series Issuer certifies verification was performed;
   - Processor performs independent OFAC Screening.

**4.5 Suspicious Activity Monitoring and Reporting**

(a) **Transaction Monitoring:** Processor shall monitor transactions for suspicious activity, including:
   - Unusual payment patterns (structuring, rapid deposits/withdrawals);
   - Payments inconsistent with Investor profile;
   - Payments from high-risk jurisdictions;
   - Negative news or adverse media regarding Investor;
   - Unusual requests (e.g., immediate withdrawal after deposit).

(b) **SAR Filing:** If Processor detects suspicious activity, Processor shall:
   - File Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) with FinCEN within 30 calendar days (or 60 days if no suspect identified);
   - NOT notify Investor, Company, or Series Issuer of SAR filing (SAR confidentiality);
   - Maintain SAR and supporting documentation for 5 years.

(c) **SAR Thresholds:** Processor shall file SAR for:
   - Known or suspected violations of law involving transactions >$5,000;
   - Transactions involving potential money laundering;
   - Transactions with no apparent business purpose.

**4.6 Currency Transaction Reporting**

(a) **CTR Filing:** If Processor receives cash or cash-equivalent payment >$10,000 in single transaction or related transactions, Processor shall file Currency Transaction Report (CTR) with FinCEN within 15 calendar days.

(b) **Aggregation:** Processor shall aggregate multiple payments by same Investor within single business day for CTR threshold determination.

(c) **Exemptions:** Processor may exempt certain low-risk entities from CTR filing pursuant to FinCEN exemption procedures.

**4.7 Recordkeeping**

Processor shall maintain following records for minimum 5 years after account closure or last transaction:

(a) CIP records (identification data, verification methods, documentary copies);

(b) OFAC screening results (date, lists checked, match status, resolution);

(c) Transaction records (date, amount, parties, payment method);

(d) SARs and supporting documentation (kept confidential);

(e) CTRs;

(f) Correspondence with regulators;

(g) Training records;

(h) Independent testing reports;

(i) Policy and procedure versions.

**4.8 Compliance Certifications**

(a) **Annual Certification:** Processor shall provide annual written certification to Company attesting that:
   - AML program is implemented and functioning;
   - OFAC screening performed on all Investors;
   - No unresolved OFAC matches;
   - No material compliance violations;
   - Independent testing conducted within past 12 months;
   - Training provided to all relevant personnel.

(b) **Certification Timing:** Within 45 days of each anniversary of Effective Date.

(c) **Material Exceptions:** Certification shall disclose any material exceptions, findings, or remediation in progress.

**4.9 Regulatory Examinations**

(a) **Cooperation:** Processor shall cooperate fully with examinations by regulatory authorities (FinCEN, state banking regulators, OFAC).

(b) **Notification:** Processor shall notify Company within 5 business days of receiving notice of regulatory examination related to Services.

(c) **Access:** Processor shall provide Company access to examination reports and findings (subject to confidentiality restrictions) that may affect Services.

(d) **Remediation:** Processor shall promptly remediate any deficiencies identified by regulators and provide Company with remediation plan and status updates.

**4.10 Compliance Review by Company**

(a) **Audit Rights:** Company may conduct Compliance Review of Processor's AML program, OFAC screening procedures, and compliance with this Agreement:
   - Upon 30 days' written notice;
   - During normal business hours;
   - At Company's expense (unless material deficiency found);
   - No more than once per calendar year (unless material deficiency found or regulatory requirement).

(b) **Scope:** Compliance Review may include:
   - Review of written policies and procedures;
   - Testing of OFAC screening systems;
   - Sample transaction review;
   - Interview of compliance personnel;
   - Review of training records and testing reports.

(c) **Remediation:** Processor shall promptly remediate any deficiencies identified by Compliance Review and provide written remediation plan within 30 days.

## ARTICLE 6: OPERATIONS AND REPORTING

**6.1 Payment Processing Procedures**

(a) **Payment Instructions:** Series Issuer (or Company on Series Issuer's behalf) shall provide Payment Instructions to Processor specifying:
   - Series Issuer name and identifier;
   - Offering details (securities type, price, minimum/maximum investment);
   - Payment methods accepted;
   - Bank account for remittance;
   - Remittance schedule (immediate upon clearance, batch daily, batch weekly);
   - Special instructions (if any).

(b) **Investor Payment Flow:**
   1. Investor completes Subscription Agreement [DOC:F2] with payment details;
   2. Investor initiates payment to Processor (ACH, wire, card);
   3. Processor receives payment and holds in Trust Account;
   4. Processor performs OFAC Screening on Investor (see Article 4.2);
   5. If OFAC clear: Processor confirms payment receipt to Company and Series Issuer;
   6. If OFAC match: Processor blocks payment and notifies Company/Series Issuer (see Article 4.2(f));
   7. Series Issuer reviews and accepts/rejects subscription;
   8. If subscription accepted: Processor remits Collected Funds (less Processor Fees) to Series Issuer per Payment Instructions;
   9. If subscription rejected: Processor returns payment to Investor (less any non-refundable fees disclosed to Investor);
   10. Series Issuer issues Securities to Investor.

(c) **Remittance Timing:**
   - **Standard:** Within 2 Business Days of Series Issuer acceptance of subscription;
   - **Expedited:** Same Business Day if subscription accepted before [2:00 PM ET] (additional fee may apply);
   - **Batch:** Per batch schedule in Payment Instructions (daily, weekly).

(d) **Failed Payments:** If payment fails (insufficient funds, ACH return, chargeback):
   - Processor notifies Company and Series Issuer within 1 Business Day;
   - Processor may assess Chargeback Fee;
   - Series Issuer responsible for canceling subscription or obtaining replacement payment from Investor.

**6.2 Trust Account**

(a) **Segregation:** Processor shall hold Collected Funds in Trust Account segregated from Processor's operating funds and funds of other clients.

(b) **Bank:** Trust Account maintained at [BANK NAME], an FDIC-insured depository institution with minimum [Tier 1 capital ratio of X%].

(c) **Fiduciary Duty:** Processor holds Collected Funds in trust for Series Issuers and shall not use, pledge, or encumber such funds except as directed by Payment Instructions.

(d) **FDIC Insurance:** Collected Funds eligible for FDIC pass-through insurance up to $250,000 per Series Issuer (separate coverage for each Series as separate legal entity).

(e) **Interest:** Interest earned on Trust Account (if any) shall be:
   - Paid to Series Issuers pro rata based on funds held; OR
   - Retained by Processor as additional compensation (specify in Schedule A).

(f) **Account Statements:** Processor shall provide monthly Trust Account statements to Company showing:
   - Opening balance;
   - Deposits by Series Issuer;
   - Remittances to Series Issuers;
   - Fees deducted;
   - Closing balance by Series Issuer.

**6.3 Transaction Reporting**

(a) **Real-Time Notifications:** Processor shall provide real-time notifications to Company and Series Issuer (via API, email, or dashboard) for:
   - Payment received;
   - OFAC screening completed (clear/match);
   - Payment cleared/available;
   - Remittance initiated;
