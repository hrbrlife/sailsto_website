## ARTICLE 1: PURPOSE AND PRINCIPLES

**1.1 Purpose**

This Pricing and Fee Transparency Schedule ("**Schedule**") establishes the complete fee structure for Platform services provided by Sails DAO LLC ("**Company**") to Issuer Series ("**Series Issuers**"). This Schedule implements the pricing transparency principles set forth in Operating Agreement [DOC:B1 §10].

**1.2 Pricing Principles**

Company commits to the following pricing principles:

**(a) Transparency:** All fees fully disclosed in advance. No hidden fees. No surprise charges.

**(b) Cost-Plus Methodology:** Fees based on Company's actual costs plus reasonable margin:
- Direct costs (technology, compliance, legal, administrative)
- Allocated overhead
- Margin (disclosed percentage)

**(c) Audit Rights:** Series Issuers have right to audit fee calculations and cost allocations per Section 8 of this Schedule.

**(d) Competitive Pricing:** Fees competitive with industry alternatives. If Series Issuer obtains bona fide lower quote for equivalent services, Company will match or release Series Issuer from exclusivity.

**(e) Volume Discounts:** Fees reduced for higher volume Series Issuers per discount schedule.

**(f) No Conflicts:** Fee structure does not create conflicts of interest between Company and Series Issuers.

**1.3 Fee Categories**

Fees organized into following categories:
- Formation Fees (one-time)
- Platform Fees (ongoing)
- Transaction Fees (per-transaction)
- Technology Fees (usage-based)
- Compliance Fees (as incurred)
- Third-Party Pass-Through Fees

## ARTICLE 8: COST-PLUS METHODOLOGY AND AUDIT RIGHTS

**8.1 Cost-Plus Disclosure**

Upon Series Issuer written request (no more than once per year), Company shall provide:

**(a) Cost Breakdown:**
- Direct costs attributable to Series Issuer services
- Allocated overhead (methodology disclosed)
- Margin percentage

**(b) Cost Categories:**
- Technology (infrastructure, software, development)
- Compliance (personnel, tools, filings)
- Administrative (personnel, facilities, general)
- Third-party services (legal, accounting, other)

**(c) Margin Disclosure:**
- Target margin: [XX]% of direct costs
- Actual margin achieved (calculated)

**8.2 Audit Rights**

**(a) Right to Audit:** Series Issuer may audit Company's fee calculations and cost allocations:
- Upon 30 days' written notice
- At Series Issuer's expense (unless material discrepancy found)
- No more than once per calendar year
- Conducted by independent CPA acceptable to both parties

**(b) Audit Scope:**
- Verification of fees charged match this Schedule
- Verification of pass-through fees at cost
- Review of cost allocation methodology
- Review of margin calculations

**(c) Material Discrepancy:** If audit reveals overcharges >5% of fees paid:
- Company refunds overcharge plus interest (prime + 2%)
- Company pays audit costs
- Series Issuer may terminate with 30 days' notice

**(d) Confidentiality:** Audit results confidential. Auditor may not disclose Company proprietary information.

**8.3 Annual Fee Review**

Company shall conduct annual review of fee schedule:
- Review direct costs and market rates
- Adjust fees if costs materially change (up or down)
- Provide 60 days' notice of fee changes
- Series Issuer may terminate within 30 days of fee increase notice without penalty
