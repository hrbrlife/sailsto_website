## ARTICLE 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

**1.1 Purpose**

This Due Diligence Procedures document ("**Procedures**") establishes the framework for verifying investors and Series Issuers to ensure compliance with:

- Securities Act of 1933 (Regulation D exemptions)
- Anti-Money Laundering (AML) requirements
- OFAC sanctions compliance
- Know Your Customer (KYC) obligations
- Anti-fraud provisions
- Investor suitability (where applicable)

**1.2 Scope**

These Procedures apply to:

**(a) Investor Due Diligence:**
- All investors in any Series Issuer offering
- Accredited investor verification (Rule 506(b) and 506(c))
- Identity verification
- OFAC/sanctions screening
- Source of funds verification (risk-based)

**(b) Series Issuer Due Diligence:**
- All Series Issuer applicants
- Series Manager vetting
- Business verification
- Beneficial ownership identification
- Use of proceeds verification

**1.3 Definitions**

- **Accredited Investor:** Person meeting SEC criteria under Rule 501 of Regulation D
- **Beneficial Owner:** Individual who owns 25%+ of entity or exercises significant control
- **CIP:** Customer Identification Program
- **EDD:** Enhanced Due Diligence
- **PEP:** Politically Exposed Person
- **SDD:** Simplified Due Diligence

## ARTICLE 3: ACCREDITED INVESTOR VERIFICATION

**3.1 Accredited Investor Categories**

Per Rule 501 of Regulation D:

**(a) Natural Person - Income Test:**
- Individual income exceeding $200,000 in each of past 2 years, OR
- Joint income with spouse/partner exceeding $300,000 in each of past 2 years, AND
- Reasonable expectation of same level in current year

**(b) Natural Person - Net Worth Test:**
- Net worth exceeding $1,000,000 (individually or jointly with spouse/partner)
- EXCLUDING primary residence value
- Include any debt secured by primary residence only if exceeds home value

**(c) Professional Certifications:**
- Series 7 license (General Securities Representative)
- Series 65 license (Investment Adviser Representative)
- Series 82 license (Private Securities Offerings Representative)
- In good standing

**(d) Knowledgeable Employees:**
- Director, executive officer, or general partner of issuer
- Director, executive officer, or general partner of GP/managing member of issuer
- Employee participating in investment activities (certain conditions)

**(e) Entity - Assets Test:**
- Corporation, partnership, LLC, trust with assets exceeding $5,000,000
- Not formed for specific purpose of acquiring securities offered

**(f) Entity - Accredited Owners Test:**
- Entity in which all equity owners are accredited investors

**(g) Regulated Entities:**
- Banks, insurance companies, registered investment companies
- Employee benefit plans with assets exceeding $5,000,000
- Business development companies

**(h) Family Offices:**
- Family office with assets under management exceeding $5,000,000
- Not formed for specific purpose of acquiring securities offered
- Investment directed by person with financial sophistication

**3.2 Verification Methods - Rule 506(b)**

For Rule 506(b) offerings, Company may rely on **self-certification** by investor:

**(a) Acceptable Methods:**
- Investor questionnaire with representations
- Accredited investor self-certification
- Subscription agreement representations

**(b) Required Representations:**
- Specific accredited investor category
- Basis for qualification
- Acknowledgment of reliance

**(c) Reasonable Steps:**
- Review investor questionnaire for consistency
- Consider any contradictory information
- Document review and conclusions

**3.3 Verification Methods - Rule 506(c)**

For Rule 506(c) offerings, Company must take **reasonable steps to verify** accredited investor status:

**(a) Income Verification (Natural Persons):**

**Acceptable Documentation:**
- IRS forms (W-2, K-1, 1040) for past 2 years, AND
- Written representation regarding reasonable expectation for current year

**Alternative:**
- Written confirmation from CPA, attorney, registered broker-dealer, or SEC-registered investment adviser

**(b) Net Worth Verification (Natural Persons):**

**Assets - Acceptable Documentation:**
- Bank/brokerage statements (dated within 90 days)
- CPA certified financial statements
- Tax assessments for real property
- Appraisals for real estate
- Third-party valuation for business interests

**Liabilities - Acceptable Documentation:**
- Credit report (dated within 90 days)
- Investor certification of liabilities

**Alternative:**
- Written confirmation from CPA, attorney, registered broker-dealer, or SEC-registered investment adviser

**(c) Professional Certification Verification:**
- FINRA BrokerCheck verification
- Written confirmation of license status
- License documentation

**(d) Entity Verification:**

**Assets Test:**
- Audited financial statements, OR
- Bank/brokerage statements, OR
- Tax return (if assets evident), OR
- Written confirmation from CPA, attorney, etc.

**Accredited Owners Test:**
- Verify each equity owner meets accredited investor standards

**(e) Third-Party Verification Services:**
- Use SEC-approved verification letters from:
  - Registered broker-dealers
  - SEC-registered investment advisers
  - Licensed CPAs
  - Licensed attorneys

- Letter must:
  - Be dated within 90 days of investment
  - State category and basis for determination
  - Confirm verification steps taken
  - Be signed by qualified professional

**3.4 Verification Documentation**

**(a) Record Requirements:**

For each accredited investor, maintain:
- Category of accreditation
- Verification method used
- Supporting documentation
- Date of verification
- Reviewer name
- Determination

**(b) File Organization:**

```
/Investor Files/[Investor Name]/
  - Identity Verification/
    - Government ID
    - Address verification
  - Accredited Verification/
    - Questionnaire
    - Supporting documentation
    - Third-party letter (if applicable)

```

**3.5 Timing**

| **Offering Type** | **Verification Timing** |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| Rule 506(b) | Before subscription acceptance |
| Rule 506(c) | Before subscription acceptance; reasonable steps completed |
| Follow-on Investment | Re-verify if more than 3 years since prior verification |

## ARTICLE 4: ENHANCED DUE DILIGENCE (EDD)

**4.1 EDD Triggers**

Enhanced due diligence required for:

**(a) High-Risk Geographic Factors:**
- Investor residence in high-risk jurisdiction (FATF gray/black list)
- Funds from high-risk jurisdiction
- Connections to sanctioned countries

**(b) High-Risk Investor Factors:**
- Politically Exposed Person (PEP) or family/close associate
- Adverse media hits
- Complex or unusual ownership structure
- Shell company indicators
- Cash business involvement
- Investment amount inconsistent with apparent resources

**(c) High-Risk Transaction Factors:**
- Large investment relative to offering
- Unusual payment method
- Last-minute changes to payment instructions
- Requests for unusual terms or side letters

**(d) OFAC/Sanctions Factors:**
- Potential match requiring investigation
- Close association with sanctioned person
- Prior blocked transactions

**4.2 EDD Procedures**

**(a) Source of Funds:**

For high-risk investors, verify source of funds:

**Documentation Required:**
- Explanation of funds source (employment, business, inheritance, investment returns)
- Supporting documentation:
  - Employment: recent pay stubs, employment letter
  - Business: business financial statements, tax returns
  - Inheritance: probate documents, trust distribution
  - Investment: brokerage statement showing liquidation

**(b) Source of Wealth:**

For very high-risk investors, verify overall source of wealth:

**Documentation:**
- Summary of wealth accumulation
- Employment history with key positions
- Business ownership history
- Major asset documentation

**(c) PEP Procedures:**

**PEP Identification:**
- Screen against PEP databases
- Include domestic and foreign PEPs
- Include family members and close associates

**Additional Requirements:**
- Senior management approval for PEP investors
- Enhanced source of funds verification
- Enhanced ongoing monitoring
- Document rationale for accepting PEP investor

**(d) Adverse Media:**

**Search Scope:**
- Major news databases
- Regulatory enforcement databases
- Court records (civil and criminal)
- Industry publications

**Adverse Information:**
- Document findings
- Assess relevance and credibility
- Determine if disqualifying
- Senior approval if proceeding despite adverse information

**4.3 EDD Approval**

- CCO reviews all EDD cases
- Senior management approval for highest-risk cases
- Document approval rationale
- Implement enhanced monitoring

## ARTICLE 5: OFAC AND SANCTIONS SCREENING

**5.1 Screening Requirements**

Per [DOC:N1 Article 4] and OFAC regulations:

**(a) Who Is Screened:**
- All investors (individual and entity)
- All beneficial owners
- All control persons
- Series Issuers
- Series Managers

**(b) When Screening Occurs:**
- Before subscription acceptance
- Before payment acceptance
- At periodic intervals (ongoing monitoring)
- Upon list updates

**5.2 Screening Lists**

**(a) Primary Lists:**
- OFAC SDN (Specially Designated Nationals) List
- OFAC Consolidated Sanctions List
- OFAC Sectoral Sanctions Identifications List

**(b) Additional Lists:**
- UN Consolidated Sanctions List
- EU Consolidated Sanctions List
- UK Sanctions List
- Other applicable jurisdictions

**(c) Secondary Lists (as applicable):**
- FinCEN 311 Special Measures
- FATF High-Risk Jurisdictions
- PEP databases

**5.3 Screening Process**

**(a) Automated Screening:**
- Use approved screening vendor
- Screen full name (including variations)
- Screen date of birth
- Screen address/country
- Screen entity name
- Screen beneficial owners

**(b) Match Categories:**

| **Category** | **Description** | **Action** |
|--------------|-----------------|-----------|
| True Match | Confirmed sanctioned party | Block, reject, report |
| Potential Match | Requires investigation | Investigate, resolve |
| False Positive | Confirmed not sanctioned | Document, clear |
| No Match | No potential matches | Proceed |

**(c) Potential Match Resolution:**

For potential matches, investigate:
- Full name comparison (exact vs. similar)
- Date of birth comparison
- Address/country comparison
- Other identifying information
- Contact investor for additional information

**Resolution Documentation:**
- Match details
- Investigation steps
- Supporting information obtained
- Resolution determination
- Reviewer name and date

**5.4 Positive Match Procedures**

**(a) Immediate Actions:**
- Do NOT accept subscription
- Do NOT process payment
- Block any funds already received
- Do NOT return funds without OFAC guidance
- Escalate to CCO and legal counsel

**(b) Blocking:**
- Block assets if investor is SDN
- Report blocked property to OFAC within 10 business days
- Maintain blocked funds in interest-bearing account

**(c) OFAC Reporting:**

**Report Blocked Property:**
- File annual report by September 30
- Report blocking within 10 business days

**Voluntary Self-Disclosure:**
- Consider VSD for apparent violations
- Consult legal counsel before filing

**5.5 Ongoing Monitoring**

**(a) Periodic Rescreening:**
- Screen existing investors at least annually
- Screen upon notification of list updates
- Screen upon investor address/information changes

**(b) Update Monitoring:**
- Subscribe to OFAC list update notifications
- Re-screen within 24 hours of material list updates
- Document screening dates

## ARTICLE 7: PAYMENT VERIFICATION

**7.1 Accepted Payment Methods**

**(a) Preferred Methods:**
- Wire transfer from verified bank account
- ACH transfer from verified bank account

**(b) Restricted Methods:**
- Third-party payments (require additional verification)
- International wire transfers (require enhanced due diligence)
- Cash (NOT ACCEPTED)
- Cryptocurrency (case-by-case, enhanced due diligence)

**7.2 Bank Account Verification**

**(a) Requirements:**
- Bank account must be in investor's name
- Verify account ownership (micro-deposits, statement, or banking verification service)
- Document bank account information

**(b) Third-Party Payments:**
- Generally not accepted
- Exceptions require:
  - Documentation of relationship
  - Identity verification of third party
  - OFAC screening of third party
  - CCO approval

**7.3 Payment Verification Process**

**(a) Pre-Payment:**
- Complete identity verification
- Complete accredited investor verification
- Complete OFAC screening
- Approve subscription

**(b) Payment Receipt:**
- Verify payment amount matches subscription
- Verify payment source matches verified account
- Confirm no OFAC holds on wire

**(c) Post-Payment:**
- Reconcile payment to subscription
- Issue subscription confirmation
- Update investor records

## ARTICLE 8: FOREIGN INVESTOR PROCEDURES

**8.1 Additional Requirements for Non-U.S. Investors**

**(a) Identity Verification:**
- Valid passport (primary)
- Additional government-issued ID (if passport insufficient)
- Address verification (utility bill, bank statement)
- Enhanced database verification

**(b) Tax Documentation:**
- Form W-8BEN (individuals)
- Form W-8BEN-E (entities)
- Tax residency certification

**(c) FATCA Compliance:**
- FATCA status determination
- Necessary certifications
- Withholding requirements

**8.2 High-Risk Jurisdiction Procedures**

**(a) FATF Gray/Black List Countries:**
- Enhanced due diligence required
- Source of funds documentation required
- Senior management approval required
- Consider refusing investment

**(b) OFAC Comprehensive Sanctions:**
- Investment from Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Crimea region: PROHIBITED
- No exceptions without OFAC license

**(c) Sectoral Sanctions:**
- Review specific sectoral restrictions
- Ensure investment does not violate restrictions

**8.3 Documentation for Non-U.S. Investors**

| Passport copy | ✓ |
| Address verification | ✓ |
