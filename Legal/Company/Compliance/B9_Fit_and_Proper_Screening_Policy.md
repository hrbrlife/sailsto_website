## SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1. This Policy sets requirements for screening [TERM:Officers], key personnel, and controlled function roles to meet fit-and-proper standards.

1.2. This Policy applies to initial hiring, role changes, and periodic re-screening.

---

## SECTION 2: PRE-EMPLOYMENT SCREENING
[FLOW:Fit_Proper_Screening]
[OBL:OBL-OFF-001]

2.1. Background checks shall include identity verification, criminal history, sanctions screening, employment verification, and reference checks appropriate to role risk.

2.2. Roles affecting compliance, custody, finance, or technology require enhanced screening and conflicts-of-interest review.

2.3. Anti-corruption and ethics screening shall include:

2.3.1. Review of any prior involvement in bribery, corruption, fraud, or financial crime matters.

2.3.2. Politically exposed person screening for candidates and their close associates.

2.3.3. Adverse media review for ethics, integrity, and reputational concerns.

2.3.4. Disclosure of any ongoing investigations or regulatory proceedings.

2.4. Candidates with prior anti-corruption or ethics violations may be declined or subject to enhanced supervision and conditions.

### 2.5. Chief Compliance Officer / BSA Officer Enhanced Standards
[FLOW:CCO_Qualification]

2.5.1. In addition to standard screening requirements, candidates for the [TERM:CCO] position must meet the following enhanced standards:

2.5.2. **Professional Certification:** Hold current CAMS (Certified Anti-Money Laundering Specialist), ACAMS, or equivalent AML certification from a recognized professional body.

2.5.3. **Experience:** Minimum of three years of direct BSA/AML compliance experience in a money services business, bank, or other regulated financial institution.

2.5.4. **Regulatory Knowledge:** Demonstrated knowledge of FinCEN regulations, state money transmitter requirements, OFAC sanctions compliance, and travel rule obligations.
[DOC:B5]

2.5.5. **MSB Experience Preferred:** Prior experience as a compliance officer or BSA Officer at a registered MSB is strongly preferred.

2.5.6. **Capacity Assessment:** If the [TERM:CCO] candidate will serve multiple [TERM:ClientSeries] MSBs or multiple entities, the [TERM:Company] shall assess the candidate's capacity to fulfill BSA Officer duties for all entities effectively.

2.5.7. **Independence:** The [TERM:CCO] shall have sufficient independence and authority to implement the BSA/AML Program without undue interference, including direct reporting access to the [TERM:Manager].

2.5.8. **Continuing Education:** The [TERM:CCO] shall complete at least twenty hours of AML/BSA continuing education annually.
[OBL:OBL-REG-002]

## SECTION 3: PERIODIC RE-SCREENING
[OBL:OBL-REG-002]

3.1. Re-screening for regulated roles shall occur at least every two years or upon role change.

3.2. Adverse findings shall be escalated to the [TERM:CCO] and [TERM:CEO] for decision and remediation.

---

## SECTION 4: DECLARATIONS AND ATTESTATIONS

4.1. Covered personnel shall complete periodic personal and professional questionnaires, disclosing criminal, regulatory, financial, and conflict matters.

4.2. Attestations to [TERM:Company] policies, including AML, privacy, security, and anti-corruption, shall be renewed annually.
[DOC:B5] [DOC:B8] [DOC:B6]

4.3. Personnel shall attest annually that they have not engaged in bribery, corruption, or improper payments and have reported any concerns through appropriate channels.

---

## SECTION 5: TRAINING REQUIREMENTS

5.1. Role-based training shall be assigned and completed before performing regulated functions and renewed at least annually.
[OBL:OBL-REG-002]

5.2. Completion shall be documented and retained.
[DOC:B4]
