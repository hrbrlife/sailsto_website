## CERTIFICATION OVERVIEW

This Annual Self-Certification Checklist implements the compliance certification requirements of Operating Agreement [DOC:B1 §8.9]. The Chief Compliance Officer ("**CCO**") must complete this checklist annually, certifying that Company and all Series Issuers maintain compliance with applicable laws and internal policies.

**Certification Timeline:**
- Review Period: Calendar Year (January 1 - December 31)
- Completion Deadline: January 31 of following year
- Board Review: February Board meeting
- Remediation: Material deficiencies remediated within 60 days (or remediation plan filed)
