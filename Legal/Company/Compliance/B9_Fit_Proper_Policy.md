## ARTICLE 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

**1.1 Purpose**

This Fit and Proper Policy ("**Policy**") establishes standards and procedures for ensuring that all persons in positions of responsibility within Sails DAO LLC ("**Company**") and its Series Issuers are fit and proper to serve in their respective roles.

A robust fit and proper framework:
- Protects investor interests
- Maintains regulatory compliance
- Preserves Company reputation
- Reduces operational risk
- Ensures competent management

**1.2 Scope**

This Policy applies to:

**(a) Covered Persons:**
- Officers and Directors/Managers of the Company
- Governance Token holders with voting rights exceeding 10%
- Series Issuer managers and representatives
- Compliance Officer and AML Compliance Officer
- Information Security Officer
- Personnel with access to investor NPI
- Personnel with authority to execute transactions
- Personnel with access to Company or Series treasury
- Third-party service providers in key roles

**(b) Covered Activities:**
- Initial appointment/hiring
- Ongoing fitness assessment
- Material change notifications
- Periodic recertification

**1.3 Definitions**

- **Fit:** Having the competence, knowledge, experience, and qualifications appropriate for the role
- **Proper:** Having integrity, reputation, and character suitable for the role; absence of disqualifying events
- **Disqualifying Event:** Event that renders a person unsuitable for their role
- **Material Change:** Any change that may affect fitness or propriety determination

## ARTICLE 2: FITNESS STANDARDS

**2.1 Competence Requirements**

Covered Persons must possess competence appropriate to their role:

| **Role** | **Competence Requirements** |
|----------|----------------------------|
| Officers/Managers | Business management, corporate governance, relevant industry experience |
| Chief Compliance Officer | Regulatory knowledge, compliance program development, securities law familiarity |
| AML Compliance Officer | BSA/AML knowledge, SAR procedures, OFAC sanctions, CIP/KYC procedures |
| Information Security Officer | Cybersecurity expertise, risk management, incident response |
| Finance/Treasury | Accounting, financial controls, treasury management |
| Legal | Applicable legal qualifications, securities law, corporate law |
| Series Issuer Managers | Industry-specific expertise, business management |
| Technology | Technical competence in platform systems, smart contracts |

**2.2 Experience Requirements**

**(a) Officers and Managers:**
- Minimum 5 years relevant experience, or
- 3 years experience plus relevant advanced degree, or
- Equivalent combination of education and experience

**(b) Compliance Roles:**
- Minimum 3 years compliance experience
- Demonstrated regulatory knowledge
- Prior examination experience preferred

**(c) Series Issuer Managers:**
- Industry experience in the Series' business area
- Prior management/fiduciary experience
- Track record of successful business operations

**2.3 Educational and Professional Qualifications**

**(a) Relevant Qualifications (examples):**
- Advanced degrees (JD, MBA, relevant technical degrees)
- Professional certifications (CPA, CAMS, CIPP, CISSP, Series licenses)
- Industry-specific certifications
- Continuing education in role-specific areas

**(b) Documentation:**
- Verify educational credentials
- Confirm professional licenses current and in good standing
- Document in personnel file

**2.4 Ongoing Competence**

**(a) Continuing Education:**
- Annual training in role-specific topics
- Regulatory updates as applicable
- Industry developments

**(b) Performance Assessment:**
- Regular performance evaluations
- Competence monitoring
- Remediation for identified gaps

## ARTICLE 3: PROPRIETY STANDARDS

**3.1 Integrity and Reputation**

Covered Persons must demonstrate:
- Honesty and integrity in business dealings
- Ethical conduct
- Sound judgment
- Absence of conflicts of interest (or proper disclosure)
- Reputation consistent with position of trust

**3.2 Disqualifying Events**

The following constitute disqualifying events that may render a person unfit:

**(a) Criminal Matters:**
- Felony conviction (any type)
- Misdemeanor involving:
  - Fraud or dishonesty
  - Financial crimes
  - Securities violations
  - Money laundering
  - Tax evasion
  - Theft or embezzlement
- Pending criminal charges for above offenses
- Criminal conviction equivalent in foreign jurisdiction

**(b) Regulatory Matters:**
- SEC or state securities enforcement action
- FinCEN enforcement action
- Banking regulator enforcement action
- Bar from securities industry
- Revocation of professional license
- Cease and desist order
- Consent decree for misconduct
- Pending regulatory investigation (material)

**(c) Civil Matters:**
- Civil judgment for fraud
- Civil judgment for breach of fiduciary duty
- Civil judgment involving dishonesty
- Bankruptcy or insolvency (within past 7 years)
- Consent judgment in SEC action

**(d) Professional Matters:**
- Termination for cause from prior position
- License revocation or suspension
- Professional ethics violation findings
- Disbarment or suspension from practice

**(e) Other Matters:**
- OFAC sanctions list designation
- Refusal to submit to background check
- Material misrepresentation in application
- Pattern of regulatory violations
- Willful violation of Company policies

**3.3 Assessment of Disqualifying Events**

**(a) Automatic Disqualification:**
- Current OFAC sanctions listing
- Felony conviction within past 10 years for crimes involving fraud, dishonesty, or financial crimes
- SEC or state securities bar in effect
- Current FinCEN enforcement action

**(b) Discretionary Assessment:**

For other disqualifying events, consider:
- Nature and severity of offense
- Circumstances surrounding the event
- Time elapsed since event
- Subsequent conduct and rehabilitation
- Relevance to proposed role
- Regulatory requirements for the position
- Overall pattern of conduct

**3.4 Conflicts of Interest**

**(a) Disclosure Required:**
- Other business activities
- Board positions
- Ownership interests in competing or related businesses
- Family relationships with vendors/partners
- Financial interests affecting judgment

**(b) Conflict Management:**
- Recusal from conflicted decisions
- Disclosure to Board/Managers
- Ongoing monitoring
- Documentation of conflict management

## ARTICLE 4: VETTING PROCEDURES

**4.1 Initial Vetting**

**(a) Application Requirements:**

Candidates for Covered Person positions must provide:
- Resume/CV
- Employment history (10 years minimum)
- Educational credentials
- Professional licenses and certifications
- Disclosure of disqualifying events
- Authorization for background check
- References (professional)

**(b) Disclosure Questionnaire:**

Completed questionnaire covering:
- Criminal history
- Regulatory history
- Civil litigation history
- Bankruptcy history
- Disciplinary history
- Conflicts of interest
- Other relevant matters

**4.2 Background Checks**

**(a) Scope:**

| **Check Type** | **All Covered Persons** | **Officers/CCO/AML** | **Treasury Access** |
|----------------|------------------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Identity verification | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Criminal history (7 years) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Criminal history (10+ years) | | ✓ | ✓ |
| Civil records | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Bankruptcy records | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Education verification | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Employment verification | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Professional license verification | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| SEC/FINRA records (BrokerCheck) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| OFAC screening | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Credit history | | Discretionary | ✓ |
| Global sanctions screening | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Media/adverse news search | | ✓ | ✓ |

**(b) Background Check Provider:**
- Use reputable third-party provider
- FCRA-compliant procedures
- Global capabilities for international candidates

**(c) FCRA Compliance:**
- Provide required disclosures
- Obtain written authorization
- Pre-adverse action notice if applicable
- Adverse action procedures if denying based on report

**4.3 Reference Checks**

**(a) Professional References:**
- Minimum 3 professional references
- Prior supervisors preferred
- Verify claims and assess character

**(b) Reference Questions:**
- Confirm employment dates and titles
- Reason for departure
- Performance assessment
- Any concerns about integrity
- Rehire eligibility
- Role-specific competence

**4.4 Verification of Credentials**

**(a) Education:**
- Verify degrees from issuing institutions
- Confirm graduation dates
- Use verification service for efficiency

**(b) Professional Licenses:**
- Verify with licensing authority
- Confirm current and in good standing
- Check for disciplinary history

**(c) Employment History:**
- Verify with prior employers
- Confirm dates, titles, responsibilities
- Note any discrepancies

**4.5 Regulatory Screening**

**(a) SEC EDGAR:**
- Search for enforcement actions
- Review any required filings

**(b) FINRA BrokerCheck:**
- All Covered Persons screened
- Document results

**(c) OFAC Screening:**
- SDN list screening
- Consolidated sanctions list
- Per [DOC:N1 Article 4] screening procedures

**(d) State Regulatory:**
- State securities enforcement records
- State professional licensing records

## ARTICLE 5: APPROVAL PROCESS

**5.1 Approval Authority**

| **Position** | **Approval Authority** |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Officers/Managers | Board of Managers |
| Chief Compliance Officer | Board of Managers |
| Information Security Officer | Officer + CCO |
| Series Issuer Managers | Designated Company Officer |
| Employees with NPI access | Hiring Manager + CCO |
| Key service providers | Officer + CCO |

**5.2 Approval Requirements**

**(a) Documentation Package:**
- Background check results
- Disclosure questionnaire
- Reference check notes
- Credential verification
- Regulatory screening results
- Assessment summary
- Recommendation

**(b) Assessment Summary:**
- Overview of candidate
- Fitness assessment (competence, experience, qualifications)
- Propriety assessment (disqualifying events, integrity)
- Any concerns or conditions
- Recommendation (approve, conditional approval, deny)

**5.3 Conditional Approval**

Approval may be conditioned on:
- Obtaining specific credential
- Completing specific training
- Enhanced monitoring
- Restriction from certain activities
- Time-limited probationary period
- Additional vetting (e.g., credit check)

**5.4 Denial and Appeals**

**(a) Denial:**
- Document reasons for denial
- FCRA adverse action procedures if based on consumer report
- No obligation to disclose all reasons to candidate

**(b) Appeal:**
- Candidates may request reconsideration
- Provide additional information
- Final decision by approval authority

## ARTICLE 6: ONGOING MONITORING

**6.1 Annual Recertification**

**(a) Annual Requirements:**
- Update disclosure questionnaire annually
- Refresh OFAC screening annually
- Verify professional licenses current
- Review for changed circumstances

**(b) Recertification Form:**

Annual certification by Covered Person:
- No new disqualifying events
- No material changes to disclosed information
- Continued compliance with Policy
- Update of any changed information

**6.2 Continuous Monitoring**

**(a) OFAC Monitoring:**
- Screen against updates to sanctions lists
- Per [DOC:N1 Article 4.6] ongoing monitoring
- Immediate action if match identified

**(b) Regulatory Monitoring:**
- Monitor SEC enforcement announcements
- Monitor state enforcement actions
- Google Alerts for key personnel (discretionary)

**(c) Performance Monitoring:**
- Regular performance evaluations
- Competence assessment
- Policy compliance monitoring

**6.3 Material Change Reporting**

**(a) Self-Reporting Obligation:**

Covered Persons must promptly report (within 5 business days):
- Any criminal arrest or charge
- Any regulatory investigation notice
- Any civil lawsuit involving fraud/dishonesty
- Bankruptcy filing
- Professional license suspension/revocation
- Other potential disqualifying event

**(b) Failure to Report:**
- Failure to report material change is itself a disqualifying event
- May result in immediate suspension pending investigation
- Grounds for termination

**6.4 Reassessment Triggers**

Fitness and propriety must be reassessed upon:
- Self-reported material change
- Criminal conviction or regulatory action
- Change in role or responsibilities
- Information from external sources
- Complaint or allegation
- Periodic scheduled review

## ARTICLE 9: GOVERNANCE TOKEN HOLDERS

**9.1 Significant Token Holders**

Governance token holders with >10% voting rights:
- Subject to disclosure requirements
- Background check required
- OFAC screening required
- Annual recertification

**9.2 Vetting Process**

**(a) Initial Acquisition:**
- Disclosure questionnaire before acquisition above threshold
- Background check consent
- OFAC screening

**(b) Ongoing:**
- Annual recertification
- Material change reporting
- Ongoing OFAC monitoring

**9.3 Disqualification Consequences**

If significant token holder fails fit and proper:
- May not exercise voting rights until resolved
- Possible mandatory redemption per Operating Agreement
- Possible restrictions on further acquisition

## ARTICLE 11: COMPLIANCE ROLES SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS

**11.1 Chief Compliance Officer**

**(a) Qualifications:**
- 5+ years compliance experience
- Securities law knowledge
- AML/BSA familiarity
- Strong ethical character
- No regulatory enforcement history

**(b) Enhanced Vetting:**
- Extended background check (10+ years)
- Regulatory database searches
- Media/adverse news search
- Professional reference emphasis

**(c) Independence:**
- Direct reporting to Board
- Authority to escalate concerns
- Protection from retaliation

**11.2 AML Compliance Officer**

**(a) Qualifications:**
- CAMS certification preferred
- 3+ years AML experience
- BSA/OFAC knowledge
- SAR preparation experience

**(b) Ongoing Requirements:**
- Annual AML training
- Current on regulatory developments
- Active participation in AML community

**11.3 Information Security Officer**

**(a) Qualifications:**
- CISSP, CISM, or equivalent
- 5+ years security experience
- Incident response capability
- Risk management background

**(b) Ongoing Requirements:**
- Annual security certifications maintained
- Current on threat landscape
- Participation in security community
