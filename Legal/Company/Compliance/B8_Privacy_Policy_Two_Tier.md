## INTRODUCTION

Sails DAO LLC ("**Company**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") operates a securities offering platform enabling private placements of debt, equity, and convertible securities. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information of investors, prospective investors, and other individuals who interact with our services.

We employ a **Two-Tier Privacy Framework** to comply with applicable privacy laws in the United States and internationally:

- **Tier I (U.S. Investors):** Compliance with Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and other U.S. state privacy laws.

- **Tier II (International Investors):** Compliance with General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for EU/EEA/UK residents, and other applicable international privacy laws.

For EU/EEA/UK residents, we process personal data under the following legal bases:

Processing for our legitimate business interests (fraud prevention, security, service improvement, investor communications), balanced against your privacy rights.

**4.5 Vital Interests**
Processing necessary to protect vital interests (rare, emergency situations only).

## 8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

**8.1 Rights Under U.S. Law (Tier I)**

**GLBA Rights:**
- Receive privacy notice
- Opt out of certain information sharing with non-affiliates (limited sharing permitted)

**CCPA/CPRA Rights (California Residents):**
- **Know:** Request disclosure of personal information collected, used, and shared
- **Access:** Obtain copy of personal information
- **Delete:** Request deletion of personal information (subject to exceptions)
- **Correct:** Request correction of inaccurate information
- **Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing:** We do not sell/share, but you may exercise this right
- **Limit Use of Sensitive Information:** Limit use to necessary purposes
- **Non-Discrimination:** Equal service regardless of exercising privacy rights

**Other State Rights:**
Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and other states provide similar rights. Contact us to exercise rights under your state's law.

**8.2 Rights Under GDPR (Tier II - EU/EEA/UK)**

- **Access (Art. 15):** Obtain confirmation of processing and copy of personal data
- **Rectification (Art. 16):** Correct inaccurate personal data
- **Erasure (Art. 17):** Request deletion ("right to be forgotten") subject to legal retention requirements
- **Restriction (Art. 18):** Restrict processing in certain circumstances
- **Data Portability (Art. 20):** Receive personal data in machine-readable format
- **Object (Art. 21):** Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing
- **Withdraw Consent (Art. 7):** Withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing)
- **Lodge Complaint (Art. 77):** File complaint with supervisory authority

**8.3 How to Exercise Your Rights**

**Submit Request:**
- Email: privacy@sails.io
- Mail: Sails DAO LLC, Attn: Privacy Officer, [ADDRESS], Cheyenne, WY 82001
- Online: [Privacy Request Portal URL]

**Verification:**
We verify identity before fulfilling requests using:
- Account credentials (if registered user)
- Government-issued ID (for sensitive requests)
- Two data points matching our records

**Response Time:**
- CCPA: 45 days (extendable to 90 days with notice)
- GDPR: 30 days (extendable to 90 days with notice)

**Authorized Agents:**
You may authorize an agent to submit requests on your behalf with written authorization.

**8.4 Limitations on Rights**

Certain rights are subject to exceptions, including:
- Legal retention requirements (securities law, tax, AML)
- Ongoing litigation or regulatory proceedings
- Fraud prevention and security
- Free speech and journalistic purposes

We will explain any limitations when responding to your request.

## 9. SECURITY MEASURES

**9.1 Technical Safeguards**

- **Encryption:** AES-256 encryption at rest; TLS 1.2+ in transit
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access, multi-factor authentication, least privilege
- **Network Security:** Firewalls, intrusion detection, segmentation
- **Monitoring:** 24/7 security monitoring, logging, alerting

**9.2 Organizational Safeguards**

- **Policies:** Written information security policy, incident response plan
- **Training:** Annual security awareness training for all personnel
- **Vendor Management:** Security assessments of third-party providers
- **Audits:** Annual penetration testing, SOC 2 Type II audits

**9.3 Data Breach Response**

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information:
- We will notify you within 72 hours of discovery (or as required by law)
- Notification will describe the breach, data affected, and remediation steps
- We will offer credit monitoring if SSN or financial data is compromised
- We will notify regulators as required by law
