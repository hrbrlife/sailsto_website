## SECTION 2: PHASE 1 FORMATION

Phase 1 involves filing formation documents with the [TERM:Montana] Montana Secretary of State.

### 2.1. Step 1 Prepare [DOC:A1] Articles of Organization

2.1.1. Complete all bracketed fields in document [DOC:A1] A1_Articles_of_Organization.md.

2.1.2. Verify entity name availability at biz.sosmt.gov before filing.

2.1.3. Select a [TERM:RegisteredAgent] registered agent, either a Commercial Registered Agent or a noncommercial registered agent with a [TERM:Montana] Montana physical address.

2.1.4. Obtain [TERM:RegisteredAgent] registered agent consent to the appointment.

2.1.5. Complete [DOC:A2] Exhibit A, which is the Series List contained in document A2_Exhibit_A_Series_List.md.

2.1.6. Complete [DOC:A3] Exhibit B, which contains the Series Operating Agreements in document A3_Exhibit_B_Series_Operating_Agreements.md.

2.1.7. Have the [TERM:Organizer] Organizer sign the [DOC:A1] Articles of Organization.

### 2.2. Step 2 File [DOC:A1] Articles of Organization

2.2.1. Go to biz.sosmt.gov and select Start a Business, then select Articles of Organization.

2.2.2. Select [TERM:SeriesLLC] Series Limited Liability Company as the entity type.

2.2.3. Enter all information from document [DOC:A1].

2.2.4. Upload [DOC:A2] Exhibit A and [DOC:A3] Exhibit B as attachments in PDF format.

2.2.5. Pay the filing fee. The base filing fee is seventy dollars per the official Montana Series LLC Articles of Organization form. The series fee is fifty dollars per series member multiplied by ten series, totaling five hundred dollars. The subtotal is five hundred seventy dollars. Optional twenty-four hour expedite costs an additional twenty dollars (totaling ninety dollars base). Optional one-hour expedite costs an additional one hundred dollars (totaling one hundred seventy dollars base). Verify current fees at sos.mt.gov before filing.

2.2.6. Save the confirmation number provided.

2.2.7. Download the filed and stamped [DOC:A1] Articles of Organization for your records.
