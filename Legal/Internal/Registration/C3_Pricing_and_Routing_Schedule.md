## SECTION 4: TRANSACTION FEES

<!-- [FLOW:Transaction_Pricing] -->

4.1. Transaction fees are calculated as a percentage markup on the [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs incurred by the [TERM:Company] Company to process each transaction.

4.1.1. **Definition of [TERM:DirectCosts] Direct Costs.** "Direct Costs" means only those costs that are: (a) actually incurred by the [TERM:Company] Company; (b) directly attributable to a specific transaction or group of transactions; and (c) charged by a third-party provider in connection with processing the transaction. [TERM:DirectCosts] Direct Costs include the following categories:

   (a) **[TERM:PaymentProcessor] Payment Processor Fees:** Per-transaction fees charged by [TERM:PaymentProcessor] payment processors, acquirers, or payment facilitators for processing the transaction;

   (b) **[TERM:BankingPartner] Banking Fees:** Wire transfer fees, [TERM:ACH] ACH fees, [TERM:SWIFT] SWIFT charges, and similar fees charged by [TERM:CorrespondentBank] correspondent banks, [TERM:IntermediaryBank] intermediary banks, or receiving banks;

   (c) **[TERM:ForeignExchange] Foreign Exchange Spreads:** The actual spread or margin charged by the [TERM:ForeignExchange] foreign exchange provider between the interbank rate and the rate provided to the [TERM:Company] Company for the transaction, documented by reference to the provider's quoted rate at the time of execution;

   (d) **[TERM:CorrespondentBank] Correspondent and [TERM:IntermediaryBank] Intermediary Charges:** Fees charged by [TERM:CorrespondentBank] correspondent banks, [TERM:IntermediaryBank] intermediary financial institutions, or payment networks for routing or settling the transaction;

   (e) **Regulatory and Compliance Levies:** Transaction-specific fees imposed by regulators, clearing systems, or industry utilities (such as [TERM:OFAC] OFAC screening fees charged per transaction by third-party providers).

4.1.2. **Exclusions from [TERM:DirectCosts] Direct Costs.** [TERM:DirectCosts] Direct Costs expressly exclude: (a) the [TERM:Company] Company's internal overhead, salaries, rent, utilities, or general administrative expenses; (b) technology development or maintenance costs (which are covered by the [TERM:ITLicenseFee] IT License Fee); (c) compliance program costs not charged per transaction by a third party; (d) marketing, sales, or business development expenses; and (e) any costs that cannot be documented with third-party invoices or statements.

4.1.3. **Documentation.** The [TERM:Company] Company shall maintain documentation from third-party providers substantiating all [TERM:DirectCosts] Direct Costs charged to clients. Upon reasonable request by a client (not more than once per calendar quarter), the [TERM:Company] Company shall provide a summary of the categories of [TERM:DirectCosts] Direct Costs incurred for that client's transactions during the preceding quarter.

4.2. The markup percentage varies based on the [TERM:RiskClassification] risk classification of the transaction and the monthly transaction volume of the [TERM:ClientSeries] client series. Lower-risk transactions and higher volumes receive more favorable pricing, reflecting the reduced operational cost and improved efficiency of mature client relationships.

### 4.3. [TERM:LowRiskTransaction] Low-Risk Transaction Pricing

4.3.1. [TERM:LowRiskTransaction] Low-risk transactions are those involving standard payment rails, established counterparties, low-risk jurisdictions, and routine transaction patterns within normal parameters.

4.3.2. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume below one hundred thousand dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:LowRiskTransaction] low-risk transactions is forty percent.

4.3.3. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume between one hundred thousand dollars and five hundred thousand dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:LowRiskTransaction] low-risk transactions is thirty percent.

4.3.4. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume between five hundred thousand dollars and one million dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:LowRiskTransaction] low-risk transactions is twenty percent.

4.3.5. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume exceeding one million dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:LowRiskTransaction] low-risk transactions is fifteen percent.

### 4.4. [TERM:MediumRiskTransaction] Medium-Risk Transaction Pricing

4.4.1. [TERM:MediumRiskTransaction] Medium-risk transactions are those involving elevated risk factors such as higher-risk jurisdictions, [TERM:EDD] enhanced due diligence customers, larger transaction amounts, or non-standard transaction patterns requiring additional review.

4.4.2. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume below one hundred thousand dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:MediumRiskTransaction] medium-risk transactions is forty-five percent.

4.4.3. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume between one hundred thousand dollars and five hundred thousand dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:MediumRiskTransaction] medium-risk transactions is thirty-five percent.

4.4.4. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume between five hundred thousand dollars and one million dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:MediumRiskTransaction] medium-risk transactions is twenty-five percent.

4.4.5. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume exceeding one million dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:MediumRiskTransaction] medium-risk transactions is twenty percent.

### 4.5. [TERM:HighRiskTransaction] High-Risk Transaction Pricing

4.5.1. [TERM:HighRiskTransaction] High-risk transactions are those involving significant risk factors such as high-risk jurisdictions, complex structures, [TERM:PEP] politically exposed persons, large value transfers, or transaction patterns requiring substantial compliance resources.

4.5.2. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume below one hundred thousand dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:HighRiskTransaction] high-risk transactions is fifty percent.

4.5.3. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume between one hundred thousand dollars and five hundred thousand dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:HighRiskTransaction] high-risk transactions is forty-five percent.

4.5.4. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume between five hundred thousand dollars and one million dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:HighRiskTransaction] high-risk transactions is thirty-five percent.

4.5.5. For [TERM:ClientSeries] client series with monthly volume exceeding one million dollars, the markup on [TERM:DirectCosts] direct costs for [TERM:HighRiskTransaction] high-risk transactions is thirty percent.

### 4.6. Included Services

4.6.1. Transaction fees include all [TERM:ComplianceServices] compliance services, [TERM:TransactionMonitoring] transaction monitoring, [TERM:SanctionsScreening] sanctions screening, [TERM:RegulatoryReporting] regulatory reporting, customer support, dispute resolution, and operational management. There are no separate charges for these services.

4.6.2. Transaction fees include access to the full range of payment rails and providers available through the [TERM:Company] Company's network, with routing optimized as described in Section 6.

### 4.7. [TERM:PartnerMSB] Partner MSB Transaction Fees

4.7.1. Transactions routed through a [TERM:PartnerMSB] Partner MSB for interstate or multi-state transmission are subject to additional fees charged by the [TERM:PartnerMSB] Partner MSB for utilizing its state licenses.

4.7.2. [TERM:PartnerMSB] Partner MSB fees are passed through to the [TERM:ClientSeries] Client Series at cost plus a coordination markup of ten percent. These fees are in addition to the transaction fees described in Sections 4.3 through 4.5.

4.7.3. Typical [TERM:PartnerMSB] Partner MSB fees range from twenty-five basis points to one hundred basis points of transaction value, depending on the destination state, transaction type, and [TERM:PartnerMSB] Partner MSB pricing.

4.7.4. The [TERM:Company] Company shall provide clients with a breakdown of [TERM:PartnerMSB] Partner MSB fees on transaction records. Clients may request a summary of [TERM:PartnerMSB] Partner MSB fee categories and typical ranges upon onboarding.

4.7.5. Transactions that can be completed within [TERM:Montana] Montana or through crypto-exempt state pathways (such as [TERM:Wyoming] Wyoming's virtual currency exemption) are not subject to [TERM:PartnerMSB] Partner MSB fees.

## SECTION 6: ROUTING PHILOSOPHY AND PROVIDER ALLOCATION

<!-- [FLOW:Routing] -->

6.1. The [TERM:Company] Company maintains relationships with multiple [TERM:BankingPartner] banking partners, [TERM:PaymentProcessor] payment processors, [TERM:ForeignExchange] foreign exchange providers, and service providers across jurisdictions. The [TERM:Company] Company continuously seeks to expand this network to improve options, reliability, and pricing for clients.

6.2. For each transaction, the [TERM:Company] Company routes through the most cost-effective compliant option available, considering the destination country, transaction speed requirements, [TERM:RiskClassification] risk classification, transaction amount, [TERM:ForeignExchange] foreign exchange considerations, and provider reliability.

6.3. The [TERM:Company] Company may also consider internal operational factors in routing decisions, including minimum usage requirements with providers, diversification of provider relationships, and capacity management. These factors are applied fairly across all clients and are not used to disadvantage any individual client.

6.4. Clients benefit from the [TERM:Company] Company's aggregated volume across all [TERM:ClientSeries] client series, which enables access to institutional pricing and provider relationships that would not be available to individual clients operating independently.

6.5. The [TERM:Company] Company does not guarantee that any specific provider or rail will be available for any specific transaction. Provider availability may change due to regulatory requirements, provider policies, capacity constraints, or other factors outside the [TERM:Company] Company's control.

6.6. The [TERM:Company] Company provides clients with transaction records showing the routing used for each transaction, enabling clients to understand how their transactions are processed and verify that routing is consistent with this Schedule.

## SECTION 8: AUDIT AND INSPECTION RIGHTS

<!-- [FLOW:Client_Audit] -->

8.1. Upon not less than thirty days prior written notice, a client may, at its sole expense, engage an independent certified public accountant or other qualified professional (the "[TERM:Auditor] Auditor") to audit the [TERM:Company] Company's invoices and supporting documentation for [TERM:DirectCosts] Direct Costs charged to that client during the preceding twelve-month period.

8.2. The [TERM:Auditor] Auditor must execute a [TERM:ConfidentialityAgreement] confidentiality agreement in form acceptable to the [TERM:Company] Company before any audit commences. The audit shall be limited to verification of [TERM:DirectCosts] Direct Costs and compliance with this Schedule and shall not extend to the [TERM:Company] Company's proprietary systems, pricing negotiations with providers, or other clients' information.

8.3. The audit shall be conducted during normal business hours at the [TERM:Company] Company's offices or via secure remote access, as determined by the [TERM:Company] Company. The [TERM:Company] Company shall provide reasonable cooperation and access to invoices, transaction records, and provider statements relevant to the requesting client's transactions.

8.4. If the audit reveals that the [TERM:Company] Company overcharged the client by more than five percent of the audited amount for any quarterly period, the [TERM:Company] Company shall: (a) refund the overcharged amount within thirty days; and (b) reimburse the client's reasonable audit costs for that period, up to a maximum of ten thousand dollars.

8.5. If the audit reveals no overcharge, or an overcharge of five percent or less, the client shall bear all audit costs and the [TERM:Company] Company shall refund only the actual overcharged amount, if any.

8.6. Clients may exercise audit rights under this Section not more than once per calendar year, and audit requests must be made within eighteen months of the period being audited. This right survives termination of the [TERM:ClientServicesAgreement] Client Services Agreement for eighteen months.

## SECTION 9: FEE DISPUTE RESOLUTION

<!-- [FLOW:Fee_Dispute] -->

9.1. **Notice of Dispute.** If a client disputes any invoice or fee charged under this Schedule, the client shall provide written notice to the [TERM:Company] Company within thirty days of the invoice date, specifying: (a) the invoice or fee in dispute; (b) the amount disputed; and (c) the basis for the dispute with reasonable supporting detail.

9.2. **Good Faith Reconciliation.** Within fifteen business days of receiving a fee dispute notice, the parties shall confer in good faith to review the disputed charges, exchange relevant documentation, and attempt to reconcile the dispute. The [TERM:Company] Company shall provide transaction-level detail and supporting documentation for disputed [TERM:DirectCosts] Direct Costs upon request.

9.3. **Payment Pending Resolution.** The client shall pay the undisputed portion of any invoice when due. Failure to pay undisputed amounts shall constitute a [TERM:MaterialBreach] material breach notwithstanding any pending fee dispute. The disputed portion shall be held in abeyance pending resolution but shall accrue interest at the rate specified in Section 10.4 if ultimately determined to be owed.

9.4. **[TERM:Arbitration] Arbitration of Fee Disputes.** If the parties cannot resolve a fee dispute through good faith reconciliation within thirty days, either party may submit the dispute to binding [TERM:Arbitration] arbitration in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions of the [TERM:ClientServicesAgreement] Client Services Agreement ([DOC:B11], Section 16). The arbitrator's authority in fee disputes shall be limited to: (a) interpreting and applying the terms of this Schedule; (b) determining whether fees were calculated in accordance with this Schedule; and (c) ordering refunds or payments as appropriate. The arbitrator shall have no authority to modify, amend, or reform the fee structure set forth in this Schedule.

9.5. **Limitations.** Fee disputes not raised within thirty days of the applicable invoice date are waived, except for claims of fraud or intentional misconduct. The maximum recovery in any fee dispute shall be the amount actually overcharged plus interest; consequential, punitive, or exemplary damages are not recoverable in fee disputes.
