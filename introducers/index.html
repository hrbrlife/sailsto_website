Who can be an introducer?

Anyone with business relationships can participate. You don't need a license because you're not advising, selling, or handling funds. You're simply making an introduction.

Accountants, lawyers, consultants, executives, board members, industry contacts. If you know people who need capital, you can earn from that relationship.

  • Accountants & Advisors

    You see your clients' financials. You know who's ready to raise and who could benefit from structured capital.

  • Lawyers & Consultants

    Corporate counsel and business consultants often know when clients are exploring funding options.

  • Industry Contacts

    Trade associations, industry groups, business networks. You hear about expansion plans before anyone else.

  • Former Colleagues

    Know a founder, CEO, or CFO from your network? That relationship has value.

What businesses are we looking for?

Established businesses with real revenue and a clear use of funds. Not early-stage startups. Not speculative ventures. Companies that would traditionally raise from banks or private equity, but want more flexibility.

Our sweet spot: $600K to $10M raises for expansion, equipment, working capital, or project finance. All offerings have a soft cap (minimum) and hard cap (maximum).

  • Revenue-Generating

    At least 2 years operating history with demonstrable revenue.

  • Clear Use of Funds

    Expansion, equipment, inventory, project finance, working capital.

  • Professional Management

    Proper books, governance, and willingness to provide disclosure.
