For visionary businesses and pragmatic investors alike, Sails.to is sovereign infrastructure to issue, manage, and trade bonds across DeFi/TradFi borders — on Solana, TON (Telegram), or via traditional bank and broker rails (ISIN/Clearstream).

Imagine a bond that can sit in a Solana wallet today and a Swiss bank account tomorrow — same instrument, same rights, different format. That's what we built. Solana token, TON wrapped asset, or ISIN held at any brokerage: pick one, switch later, your ownership doesn't change.

The catch? You have to be a KYC-verified professional investor. No exceptions. But once you're in, you choose where and how you hold — and you can move between formats whenever you want.

If you're raising capital, you have a choice. Run the subscription yourself — verify investors, collect funds, keep your data private. Or hand distribution to a network of licensed OTC dealers and let them bring the buyers. Mix both if you like.

Same network handles secondary trading. No public exchange, no order book tourists. Just verified buyers and sellers, matched quietly.
