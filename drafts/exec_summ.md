Brand Portfolio & Super-App Strategy
Multi-brand approach (Unilever model): Each brand targets specific customer pain points

• instabroker.app (brokerage)
• instatrust.app (instant personal SVG trust)

Why multiple brands and channels?
• Core service dependency: Each financial service naturally requires complementary services (e.g.,
company formation needs banking, banking needs investment options)
• Unilever-style segmentation: Like Unilever markets different soap brands to different
demographics, we market specific financial solutions to specific pain points
• Cost-effective with AI: AI assistance and SMM make content generation and parallel marketing
streams economically viable
• Larger market capture: Multiple entry points capture customers who might not respond to a
generic “financial services” offering
Super-app philosophy: Capture customers through individual service needs, then seamlessly open entire
ecosystem post-onboarding. Our technology stack makes this realistic and safe to execute at scale.
