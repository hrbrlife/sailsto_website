AUTHORITATIVE FEE STRUCTURE (CORRECTED January 23, 2026)
================================================================================

THIS IS THE TRUTH ABOUT FEES - ALL PAGES MUST REFLECT THIS:

DURING SOFT CAP PHASE (Phase A / Stage 1: Escrow):
--------------------------------------------------
• NO brokerage fees (0.5%) charged - this is a built-in discount
• NO distribution fees (1% or 6%) charged yet - only applied if soft cap reached
• ONLY FEE: Clearstream fees (if Channel 3 used) - at cost, no markup
• Purpose: Smooth reimbursement if soft cap fails

WHEN SOFT CAP IS REACHED:
-------------------------
• Distribution fees (1% referral OR 6% broker-sourced) are deducted from 
  the SOFT CAP AMOUNT (not ongoing amounts)
• Funds released to issuer minus these fees
• After soft cap: selling party is partner broker (normal fees apply)

AFTER SOFT CAP (Phase B/C):
---------------------------
• Normal fee structure applies:
  - 0.5% brokerage on trades (⅓ platform, ⅓ buy-side, ⅓ sell-side)
  - Distribution fees on new subscriptions
  - All standard fees operational

IF SOFT CAP NOT REACHED:
------------------------
• Full reimbursement to investors (no brokerage, no distribution fees charged)
• Only Clearstream fees (if used) are non-refundable
• This is why soft cap phase has NO transaction fees - enables clean refund

SECONDARY TRADING (Post Phase C):
---------------------------------
• 0.5% brokerage fee applies
• Split: ⅓ platform, ⅓ buy-side broker, ⅓ sell-side broker
