Your Obligations as an Issuer

In exchange for access to capital, you agree to specific obligations that protect investors and ensure transparency.

Pre-Issuance Requirements

Due Diligence Documents

  • Financial statements — 1-3 years of CPA-prepared or audited financials
  • KYC/AML verification — All officers and beneficial owners verified
  • Letters of comfort — From your bank, accountant, and lawyer confirming standing
  • Business plan — Detailed plan for the project being funded
  • Use of funds — Clear breakdown of how capital will be deployed
  • Repayment plan — How investors will be repaid (schedule, sources, contingencies)
  • Legal opinion — Your lawyer confirms you can legally receive funds through your DAO LLC
  • Asset/liability disclosure — Certified info on pledges, loans, cashflow, assets, liabilities
  • DAO LLC formation — Wyoming DAO Series LLC registered and operating agreement in place

Step-by-Step

1 Form Your DAO Series LLC

Create your Wyoming DAO Series LLC with the required Series:

  • Operating Series — will hold pledges and receive revenue
  • Treasury Series — will hold reserves and handle distributions
  • ISIN Lockbox Series — optional, for traditional rails conversion

We can help with formation or work with your existing structure.

2 Submit Application

Submit your application with:

  • Company overview and history
  • Funding amount requested (soft cap and hard cap)
  • Use of funds summary
  • Proposed return structure
  • Available collateral/security

Timeline: Review within 2-5 business days

3 Due Diligence

Submit all required documentation. The Foundation and Trust verify everything.

Timeline: 1-2 weeks (can be faster with prepared documents)

4 Engage Brokers

Connect with licensed brokers who will distribute your offering to their investor networks:

  • Brokers review your offering materials
  • Agree on distribution terms
  • Your offering becomes available to their professional investor clients

5 Sign Agreements

Execute the legal documents:

  • DAO LLC Operating Agreement (your governance structure)
  • Trust Services Agreement (escrow and distribution)
  • Broker Distribution Agreements
  • Security/Pledge Agreements

6 Pledges to Operating Series

All security interests are perfected and held in your Operating Series — filings made, collateral transferred or encumbered, personal guarantees executed.

7 Tokens Minted

Security tokens are minted (amount = hard cap) and placed in your Treasury. The project agreement is now tokenized.

8 Distribution Opens

Your offering goes live across the three distribution channels:

  • Channel 1: Direct sales to your known investors (0.5% fee)
  • Channel 2: Broker network (6% fee for investors they source)
  • Channel 3: ISIN / Clearstream (6% fee + conversion fees) — you self-register the ISIN

Optional early-bird discount can incentivize early investment.

9 Soft Cap Reached → Funds Released

Once soft cap is reached, the Trust releases funds to you. Distribution continues to hard cap. If soft cap is not reached by duration end, investors receive zero-coupon redemption.

10 Ongoing Operations

Submit reports, make scheduled payments through the Trust, and comply with covenants until maturity. Your Melusina OS instance manages everything.

How "Known vs Unknown" Works

If you bring your own investors (partners, existing contacts, your network):

  • They use your referral code when investing
  • You pay only 0.5% on those investments

If the platform's distribution network finds new investors for you:

  • You pay 6% on those investments
  • This compensates the brokers and platform for sourcing capital

The channel (Solana direct, broker OTC, Clearstream) doesn't matter — only whether you already knew the investor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really control my own DAO LLC?

Yes. The DAO LLC is yours — you form it, you own it, you control it. The Foundation sets standards you agree to follow, and the Trust provides escrow/distribution services, but the securities are in your legal structure. This is fundamentally different from platforms that custody your assets.

What if I want to raise less than $600,000?

The minimum raise is $600,000 to ensure the economics work for all parties and sufficient investor protection structures can be put in place. For smaller amounts, consider traditional financing options.

How do I reach investors?

You work with licensed brokers who have professional investor clients. You can also bring your own investors (Channel 1 at 0.5% fee). Brokers handle KYC and onboarding for their clients.

Can I self-register an ISIN?

Yes. If you want your security available on traditional rails (Clearstream), you can register your own ISIN directly with a National Numbering Agency. Your ISIN Lockbox Series handles the 1:1 conversion between Solana tokens and ISIN securities.

What happens if I can't make a scheduled payment?

Contact the Trust immediately. They will work with you on options — payment deferrals, restructuring, or in severe cases, enforcement of security interests held in your Operating Series. Transparency and early communication are key.

How long does the process take?

With prepared documentation and an existing DAO LLC: as fast as 1-2 weeks from application to tokens minted. Add 1-2 weeks if you need to form the LLC. Distribution period length is up to you.
