For Issuers: Your Complete Guide to Fundraising

Sails.to enables companies to raise capital from professional investors through tokenized securities. This guide explains everything you need to know as an issuer — from eligible raise amounts to your ongoing obligations.

Raise Amounts & Structure

Sails.to offerings are structured in standardized increments to ensure operational efficiency and investor protection:

Parameter Value Notes
Minimum Raise $600,000 Soft cap cannot be lower than this
Increment Size $150,000 All caps must be multiples of this amount
Maximum Raise No hard limit Subject to regulatory considerations and platform approval

Example Raise Structures

Small raise: Soft cap $600K → Hard cap $900K
Medium raise: Soft cap $1.5M → Hard cap $3M
Large raise: Soft cap $5M → Hard cap $10M

All amounts in $150K increments. Soft cap = minimum to proceed; Hard cap = maximum accepted.

What This Means for You

  • Isolated liability: Each Series is legally separate — problems with other issuances don't affect yours
  • Professional management: The Series LLC is run by Sails.to with fiduciary duties to security holders
  • Your representation: You (or your designee) can sit on the Series Council in a minority position
  • Clear mandate: The Council's sole purpose is to protect the interests of the people who invested in your offering

Series Council Governance

Aspect Details
Composition Sails.to representatives (majority) + Issuer representative (minority)
Mandate Protect the interests of security holders — not the issuer, not the platform
Powers Approve disbursements, enforce covenants, manage defaults, approve restructurings
Your Role Voice in discussions, visibility into decisions, but not control
Think of it like a trust: Sails.to acts as trustee with a legal duty to the beneficiaries (investors). You're the borrower with a seat at the table, but the trustee's job is to make sure investors are protected.

Your Obligations as an Issuer

In exchange for access to capital, you agree to specific obligations that protect investors and ensure transparency.

Pre-Issuance Requirements

Due Diligence Documents

  • Financial statements — 1-3 years of CPA-prepared or audited financials
  • KYC/AML verification — All officers and beneficial owners verified on the platform
  • Letters of comfort — From your bank, accountant, and lawyer confirming standing
  • Business plan — Detailed plan for the project being funded
  • Use of funds — Clear breakdown of how capital will be deployed
  • Repayment plan — How investors will be repaid (schedule, sources, contingencies)
  • Legal opinion — Your lawyer confirms you can legally receive funds from the Series LLC
  • Asset/liability disclosure — Certified info on pledges, loans, cashflow, assets, liabilities

Security Interests & Pledges

The Series LLC will require security interests to protect investors:

🔒 Audit Rights

The Series LLC receives audit rights over your company. This means:

  • Right to inspect books and records
  • Right to commission independent audits
  • Right to verify use of proceeds

📋 Pledges & Collateral

Depending on your raise amount and structure, you may provide:

  • Security interests in company assets
  • Personal guarantees from principals
  • Pledge of equity or specific collateral
  • Assignment of receivables or contracts

🏛️ Board Representation (Large Issuances)

For larger raises, the Series LLC may request:

  • Observer seat on your board of directors
  • Or full board seat with voting rights
  • Information rights beyond standard reporting

⚠️ All Terms in Your Offer

The specific security interests, pledges, and governance rights will be detailed in your customized offer after you submit your initial application. These vary based on:

  • Raise amount
  • Your company's financial strength
  • Quality of collateral available
  • Track record and management team

Once your raise is complete and funds are deployed, you have continuing obligations to investors:

All reports must be digitally signed by an authorized officer on the Sails.to platform. This creates:

  • Immutable audit trail
  • Legal accountability for statements made
  • Timestamp proof anchored to blockchain

Investor Communications

All investor communications must go through the platform. This includes:

What Must Go Through the Platform

  • Progress updates and reports
  • Announcements and notices
  • Responses to investor inquiries
  • Requests for waivers or modifications
  • Distribution notices
  • Any material information

✅ Why This Matters

Centralized communications ensure:

  • Equal access: All investors receive information simultaneously
  • Audit trail: Complete record of what was communicated and when
  • Compliance: Platform can verify disclosures meet regulatory requirements
  • Efficiency: One place to manage all investor relations

Step-by-Step

1 Submit Initial Application

Fill out the application form with basic information about your company and funding needs. This includes:

  • Company overview and history
  • Funding amount requested (soft cap and hard cap)
  • Use of funds summary
  • Proposed return structure
  • Available collateral/security

2 Initial Review

Our team reviews your application for basic eligibility. We'll come back with initial questions or move you to due diligence.

Timeline: 2-5 business days

3 Due Diligence

Submit all required documentation (see checklist above). Our team and third-party service providers verify everything.

Timeline: 1-2 weeks (can be faster with prepared documents)

4 Receive Your Offer

Based on due diligence, you receive a customized offer detailing:

  • Approved raise amount (soft cap / hard cap)
  • Fee structure applicable to your raise
  • Required security interests and pledges
  • Governance arrangements (Council composition, board rights if any)
  • Reporting requirements and covenants
  • Distribution channel options

5 Sign Agreements

If you accept the offer, you sign:

  • Series Operating Agreement (your role in the Series LLC)
  • Loan/Investment Agreement (terms of the funding)
  • Security Agreement (pledges and collateral)
  • Platform Services Agreement

6 Pledges Secured

All security interests are perfected — filings made, collateral transferred or encumbered, personal guarantees executed.

7 Tokens Minted

Security tokens are minted (amount = hard cap) and placed in your issuer treasury. The project agreement is now tokenized.

8 Distribution Opens

Your offering goes live across the three distribution channels:

  • Channel 1: Direct sales to your known investors (0.5% fee)
  • Channel 2: Broker OTC network (6% fee for unknown investors)
  • Channel 3: ISIN / Clearstream (6% fee + conversion fees)

Optional Phase 1 discount can incentivize early investment.

9 Soft Cap Reached → Funds Released

Once soft cap is reached, funds are released to you. Distribution can continue to hard cap. If soft cap is not reached by duration end, investors receive zero-coupon redemption.

10 Ongoing Relationship

Submit reports, communicate with investors through the platform, make scheduled payments, and comply with covenants until maturity.

How "Known vs Unknown" Works

If you bring your own investors (partners, existing contacts, your network):

  • They use your referral code when investing
  • You pay only 0.5% on those investments

If the platform's distribution network finds new investors for you:

  • You pay 6% on those investments
  • This compensates the brokers and platform for sourcing capital

The channel (Solana direct, broker OTC, Clearstream) doesn't matter — only whether you already knew the investor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I control the Series LLC that holds my issuance?

No. Sails.to maintains majority control to ensure fiduciary duty to investors is upheld. You can have minority representation on the Council, but the mandate is to protect security holders, not issuer interests.

What if I want to raise less than $600,000?

The minimum raise is $600,000 to ensure the economics work for all parties and sufficient investor protection structures can be put in place. For smaller amounts, consider traditional financing options.

Can I communicate with my investors directly?

All official communications must go through the platform to ensure equal access and maintain audit trails. You can have relationships outside the platform, but investment-related communications must be on-platform.

What happens if I can't make a scheduled payment?

Contact the platform immediately. The Series Council will work with you on options — which might include payment deferrals, restructuring, or in severe cases, enforcement of security interests. Transparency and early communication are key.

How long does the process take?

With prepared documentation: as fast as 1-2 weeks from application to tokens minted. More complex situations or missing documents can extend this. Distribution period is typically 4 months (configurable).
