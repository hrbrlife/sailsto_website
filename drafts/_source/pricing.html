Common Pricing Questions

When is the 0.5% direct placement fee charged? +

The 0.5% fee is only charged after the soft cap is reached and the offering proceeds. If an offering fails to reach soft cap, investors are refunded and no platform fee applies—protecting investors from paying fees on failed raises.

Why is the broker fee 6% when direct is 0.5%? +

The 6% is industry-standard for private placement distribution. The entire amount goes to the broker who sources the investor—we don't take a cut. This incentivizes our broker network to actively distribute your offering to qualified investors globally.

What does the 1%/year trust fee cover? +

The annual trust & admin fee covers: ongoing cap table management, compliance monitoring, distribution processing, investor reporting, regulatory filings, and platform infrastructure maintenance.

Are there any hidden fees? +

No. The fees listed on this page are comprehensive. There are no setup fees, no monthly minimums, no per-investor charges, and no surprise costs. Third-party fees (like Clearstream custody for TradFi Bridge users) are pass-through costs—we don't markup external services.

Can fees be negotiated? +

For large offerings ($25M+), we're open to discussing custom arrangements. Contact our team to discuss your specific situation.
