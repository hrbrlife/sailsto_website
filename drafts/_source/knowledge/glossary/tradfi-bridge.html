TradFi Bridge

Sails.to's mechanism for converting blockchain-native security tokens to traditional ISIN securities that can be held in conventional bank custody.

Full Definition

The TradFi (Traditional Finance) Bridge is a unique feature of Sails.to that allows investors to move between blockchain and traditional custody. Tokens held on Solana can be "bridged" to receive an ISIN and be settled through Clearstream, appearing in the investor's regular brokerage account. Conversely, ISIN-held securities can be converted back to blockchain tokens. This hybrid approach gives investors flexibility and enables institutional adoption.

Why It Matters

The TradFi Bridge is what makes Sails.to different from every other security token platform. Most platforms force you to choose: blockchain OR traditional finance. We let you have both.

Start with Solana tokens for cheap transfers and 24/7 trading. When you need institutional credibility or want to use your existing broker, bridge to ISIN format and settle through Clearstream. Same security, same ownership rights, different rails. The conversion takes about a week and costs around $4K—a small price for TradFi legitimacy.
