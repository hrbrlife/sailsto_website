Our Mission

Traditional capital markets are gatekept by high costs, slow processes, and geographic limitations. Meanwhile, blockchain technology offers unprecedented efficiency but operates in regulatory gray zones.

We built Sails.to to solve this paradox — a hybrid infrastructure that delivers the best of both worlds: blockchain's speed and accessibility with traditional finance's legal clarity and institutional trust.

Our goal is simple: make it possible for any legitimate business to raise capital from global investors, compliantly, in weeks instead of months, at a fraction of traditional costs.

The Hybrid Approach

We don't believe in forcing investors to choose between innovation and familiarity. Our TradFi Bridge technology lets investors hold security tokens either natively on Solana or converted to an ISIN in traditional custody via Clearstream.

For issuers, we provide turnkey legal structure through Wyoming DAO LLCs with Series LLC architecture — giving you the liability protection of traditional corporate structures with the programmable governance of blockchain.

Built-in KYC/AML compliance, Reg D/Reg S support, and a network of licensed brokers ensure your offering meets regulatory requirements from day one.
