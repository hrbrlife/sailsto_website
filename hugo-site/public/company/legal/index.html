            <h2>Risk Disclosure</h2>
            <span class="legal-date">Last Updated: January 2026</span>
        </div>
        <div class="legal-content">
            <div class="risk-warning">
                <span class="warning-icon">⚠️</span>
                <p><strong>Important:</strong> Digital securities involve significant risks. You should not invest more than you can afford to lose. Past performance is not indicative of future results.</p>
            </div>
            <h3>1. General Investment Risks</h3>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Loss of Capital:</strong> You may lose some or all of your invested capital</li>
                <li><strong>Illiquidity:</strong> Digital securities may have limited secondary markets; you may not be able to sell when desired</li>
                <li><strong>Valuation:</strong> Accurate valuation of private securities is difficult; prices may not reflect true value</li>
                <li><strong>Dilution:</strong> Future funding rounds may dilute your ownership percentage</li>
            </ul>
            <h3>2. Technology Risks</h3>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Blockchain Risks:</strong> Smart contracts may contain bugs; blockchain networks may experience congestion or forks</li>
                <li><strong>Custody Risks:</strong> Loss of private keys results in permanent loss of tokens; custodial services have counterparty risk</li>
                <li><strong>Cybersecurity:</strong> Hacking, phishing, and other cyber threats may result in loss of assets</li>
                <li><strong>Interoperability:</strong> The TradFi Bridge system, while designed for reliability, introduces additional technical dependencies</li>
            </ul>
            <h3>3. Regulatory Risks</h3>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Legal Uncertainty:</strong> Laws governing digital securities are evolving and may change</li>
                <li><strong>Jurisdictional Restrictions:</strong> Your ability to hold or trade securities may be affected by local regulations</li>
                <li><strong>Tax Implications:</strong> Tax treatment of digital securities varies by jurisdiction and may change</li>
                <li><strong>Enforcement:</strong> Cross-border enforcement of rights may be challenging</li>
            </ul>
            <h3>4. Issuer Risks</h3>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Business Failure:</strong> Issuers may fail, resulting in total loss of investment</li>
                <li><strong>Fraud:</strong> Despite KYC/AML procedures, fraud risk cannot be eliminated</li>
                <li><strong>Information Asymmetry:</strong> Issuers possess more information than investors</li>
                <li><strong>Governance:</strong> DAO governance structures are novel and untested in many legal contexts</li>
            </ul>
            <h3>5. No Guarantees</h3>
            <p>Sails.to does not guarantee:</p>
            <ul>
                <li>The performance of any investment</li>
                <li>The accuracy of information provided by issuers</li>
                <li>The availability of secondary markets</li>
                <li>The continued operation of the platform</li>
            </ul>
            <h3>6. Seek Professional Advice</h3>
            <p>We strongly recommend consulting with qualified financial, legal, and tax advisors before making any investment decisions. The information on this platform is not a substitute for professional advice tailored to your specific circumstances.</p>
