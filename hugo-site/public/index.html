The full solution

Turnkey Legal Structure

Issuers: Wyoming DAO Series LLC, Reg S/Reg D compliance, KYC platform, document vault—all provided. Launch in weeks, not months.
Investors: Clear legal framework, verified issuer identity, comprehensive disclosures. Know exactly what you're investing in.

Independent Oversight

Issuers: Appoint a trustee from the network or bring your own. Independent verification builds investor confidence.
Investors: Licensed trustee oversight on every offering. Your interests are independently protected.

Multi-Broker OTC Network

Issuers: Access a network of licensed brokers for distribution. Reach investors globally without building relationships from scratch.
Investors: Trade through your broker of choice. Cross-broker liquidity means better execution and real secondary markets.

CrossSecurities Flexibility

Issuers: One offering reaches both on-chain and traditional investors. Unified cap table regardless of form.
Investors: Hold on-chain or cross to bankable ISIN format. CrossConvert between forms as your requirements change. Same security, your choice.

CrossConversion

Cross between on-chain and bankable

CrossSecurities can be held on-chain (Solana) or in bankable form (ISIN via Clearstream) — and crossed between forms at will. Any holder can request CrossConversion for ~0.75% of nominal value. Cross to bankable when your bank requires ISIN custody. Cross back to on-chain when you want 24/7 liquidity. Optional Vienna MTF listing for exchange visibility.

For Issuers: One offering reaches both crypto-native and traditional investors. Unified cap table regardless of which form investors hold.

For Investors: Hold in whichever form your compliance requires. Cross between forms as circumstances change. Same security, same rights — different custody rails.

Protected by Design

Your offering.
Fully protected.

Every issuance is legally separate — if another issuer has problems, yours is completely unaffected. Your investor data stays under your control, never shared with the platform or other participants. Independent trustee oversight on every offering. Full audit trail throughout.

Your KYC and investor data stays on your infrastructure. Verification credentials travel with investors across the network — the underlying data doesn't.

Legal Isolation

Each offering operates in its own legal compartment via Series LLC structure. Other issuers' problems never touch your raise or your investors.

Independent Oversight

Licensed trustee monitors every offering. Reserves handled neutrally. Full on-chain audit trail for complete transparency.
