Why Wyoming? The State Leading Crypto Innovation

By Sails.to Team • January 15, 2026 • 8 min read

When we decided to build Sails.to, one of our first decisions was where to incorporate. We chose Wyoming—and it wasn't just because of the scenery. Wyoming has quietly become the most crypto-friendly jurisdiction in the United States, passing over 30 blockchain-enabling laws since 2018.

The Wyoming Advantage

Wyoming offers something no other state does: legal recognition of DAOs as limited liability companies. This isn't a workaround or a gray area—it's explicit statute, signed into law in 2021.

This matters because it solves the fundamental legal problem of crypto: who can sign contracts? Who can sue or be sued? Who owns what? A Wyoming DAO LLC answers all these questions clearly, giving security token investors the same legal protections they'd have with any traditional company.

Key Wyoming Laws

  • SF0038 (2019): Defined digital assets as property, clarifying ownership rights
  • HB0070 (2019): Created the special purpose depository institution (SPDI) charter for crypto banks
  • SF0038 (2021): Legally recognized DAOs as a distinct business entity type
  • HB0043 (2022): Enabled decentralized unincorporated nonprofit associations (DUNAs)

What This Means for Sails.to

As a Wyoming DAO LLC, Sails.to operates with the same legal standing as any traditional corporation. We can:

  • Open bank accounts and hold assets
  • Sign contracts with service providers
  • Pursue legal claims or defend against them
  • Provide clear ownership structure for investors

Our Series LLC structure adds another layer: each token offering operates as its own isolated series, with separate assets and liabilities. If one offering fails, it doesn't affect the others. Your investment is legally ring-fenced.

The Series LLC: Efficiency Meets Protection

Traditional fund structures require a new legal entity for each offering—each with its own filing fees, registered agent, and administrative overhead. That's why fund administration typically starts at $50K/year.

Wyoming's Series LLC changes the math. We create one master LLC, then spin up new series for each offering. Same legal protection, fraction of the cost. This is how we can offer institutional-grade structure without institutional-grade prices.

"Wyoming has done more for blockchain innovation than any other jurisdiction in the world. It's not about deregulation—it's about clarity." — Caitlin Long, Founder of Custodia Bank

Beyond Wyoming: CrossConversion

Wyoming gives us legal foundation. Our CrossConversion gives us global reach. By converting tokens to ISIN format and settling through Clearstream, we make Wyoming-based tokens accessible to any investor with a brokerage account.

This hybrid approach—blockchain innovation backed by bankable finance infrastructure—is what makes Sails.to unique. We're not asking investors to choose between on-chain and bankable. We're giving them both.

The Future of Crypto Regulation

Other states are watching Wyoming closely. Texas, Colorado, and Arizona have all passed blockchain-friendly legislation. At the federal level, the conversation is shifting from "should we regulate crypto?" to "how should we regulate crypto?"

By building on Wyoming's legal framework now, Sails.to is positioned for whatever comes next. We're not operating in a regulatory gray area—we're building on explicit, tested law that's already survived multiple legislative sessions and legal challenges.
