<section class="faq-section" id="general">
    <div class="faq-container">
        <div class="faq-header">
            <span class="faq-icon">💡</span>
            <h2>General Questions</h2>
        </div>
        <div class="faq-list">
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>What is Sails.to?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Sails.to is a hybrid securities infrastructure platform that bridges blockchain technology with traditional finance. We enable businesses to issue compliant <span class="glossary-term" data-term="security-token">security tokens</span> that can be held either natively on the <span class="glossary-term" data-term="solana">Solana</span> blockchain or converted to traditional securities with an <span class="glossary-term" data-term="isin">ISIN</span> for custody at institutions like <span class="glossary-term" data-term="clearstream">Clearstream</span>.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>How is this different from other tokenization platforms?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Most tokenization platforms force you to choose: either blockchain-native tokens (inaccessible to traditional investors) or traditional securities (missing blockchain benefits). Our <span class="glossary-term" data-term="tradfi-bridge">TradFi Bridge</span> technology lets investors choose their preferred format — and switch between them. You get blockchain speed and programmability with traditional finance legitimacy and accessibility.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>Is Sails.to a regulated entity?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Sails.to DAO LLC is a Wyoming Decentralized Autonomous Organization LLC. We provide technology infrastructure and administrative services. Securities offerings are conducted through licensed broker-dealers in applicable jurisdictions, ensuring full regulatory compliance. We are not ourselves a broker-dealer or investment adviser.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>What types of assets can be tokenized?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>We support a wide range of asset classes including:</p>
                    <ul>
                        <li>Private company equity</li>
                        <li>Real estate (commercial, residential, REITs)</li>
                        <li>Revenue-sharing agreements</li>
                        <li>Debt instruments and bonds</li>
                        <li>Fund interests (PE, VC, hedge funds)</li>
                        <li>Royalties and licensing rights</li>
                    </ul>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>What countries do you support?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Our platform is available to issuers and investors worldwide, with some restrictions. We support offerings under U.S. <span class="glossary-term" data-term="reg-d">Reg D</span> (accredited investors) and <span class="glossary-term" data-term="reg-s">Reg S</span> (non-U.S. investors). We cannot serve residents of OFAC-sanctioned countries or jurisdictions where digital securities are explicitly prohibited.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>
</section>
<section class="faq-section alt" id="issuers">
    <div class="faq-container">
        <div class="faq-header">
            <span class="faq-icon">🏢</span>
            <h2>For Issuers</h2>
        </div>
        <div class="faq-list">
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>How much does it cost to launch a token?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p><strong>Zero upfront cost.</strong> We operate on a success-based fee model:</p>
                    <ul>
                        <li><strong>Soft cap phase:</strong> 0% fees (brokerage waived, distribution deferred)</li>
                        <li><strong>After soft cap reached:</strong></li>
                        <ul>
                            <li><strong>Direct/referral investors:</strong> 1% distribution fee</li>
                            <li><strong>Broker-introduced investors:</strong> 6% distribution fee (includes broker commission)</li>
                        </ul>
                        <li><strong>Secondary trades:</strong> 0.5% (split ⅓ platform, ⅓ buy-side broker, ⅓ sell-side broker)</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p>If soft cap fails, investors get a <strong>full refund</strong> (only Clearstream fees at cost, if used). This includes legal structure setup (<span class="glossary-term" data-term="wyoming-dao-llc">Wyoming DAO LLC</span>), token deployment, <span class="glossary-term" data-term="kyc">KYC</span>/<span class="glossary-term" data-term="aml">AML</span> integration, and investor portal.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>How long does it take to launch?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p><strong>1-2 weeks</strong> from initial onboarding to token deployment. This includes:</p>
                    <ul>
                        <li>Issuer KYC verification (1-2 days)</li>
                        <li>Legal structure setup (3-5 days)</li>
                        <li>Token configuration and smart contract deployment (1-2 days)</li>
                        <li>Investor portal setup (1-2 days)</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p>Complex offerings with custom terms may take longer.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>What legal structure do you provide?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>We create a <span class="glossary-term" data-term="wyoming-dao-llc">Wyoming DAO LLC</span> with <span class="glossary-term" data-term="series-llc">Series LLC</span> architecture. Each offering exists as an isolated series with its own assets, liabilities, and investors — providing liability protection while maintaining operational efficiency. The DAO structure enables blockchain-native governance while being fully recognized under U.S. law.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>Do I need my own lawyer?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Our standard offering structure includes pre-vetted legal documentation for common scenarios. For offerings with unique terms, complex cap tables, or specific regulatory requirements, we recommend engaging your own securities counsel. We can provide referrals to experienced attorneys familiar with our platform.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>How do dividend/distribution payments work?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p><span class="glossary-term" data-term="distributions">Distributions</span> can be paid in stablecoins (USDC) directly to investor wallets, or via traditional bank transfer for investors holding securities in ISIN format. Our <span class="glossary-term" data-term="cap-table">cap table</span> management automatically tracks ownership and calculates pro-rata distributions.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>
</section>
<section class="faq-section" id="investors">
    <div class="faq-container">
        <div class="faq-header">
            <span class="faq-icon">💼</span>
            <h2>For Investors</h2>
        </div>
        <div class="faq-list">
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>Who can invest on Sails.to?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Offerings are available to <span class="glossary-term" data-term="accredited-investor">accredited investors</span> (U.S.) and <span class="glossary-term" data-term="professional-investor">professional investors</span> (international) only. Qualification requirements include:</p>
                    <ul>
                        <li>Net worth over $1M (excluding primary residence), OR</li>
                        <li>Annual income over $200K ($300K with spouse), OR</li>
                        <li>Professional certifications (Series 7, 65, 82), OR</li>
                        <li>Institutional status (banks, pension funds, etc.)</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p>All investors must complete <span class="glossary-term" data-term="kyc">KYC</span>/<span class="glossary-term" data-term="aml">AML</span> verification.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>How do I custody my tokens?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>You have two options:</p>
                    <ol>
                        <li><strong>Self-custody (Solana):</strong> Hold tokens in any Solana-compatible wallet (Phantom, Solflare, Ledger). You control your private keys.</li>
                        <li><strong>Traditional custody (ISIN):</strong> Convert tokens to securities held at <span class="glossary-term" data-term="clearstream">Clearstream</span>, accessible through your bank or brokerage account.</li>
                    </ol>
                    <p>You can switch between formats at any time via our <span class="glossary-term" data-term="tradfi-bridge">TradFi Bridge</span>.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>Can I sell my tokens?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p><span class="glossary-term" data-term="secondary-trading">Secondary trading</span> depends on the specific offering terms and regulatory restrictions. Many offerings have lock-up periods (typically 12 months for Reg D). After any lock-up expires, tokens can be transferred to other eligible investors through our compliant P2P marketplace or through broker-facilitated transactions.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>What are the tax implications?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Tax treatment varies by jurisdiction and individual circumstances. Security tokens are generally treated as securities for tax purposes, meaning capital gains and dividend income rules apply. We provide year-end tax statements (1099s for U.S. investors) to assist with reporting. Consult your tax advisor for specific guidance.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>What happens if Sails.to goes out of business?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Your ownership rights are recorded on the Solana blockchain and/or traditional securities registries — independent of our platform. If Sails.to ceased operations:</p>
                    <ul>
                        <li>Blockchain tokens remain in your wallet, fully under your control</li>
                        <li>ISIN-held securities remain at Clearstream, accessible through your custodian</li>
                        <li>Legal ownership is documented in the Series LLC operating agreement</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p>Our infrastructure is designed for continuity.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>

<section class="faq-section alt" id="compliance">
    <div class="faq-container">
        <div class="faq-header">
            <span class="faq-icon">⚖️</span>
            <h2>Compliance & Legal</h2>
        </div>
        <div class="faq-list">
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>How does KYC/AML work?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Our <span class="glossary-term" data-term="kyc">KYC</span>/<span class="glossary-term" data-term="aml">AML</span> process is powered by enterprise-grade compliance infrastructure:</p>
                    <ol>
                        <li>Identity verification (document + liveness check)</li>
                        <li>Sanctions screening (OFAC, EU, UN lists)</li>
                        <li>Accreditation verification (for U.S. investors)</li>
                        <li>Ongoing monitoring for suspicious activity</li>
                    </ol>
                    <p>Verification typically takes 24-48 hours. Once verified, your status is valid across all offerings on the platform.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>What regulatory exemptions do you use?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Offerings are structured under:</p>
                    <ul>
                        <li><strong><span class="glossary-term" data-term="reg-d">Reg D</span> 506(b):</strong> Up to 35 non-accredited investors (with disclosure), unlimited accredited, no general solicitation</li>
                        <li><strong>Reg D 506(c):</strong> Accredited investors only, general solicitation permitted</li>
                        <li><strong><span class="glossary-term" data-term="reg-s">Reg S</span>:</strong> Non-U.S. investors, no registration required</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p>The appropriate exemption is selected based on offering size, investor composition, and marketing strategy.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>How are transfer restrictions enforced?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Our <span class="glossary-term" data-term="smart-contract">smart contracts</span> include on-chain compliance checks:</p>
                    <ul>
                        <li>Whitelist of verified investor wallets</li>
                        <li>Lock-up period enforcement</li>
                        <li>Transfer volume limits (if applicable)</li>
                        <li>Investor category restrictions (accredited only, etc.)</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p>Tokens can only be transferred to addresses that pass all compliance checks.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>

<section class="faq-section" id="technical">
    <div class="faq-container">
        <div class="faq-header">
            <span class="faq-icon">⚙️</span>
            <h2>Technical Questions</h2>
        </div>
        <div class="faq-list">
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>Why Solana?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>We chose <span class="glossary-term" data-term="solana">Solana</span> for several reasons:</p>
                    <ul>
                        <li><strong>Speed:</strong> 400ms finality vs. 12+ minutes on Ethereum</li>
                        <li><strong>Cost:</strong> Sub-cent transaction fees vs. $5-50+ on Ethereum</li>
                        <li><strong>Scalability:</strong> 65,000+ TPS theoretical capacity</li>
                        <li><strong>Developer ecosystem:</strong> Robust tooling and growing institutional adoption</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p>For securities that may require frequent <span class="glossary-term" data-term="distributions">distributions</span> and transfers, Solana's cost-efficiency is essential.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>What wallet do I need?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Any Solana-compatible wallet works:</p>
                    <ul>
                        <li><strong>Phantom</strong> — Most popular, user-friendly</li>
                        <li><strong>Solflare</strong> — Full-featured, supports staking</li>
                        <li><strong>Ledger</strong> — Hardware wallet for maximum security</li>
                        <li><strong>Backpack</strong> — xNFT-enabled, newer option</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p>For large holdings, we strongly recommend hardware wallet custody.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>How does the TradFi Bridge work?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>The <span class="glossary-term" data-term="tradfi-bridge">TradFi Bridge</span> converts between blockchain tokens and traditional securities:</p>
                    <ol>
                        <li><strong>Token → ISIN:</strong> Burn tokens on Solana, issue equivalent securities at Clearstream with <span class="glossary-term" data-term="isin">ISIN</span> identifier</li>
                        <li><strong>ISIN → Token:</strong> Cancel Clearstream position, mint equivalent tokens to verified wallet</li>
                    </ol>
                    <p>Conversion takes 1-3 business days and maintains 1:1 correspondence. Total supply is always preserved.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="faq-item">
                <button class="faq-question">
                    <span>Is there an API?</span>
                    <span class="faq-toggle">+</span>
                </button>
                <div class="faq-answer">
                    <p>Yes! We provide REST APIs for:</p>
                    <ul>
                        <li>Investor verification status</li>
                        <li>Cap table queries</li>
                        <li>Distribution triggers</li>
                        <li>Transaction history</li>
                    </ul>
                    <p>API access requires authentication and is available to issuers and authorized integrators. See our <a href="docs/api-reference.html">API Documentation</a> for details.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
        </div>
    </div>
