Platform Overview

Sails.to is a compliant platform for issuing, investing in, and distributing security tokens. We combine blockchain efficiency with traditional finance compliance to offer the best of both worlds.

What We Offer

  • Issuers: Tokenize assets, raise capital globally, manage cap tables automatically
  • Investors: Access curated offerings, choose your custody model, receive automated distributions
  • Brokers: Expand your product offering, earn competitive commissions, serve global clients

Key Features

  • Compliant by design: Reg D 506(c) / Reg S framework with full KYC/AML
  • Hybrid custody: Self-custody on Solana or institutional via Clearstream
  • Wyoming DAO LLC: Legal entity recognition with Series LLC protection
  • Secondary trading: Compliant peer-to-peer transfers

For Issuers

Launch a tokenized securities offering in weeks, not months. Here's how to get started:

1

Create Your Issuer Account

Sign up at sails.to/signup and select "Issuer." Provide basic company information to start the process.

2

Complete Issuer Verification

Submit company documentation: formation documents, ownership structure, authorized representatives. Our team reviews within 48 hours.

3

Structure Your Offering

Work with our team to define: asset type, raise amount, token economics, investor eligibility, and geographic scope. We'll set up your Series LLC.

4

Legal Documentation

We provide templates for: Private Placement Memorandum (PPM), subscription agreements, operating agreement. Review with your counsel and customize as needed.

5

Token Deployment

We deploy compliant tokens on Solana with embedded transfer restrictions, whitelist enforcement, and distribution capabilities.

6

Launch & Raise Capital

Your offering goes live. Investors are verified through our KYC process, subscriptions are processed, and tokens are distributed.

Issuer Requirements

  • Registered legal entity (any jurisdiction)
  • Asset with clear ownership/rights
  • Minimum offering size: $500,000
  • Commitment to regulatory compliance

For Investors

Access institutional-quality tokenized securities. Here's your path to investing:

1

Create Your Investor Account

Sign up at sails.to/signup and select "Investor." Provide contact information and country of residence.

2

Complete Identity Verification

Standard KYC process: government ID, proof of address, selfie verification. Typically completed in minutes.

3

Verify Investor Status

US Investors: Provide accredited investor documentation (income/net worth verification or professional certification).
Non-US: Confirm professional/qualified investor status per your jurisdiction.

4

Choose Your Custody Model

On-Chain Form: Connect or create a Solana wallet for direct token ownership.
Bankable Form: Select institutional custody via CrossConversion to Clearstream with ISIN assignment.

5

Browse & Invest

View available offerings, review documentation, and subscribe. Minimum investment varies by offering (typically $150,000+).

6

Receive Tokens & Distributions

Tokens arrive in your chosen custody. Distributions are automated—stablecoin to wallet or cash via custodian.

Investor Requirements

  • US investors: Must be accredited (income $200K+ or net worth $1M+)
  • Non-US: Must qualify as professional/qualified investor
  • Minimum investment: $150,000 per offering
  • Valid government ID and proof of address

Verification Process

Identity Verification (All Users)

Our KYC process verifies you are who you claim to be:

  • Government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, national ID)
  • Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, less than 3 months old)
  • Liveness check (selfie matching ID photo)

Most verifications complete automatically in under 5 minutes.

Accredited Investor Verification (US)

For Reg D 506(c) offerings, we must verify accreditation:

  • Income method: Tax returns or written verification from CPA/attorney
  • Net worth method: Statements showing $1M+ (excluding primary residence)
  • Professional: Series 7, 65, or 82 license verification

Entity Verification

For companies, trusts, and funds:

  • Formation documents
  • Beneficial ownership (25%+ owners)
  • Authorized representative verification
