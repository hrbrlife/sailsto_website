Wyoming DAO LLC

A limited liability company structure recognized by Wyoming law that allows decentralized autonomous organizations to operate with legal entity status and liability protection.

Full Definition

Wyoming became the first US state to legally recognize DAOs as a distinct business entity type in 2021. A Wyoming DAO LLC combines the flexibility of smart contract governance with the legal protections of a traditional LLC, including limited liability for members. This structure is ideal for tokenized securities as it provides clear legal ownership, tax treatment, and regulatory standing while enabling on-chain governance and automated operations.

Why It Matters

The Wyoming DAO LLC is what makes Sails.to possible. By operating as a legal entity—not just code—we can sign contracts, hold assets, sue and be sued, and interact with the traditional financial system. Token holders have clear legal rights, not just cryptographic claims.

Combined with the Series LLC structure, this allows us to create isolated SPVs for each offering while maintaining a single administrative umbrella. It's the best of both worlds: blockchain efficiency with legal certainty.
