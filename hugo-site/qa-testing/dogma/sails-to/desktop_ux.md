## Core Checks

### 5-Second Test
For EACH page, answer in 5 seconds:
1. **What is this?** (A securities platform? A crypto exchange? Unclear?)
2. **What can I do?** (Issue securities? Invest? Become a broker?)
3. **Why should I care?** (What problem does this solve?)
4. **Is this legit?** (Does it feel institutional or startup-hacky?)

### Visual Hierarchy
- **F-pattern / Z-pattern**: Does the eye flow naturally?
- **Heading sizes**: Clear hierarchy from H1 → H2 → H3?
- **Whitespace**: Enough breathing room, not cramped?
- **Focal points**: Where does the eye land first? Is it the right place?
- **Above the fold**: Most important content visible without scrolling?

### Navigation
- **Primary nav**: Clear, logical grouping. Can each persona find their page?
- **Breadcrumbs**: Present on deeper pages (docs, glossary, guides)?
- **Footer nav**: Comprehensive, well-organized?
- **7±2 rule**: Not too many top-level nav items?
- **Knowledge Hub**: Easy to navigate between docs, guides, glossary, FAQ?

### CTAs (Calls to Action)
- **Visually distinct**: Buttons look like buttons, not just styled text
- **Above fold**: At least one CTA visible without scrolling on key pages
- **Consistent placement**: Same CTA style and position across pages
- **Action-specific**: "Start Your Issuance" not "Learn More"
- **Per-audience**: Different CTAs for different personas

### Content Density
- **Scannable**: Headers, bullets, short paragraphs
- **Progressive disclosure**: Summary first, detail on click/scroll
- **Reading width**: 600–800px content column for text-heavy pages
- **Data tables**: Financial data presented in clean, readable tables
- **Code/technical**: Properly formatted if docs include technical content

### Conversion Flow
Verify this flow works for each persona:
1. **Landing** → Identify audience
2. **Audience page** → Understand value prop
3. **Compare/What Sails** → Evaluate (how is this different?)
4. **Pricing** → Cost assessment
5. **Signup/Contact** → Take action (apply for private testing access)

### Visual Identity
- Sails.to aesthetic: sovereign, connected, professional
- Hero: Video/image background with clear overlay text
- Cards: Product cards, audience cards — consistent styling
- Typography: Professional, readable, consistent sizing

## Reference Expectations

sails.to should feel like:
- A **sovereign platform** — you own your infrastructure, your data, your AI
- **Fully connected** to Web3 and AI worlds — under YOUR control
- **Confident and direct** — problem-first copy, no fluff
- **Data-rich** — numbers, specs, examples, not vague promises
- **Multi-audience** — clear pathways for issuers, investors, brokers, etc.

It should NOT feel like:
- A DeFi protocol website
- A generic SaaS landing page
- A crypto marketing page with buzzwords
- An academic whitepaper
- A Melusina OS product page (sails.to has its own identity)

## Page-Specific Desktop Checks

### Landing Page (/)
- Hero video/image renders correctly
- Product cards (Bonds/Shares/RWA) clearly displayed
- Example offering section is compelling and well-laid-out
- Audience navigation is immediately visible

### Audience Pages (/brokers/, /investors/, etc.)
- Page feels dedicated to that audience, not generic
- Feature lists are scannable
- CTA is prominent and audience-specific
- Consistent layout across audience pages

### Knowledge Hub (/knowledge/)
- Sidebar or nav for docs/guides/glossary navigation
- Search functionality (if available)
- Breadcrumbs for deep content
- Clean typography for long-form reading

### Pricing (/pricing/)
- Pricing tiers clearly differentiated
- Feature comparison easy to scan
- CTA per tier
- Transparent — no "call for pricing" on basic info

## Severities

- **critical**: Page broken at 1440px, navigation unusable, hero not loading
- **high**: 5-second test fails, CTA invisible, content unreadable
- **medium**: Layout inconsistencies, suboptimal hierarchy, missing breadcrumbs
- **low**: Polish items, minor alignment, could-be-better spacing
