## Identity

**Sails.to** is a hybrid securities infrastructure platform. It lets
issuers tokenize bonds, shares, and real-world assets (RWA) as
CrossSecurities on Solana, while maintaining dual-format compatibility
with traditional finance rails (ISIN, Clearstream, Vienna MTF).

**Tagline**: Hybrid Securities Infrastructure

**Domain**: sails.to (www.sails.to)

---

## What Sails.to IS vs. IS NOT

| IS | IS NOT |

| OTC network | Exchange or DEX |

| Solana-native | Multi-chain / chain-agnostic |

## Voice & Tone

- **Problem-first framing** — every page starts with the pain, then the solution
- **Direct, confident, confrontational** — doesn't hedge or apologize
- **Specific over general** — actual numbers, actual Solana addresses, actual timelines
- **Pattern interrupts** — stats, pull quotes, visual breaks between dense sections
- **Proof points** — small badges/stats that say "this is real, not vapor"
- **No AI buzzwords** — avoid "leveraging", "seamless", "revolutionize", "cutting-edge"

## Review Philosophy

When reviewing Sails.to pages, every expert should ask:

1. **5-second test**: Can someone understand what Sails.to does within 5 seconds?
2. **Audience match**: Does this page know WHO it's talking to?
3. **OTC not exchange**: Is it clear this is an OTC network, not an exchange or DEX?
4. **Dual-format clarity**: Is the CrossConversion (on-chain ↔ traditional) explained concretely?
5. **Status accuracy**: Does it say "private testing", not "coming soon" or "live"?
6. **Number consistency**: Are the key numbers (0.75%, $150K, 3%, $600K, €3-5k) consistent?
7. **Competitor comparison**: Are the "before" descriptions vivid enough to paint the pain?
8. **Trust signals**: Is there evidence this is real — licence, regulator, Vienna MTF, Clearstream?
