## Target Personas — Know Each One

You MUST evaluate the site through the eyes of each persona.

### Persona 1: Issuers (Businesses Raising Capital)

**Who**: Founders, CFOs, financial advisors at companies wanting to raise $600K+
through tokenized securities (bonds, revenue-share, equity).

**What they want**:
- Speed: How fast from decision to first capital?
- Cost: What does it cost to issue?
- Compliance: Will this be legally defensible?
- Control: Can they manage their own cap table, comms, governance?
- Structure: DAO LLC, Series LLC, SPV options
- CrossConversion: Can investors hold both on-chain and in traditional custody?

**Journey to verify**:
1. Landing → Do they understand this is for raising capital within 5 seconds?
2. /whatsails/ or /issuers/ → Clear explanation of the issuance process?
3. /pricing/ → Can they estimate total cost? Is it transparent?
4. /signup/ → Is the next step clear and low-friction?
5. /compliance/ → Does it address their legal concerns?
6. /knowledge/docs/ → Can they go deep if they want?

## Conversion Hygiene Checks

### Navigation
- Can each persona find their dedicated page within 2 clicks?
- Does the nav make audience segmentation obvious?
- Is the Knowledge Hub accessible without burying commercial pages?

### CTA Hierarchy
- Every page should have ONE primary CTA
- CTA language should be action-specific (not generic "Learn More")
- Signup/contact CTA should be visible without scrolling
- Since all products are in private testing, CTAs should lead to
  application/contact forms, not purchase flows

### Trust Signals
- Regulatory references (Reg D, Reg S, DAO LLC jurisdictions)
- Technical credibility (Solana, Clearstream, ISIN, Vienna MTF)
- Case studies or example offerings
- Team/company transparency

### Dead-End Detection
Flag pages with no clear next action. Every page should answer "What should I do next?"

### Melusina OS References
Keep minimal. Sails.to must make sense on its own without requiring visitors
to understand Melusina OS. Cross-links to melusina-os.org should be minimal
and clearly secondary.

## Severities

- **critical**: Key persona has no path to conversion (no CTA, no audience page)
- **high**: Major persona journey broken or confusing
- **medium**: Friction that reduces conversion (unclear copy, competing CTAs)
- **low**: Optimization opportunity (better CTA text, reorder sections)
