Risk Disclosure

Last Updated: February 2026
⚠️

Important: Digital securities involve significant risks. You should not invest more than you can afford to lose. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

1. General Investment Risks

  • Loss of Capital: You may lose some or all of your invested capital
  • Illiquidity: Digital securities may have limited secondary markets; you may not be able to sell when desired
  • Valuation: Accurate valuation of private securities is difficult; prices may not reflect true value
  • Dilution: Future funding rounds may dilute your ownership percentage

2. Technology Risks

  • Blockchain Risks: Smart contracts may contain bugs; blockchain networks may experience congestion or forks
  • Custody Risks: Loss of private keys results in permanent loss of tokens; custodial services have counterparty risk
  • Cybersecurity: Hacking, phishing, and other cyber threats may result in loss of assets
  • Interoperability: The CrossConversion system, while designed for reliability, introduces additional technical dependencies

3. Regulatory Risks

  • Legal Uncertainty: Laws governing digital securities are evolving and may change
  • Jurisdictional Restrictions: Your ability to hold or trade securities may be affected by local regulations
  • Tax Implications: Tax treatment of digital securities varies by jurisdiction and may change
  • Enforcement: Cross-border enforcement of rights may be challenging

4. Issuer Risks

  • Business Failure: Issuers may fail, resulting in total loss of investment
  • Fraud: Despite KYC/AML procedures, fraud risk cannot be eliminated
  • Information Asymmetry: Issuers possess more information than investors
  • Governance: DAO governance structures are novel and untested in many legal contexts

5. No Guarantees

Sails.to does not guarantee:

  • The performance of any investment
  • The accuracy of information provided by issuers
  • The availability of secondary markets
  • The continued operation of the platform

6. Seek Professional Advice

We strongly recommend consulting with qualified financial, legal, and tax advisors before making any investment decisions. The information on this platform is not a substitute for professional advice tailored to your specific circumstances.
