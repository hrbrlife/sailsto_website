            <h2>Who's Moving and Why</h2>
            <h3>BlackRock: The World's Largest Asset Manager</h3>
            <p>When Larry Fink calls tokenization "the next generation for markets," the industry listens. BlackRock's BUIDL fund—a tokenized money market fund—crossed $500 million within months of launch. Their thesis: tokenization enables 24/7 settlement, broader access, and reduced operational costs.</p>
            <h3>JPMorgan: Banking Giant Goes Blockchain</h3>
            <p>JPMorgan's Onyx platform has processed over $1 trillion in tokenized transactions. Their focus: repo markets, collateral management, and cross-border payments. They're proving tokenization works at scale.</p>
            <h3>Franklin Templeton: $1.4T Asset Manager</h3>
            <p>Franklin Templeton launched the first US-registered mutual fund using blockchain for record-keeping. Their tokenized money market fund operates on multiple chains, demonstrating interoperability isn't just theoretical.</p>
            <h3>Goldman Sachs: Digital Asset Platform</h3>
            <p>Goldman's GS DAP enables institutional clients to issue, trade, and settle digital assets. They've executed multiple tokenized bond issuances for major corporations, proving the capital markets infrastructure is ready.</p>
            <h2>Why Now?</h2>
            <h3>1. Technology Has Matured</h3>
            <p>Early blockchain platforms couldn't handle institutional volume. Today's infrastructure—including <span class="glossary-term" data-term="solana">Solana</span>'s 65,000+ TPS and established custodians like <span class="glossary-term" data-term="clearstream">Clearstream</span>—can process real transaction volume.</p>
            <h3>2. Regulatory Clarity Emerging</h3>
            <p>Confusion killed early momentum. Now, frameworks like <span class="glossary-term" data-term="reg-d">Reg D</span>/<span class="glossary-term" data-term="reg-s">Reg S</span> exemptions provide clear paths for compliant offerings. Institutions can deploy with legal certainty.</p>
            <h3>3. Client Demand</h3>
            <p>Institutional clients are asking for digital asset access. Family offices, endowments, and pension funds want portfolio exposure to tokenized alternatives. The sell-side follows the buy-side.</p>
            <h3>4. Competitive Pressure</h3>
            <p>When your competitors launch tokenization platforms, waiting becomes riskier than moving. First-mover advantages in infrastructure and client relationships are substantial.</p>
            <div class="quote-block">
                "We believe the next generation of market infrastructure will be blockchain-based, and that security tokens will be a key component of capital markets."
                <cite>— Major investment bank internal strategy memo</cite>

            <h2>What's Being Tokenized</h2>
            <h3>Fixed Income</h3>
            <p>Bonds are the perfect first use case: predictable cash flows, clear ownership, established legal frameworks. Tokenized bonds already trade with institutional settlement rails.</p>
            <h3>Real Assets</h3>
            <p>Real estate, infrastructure, and commodities benefit most from <span class="glossary-term" data-term="tokenization">tokenization</span>. Fractional ownership unlocks liquidity in historically illiquid markets worth trillions.</p>
            <h3>Private Equity & Venture</h3>
            <p>Private fund interests are notoriously illiquid. Tokenization enables <span class="glossary-term" data-term="secondary-trading">secondary trading</span> while maintaining compliance through <span class="glossary-term" data-term="smart-contract">smart contract</span> transfer restrictions.</p>
            <h3>Money Market Funds</h3>
            <p>The fastest-growing category. Tokenized MMFs provide instant settlement, 24/7 access, and programmable cash management. BlackRock's BUIDL proved the model.</p>

            <h2>What This Means for Smaller Issuers</h2>
            <p>Institutional adoption isn't just for the big players. It validates the technology and creates network effects:</p>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Infrastructure:</strong> Custody, compliance, and trading systems developed for institutions are available to all</li>
                <li><strong>Investor comfort:</strong> When BlackRock tokenizes, institutional LPs become comfortable with the format</li>
                <li><strong>Regulatory clarity:</strong> Institutional lobbying helps shape workable regulatory frameworks</li>
