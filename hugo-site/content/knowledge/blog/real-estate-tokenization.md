            <p>Commercial real estate is a $20+ trillion market with one persistent problem: illiquidity. Properties take months to sell, ownership requires significant capital, and global investors face friction entering local markets. <span class="glossary-term" data-term="tokenization">Tokenization</span> changes all of that.</p>
            <h2>The Illiquidity Problem</h2>
            <p>Traditional commercial real estate investing works like this:</p>
            <ul>
                <li>Minimum investments of $250K–$1M+ for direct ownership</li>
                <li>Complex legal structures requiring attorney involvement</li>
                <li>Sale processes taking 6–12 months</li>
                <li>Limited access to international capital</li>
                <li>Paper-based <span class="glossary-term" data-term="cap-table">cap tables</span> and manual distributions</li>
            </ul>
            <p>This illiquidity creates an "illiquidity discount"—investors pay less because they can't easily exit. Estimates suggest 20–30% value erosion compared to liquid alternatives.</p>
            <h2>How Tokenization Solves It</h2>
            <h3>Fractional Ownership</h3>
            <p>A $50 million office building can be divided into 500,000 tokens at $100 each. Suddenly, the same institutional-quality asset is accessible to investors at any scale.</p>
            <p>But this isn't just about smaller investments. Fractional ownership enables:</p>
            <ul>
                <li>Better portfolio diversification (own pieces of many properties)</li>
                <li>Easier investor syndication</li>
                <li>Simplified estate planning and transfers</li>
            </ul>
            <h3>Secondary Liquidity</h3>
            <p>The real game-changer is <span class="glossary-term" data-term="secondary-trading">secondary trading</span>. Token holders can sell positions to other verified investors without selling the underlying property. Exit when you need to—don't wait for a property sale.</p>
            <p>On Sails.to, secondary trading is:</p>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Compliance-enforced:</strong> <span class="glossary-term" data-term="smart-contract">Smart contracts</span> verify buyer eligibility before transfers</li>
                <li><strong>Transparent:</strong> All trades recorded on blockchain</li>
                <li><strong>Efficient:</strong> Settlement in minutes, not months</li>
            </ul>
            <h3>Global Capital Access</h3>
            <p>A Dubai developer can raise capital from Singapore, Switzerland, and São Paulo simultaneously. <span class="glossary-term" data-term="reg-s">Reg S</span> enables international investors; <span class="glossary-term" data-term="reg-d">Reg D</span> brings US <span class="glossary-term" data-term="accredited-investor">accredited investors</span>.</p>
            <p>All investors go through the same <span class="glossary-term" data-term="kyc">KYC</span>/<span class="glossary-term" data-term="aml">AML</span> verification. Compliance doesn't change—access expands.</p>

            <h2>Legal Structure That Works</h2>
            <p>Real estate tokenization requires proper legal structuring. The asset doesn't go "on the blockchain"—ownership rights do.</p>
            <p>Sails.to uses <span class="glossary-term" data-term="wyoming-dao-llc">Wyoming DAO LLC</span> with <span class="glossary-term" data-term="series-llc">Series LLC</span> structure:</p>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Series isolation:</strong> Each property is a separate series, liability-protected from others</li>
                <li><strong>Token = membership interest:</strong> Tokens represent LLC membership interests, not direct property ownership</li>
                <li><strong>DAO governance:</strong> Token holders vote on major decisions per operating agreement</li>
                <li><strong>Pass-through taxation:</strong> LLC structure avoids double taxation</li>
            </ul>

            <h2>What's Tokenizable?</h2>
            <h3>Ideal Candidates</h3>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Stabilized income properties:</strong> Office, retail, multifamily with reliable cash flow</li>
                <li><strong>Development projects:</strong> Clear milestones, defined hold period</li>
                <li><strong>Debt instruments:</strong> Mortgages and mezzanine debt</li>
                <li><strong>Portfolio diversification vehicles:</strong> Funds holding multiple properties</li>
            </ul>
            <h3>Considerations</h3>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Clean title:</strong> No title issues or complex liens</li>
                <li><strong>Clear economics:</strong> Simple capital stack, defined distributions</li>
                <li><strong>Minimum size:</strong> Generally $5M+ to justify tokenization costs</li>
                <li><strong>Sponsor track record:</strong> Investor confidence matters</li>
            </ul>
            <h2>Custody Options</h2>
            <p>Real estate investors span from crypto-native to traditional institutional. Sails.to accommodates both:</p>
            <h3>Self-Custody (Solana)</h3>
            <p>Tokens held directly in investor's <span class="glossary-term" data-term="solana">Solana</span> wallet. Full control, no intermediary. Ideal for DeFi-comfortable investors.</p>
            <h3>TradFi Bridge (Clearstream)</h3>
            <p>For institutional investors requiring traditional <span class="glossary-term" data-term="custody">custody</span>, the <span class="glossary-term" data-term="tradfi-bridge">TradFi Bridge</span> converts tokens to <span class="glossary-term" data-term="isin">ISIN</span>-bearing instruments held at <span class="glossary-term" data-term="clearstream">Clearstream</span>.</p>
            <p>Same underlying security—different wrappers for different investor preferences.</p>
            <h2>The Distribution Advantage</h2>
            <p>Real estate generates regular income—rents, refinancing proceeds, sale proceeds. Traditional distribution is manual:</p>
            <ol>
                <li>Calculate each investor's share</li>
                <li>Process wire transfers individually</li>
                <li>Update records</li>
                <li>Handle failed payments</li>
                <li>Issue tax documents</li>
            </ol>
            <p>With <span class="glossary-term" data-term="smart-contract">smart contracts</span>, distributions are:</p>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Automatic:</strong> Push distributions to all token holders simultaneously</li>
                <li><strong>Accurate:</strong> Cap table IS the blockchain—no reconciliation</li>
                <li><strong>Instant:</strong> Stablecoin payments settle in seconds</li>
                <li><strong>Auditable:</strong> Complete distribution history on-chain</li>
            </ul>

                <li><strong>Ongoing operations:</strong> Distributions, reporting, secondary trading</li>
            </ol>
