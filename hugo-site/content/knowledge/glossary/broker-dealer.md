        <section class="term-section">
            <h2>Full Definition</h2>
            <p>A broker-dealer is a financial firm or individual licensed to buy and sell securities on behalf of clients (broker function) and/or for their own account (dealer function). In most jurisdictions, broker-dealers must be registered with relevant regulators (FINRA/SEC in the US, FCA in the UK, etc.).</p>
            <p>On Sails.to, licensed broker-dealers participate in our network to:</p>
            <ul>
                <li>Place their clients in primary offerings (earning up to 5% commission)</li>
                <li>Facilitate secondary trading on our OTC network (0.5% split)</li>
                <li>Provide market-making and liquidity services</li>
            </ul>
        </section>
        <section class="term-section">
            <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
            <p>Broker-dealers provide crucial infrastructure for securities markets. They ensure proper investor qualification, provide investment advice, and create liquidity through market-making. Our multi-broker network ensures competitive pricing and broad distribution for issuers.</p>
            <p>For issuers, the broker network means access to qualified investors without building distribution capabilities yourself.</p>
