<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Full Definition</h2>
    <p>A securities <strong>offering</strong> on Sails.to is a structured fundraise governed by an Offering Grain. Each offering exists as an isolated Series within a <a href="/knowledge/glossary/wyoming-dao-llc/">Wyoming DAO LLC</a>, with its own token supply, compliance configuration, investor whitelist, and distribution waterfall. Offerings can be held on-chain (<a href="/knowledge/glossary/solana/">Solana</a>) or crossed to bankable format (ISIN via Clearstream) through <a href="/knowledge/glossary/crosssecurities/">CrossSecurities</a>.</p>
    <p>Each Offering Grain encapsulates the full lifecycle of a capital raise: creation, investor onboarding, subscription, <a href="/knowledge/glossary/cap-table/">cap table</a> management, and ongoing <a href="/knowledge/glossary/distributions/">distributions</a>. The grain model ensures that every offering operates in its own security boundary with its own state, capabilities, and audit trail.</p>
</section>
<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
    <p>The offering is the fundamental unit of the platform. It's where securities are created, investors participate, and distributions flow. Because each offering is an isolated grain, there is no cross-contamination between fundraises — an issue in one offering cannot affect another.</p>
    <p>For issuers, this means a turnkey capital raise with built-in compliance, tokenization, and investor management. For investors, it means a clear, auditable record of their participation from subscription through distribution. The <a href="/knowledge/glossary/series-llc/">Series LLC</a> structure provides legal isolation that mirrors the technical isolation of the grain architecture.</p>
</section>
