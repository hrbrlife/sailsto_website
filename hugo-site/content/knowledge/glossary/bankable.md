<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Full Definition</h2>
    <p>"Bankable" refers to securities that can be:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Custodied by traditional banks</strong> - Held in standard securities accounts</li>
        <li><strong>Reported on standard statements</strong> - Appears in wealth management reports</li>
        <li><strong>Used as collateral</strong> - Accepted by banks for margin lending</li>
        <li><strong>Settled through traditional rails</strong> - SWIFT, Clearstream, Euroclear</li>
    </ul>
    <p>CrossSecurities achieve bankable status through CrossConversion, which converts on-chain tokens to ISIN-identified securities held via Clearstream.</p>
</section>
<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
    <p>Many institutional investors, family offices, and regulated entities require bankable custody. Their compliance policies, reporting systems, and collateral frameworks expect traditional securities infrastructure.</p>
    <p>CrossConversion provides an escape valve - investors can start on-chain and convert to bankable when needed, or vice versa. Same security, both custody options.</p>
</section>
