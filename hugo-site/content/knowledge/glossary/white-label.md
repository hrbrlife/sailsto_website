<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Full Definition</h2>
    <p><strong>White-Label</strong> refers to Sails.to's fully branded platform instances deployed for regulated institutions, trust companies, and <a href="/knowledge/glossary/broker-dealer/">broker-dealers</a>. A white-label deployment gives the institution its own branded investment portal — their logo, their colors, their domain — running on Sails.to's infrastructure with the full <a href="/knowledge/glossary/melusina/">Melusina</a> on-chain layer underneath. The institution's clients never see Sails.to. They see their trusted financial institution, powered by infrastructure that is lightyears beyond what that institution could build alone.</p>
    <p>White-label deployments include custom CSS theming, subdomain routing, issuer and broker approval controls, self-hosted <a href="/knowledge/glossary/kyc/">KYC</a>/<a href="/knowledge/glossary/aml/">AML</a> integration, and per-instance <a href="/knowledge/glossary/compliance/">compliance</a> configuration. Licensing is annual plus transaction fees.</p>
</section>
<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
    <p>Regulated institutions don't adopt platforms — they adopt infrastructure. A <a href="/knowledge/glossary/broker-dealer/">broker-dealer</a> with a 20-year client relationship is not going to tell those clients to "go use this crypto platform." But they will offer those clients a branded portal that happens to be powered by tokenization, <a href="/knowledge/glossary/crossconversion/">CrossConversion</a>, and on-chain <a href="/knowledge/glossary/compliance/">compliance</a>.</p>
    <p>White-label is how Sails.to scales without acquiring retail users directly. Every <a href="/knowledge/glossary/trustee/">Trustee</a>, every trust company, every regulated broker becomes a distribution channel. They bring their clients. Sails.to provides the technology. The institution maintains the relationship — and earns <a href="/knowledge/glossary/commission/">commissions</a> and <a href="/knowledge/glossary/brokerage-fee/">brokerage fees</a> on every transaction their clients execute.</p>
</section>
<section class="term-section">
    <h2>How It Works</h2>
    <ol>
        <li>The institution signs an annual licensing agreement and receives a Reseller NFT in the <a href="/knowledge/glossary/nft-hierarchy/">NFT Hierarchy</a></li>
        <li>Custom branding (CSS theming, logo, color palette) is configured for the institution's subdomain</li>
        <li>Issuer and broker approval workflows are configured to match the institution's internal policies</li>
        <li><a href="/knowledge/glossary/kyc/">KYC</a>/<a href="/knowledge/glossary/aml/">AML</a> providers are integrated — either Sails.to's default or the institution's own provider</li>
        <li>Compliance parameters (jurisdictions, investor types, minimum investments) are set per-instance</li>
        <li>The institution's clients access the portal at the branded subdomain, with full <a href="/knowledge/glossary/crosssecurities/">CrossSecurities</a> functionality</li>
    </ol>
    <p>Transaction fees are shared between the institution and Sails.to. The institution controls their client relationships entirely.</p>
</section>
