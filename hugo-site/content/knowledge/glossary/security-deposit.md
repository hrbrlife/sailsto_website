<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Full Definition</h2>
    <p>A <strong>security deposit</strong> in the context of securities issuance is an upfront payment made by issuers to begin the onboarding process with a platform or service provider. This deposit covers preliminary costs including legal review, compliance assessment, entity structuring, and initial documentation.</p>
    <p>Unlike fees tied to successful capital raises, security deposits are typically required before work begins, ensuring the platform can commit resources to preparing the offering.</p>
</section>
<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
    <p>On Sails.to, the security deposit demonstrates issuer commitment and funds the initial structuring work required to prepare a compliant offering. This includes setting up the <a href="/knowledge/glossary/series-llc/">Series LLC</a> or <a href="/knowledge/glossary/spv/">SPV</a> structure, drafting offering documents, and configuring <a href="/knowledge/glossary/smart-contract/">smart contracts</a>.</p>
    <p>The deposit is disclosed transparently in Sails.to's <a href="/pricing/">pricing schedule</a> so issuers can budget appropriately. Some platforms offset the deposit against success fees upon successful completion of the raise.</p>
</section>
