        <section class="term-section">
            <h2>Full Definition</h2>
            <p>"On-chain" means data or transactions are recorded directly on a blockchain's public ledger. For CrossSecurities, this includes:</p>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Ownership records</strong> — Who holds which securities, updated in real-time</li>
                <li><strong>Transfer history</strong> — Complete audit trail of all transactions</li>
                <li><strong>Compliance status</strong> — Investor eligibility verified before any transfer</li>
                <li><strong>Cap table</strong> — Current ownership distribution viewable by authorized parties</li>
            </ul>
            <p>CrossSecurities settle on Solana, benefiting from 400ms finality and sub-cent transaction costs.</p>
        </section>
        <section class="term-section">
            <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
            <p>On-chain records provide transparency, immutability, and automation impossible with traditional systems. There's no reconciliation needed between different parties' books — the blockchain is the single source of truth.</p>
            <p>For investors who prefer traditional custody, CrossSecurities can be CrossConverted to bankable ISIN form held via Clearstream while maintaining the same underlying security.</p>
