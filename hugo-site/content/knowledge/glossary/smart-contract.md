            <section class="term-section">
                <h2>Full Definition</h2>
                <p>Smart contracts are programs stored on a blockchain that run when triggered by transactions meeting specific conditions. For security tokens, smart contracts encode compliance rules (investor eligibility, transfer restrictions, holding periods), automate corporate actions (dividends, voting), and maintain the cap table. Once deployed, the rules execute automatically without intermediaries, providing transparency and reducing operational costs.</p>
            </section>
            <section class="term-section">
                <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
                <p>Smart contracts are what make <a href="security-token.html">security tokens</a> more than just "stocks on blockchain." They enforce rules automatically: if someone tries to transfer tokens to a non-<a href="kyc.html">KYC</a>'d wallet, the contract rejects it. When <a href="distributions.html">dividends</a> are due, the contract calculates and sends them to every holder instantly.</p>
                <p>On Sails.to, our smart contracts on <a href="solana.html">Solana</a> handle everything from compliance checks to <a href="cap-table.html">cap table</a> management. The rules are transparent, auditable, and execute the same way every time—no human error, no delays, no favoritism.</p>
