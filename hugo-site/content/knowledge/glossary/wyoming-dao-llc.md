        <section class="term-section">
            <h2>Full Definition</h2>
            <p>Wyoming became the first US state to legally recognize DAOs as a distinct business entity type in 2021. A Wyoming DAO LLC combines the flexibility of smart contract governance with the legal protections of a traditional LLC, including limited liability for members. This structure is ideal for tokenized securities as it provides clear legal ownership, tax treatment, and regulatory standing while enabling on-chain governance and automated operations.</p>
        </section>
        <section class="term-section">
            <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
            <p>The Wyoming DAO LLC is what makes Sails.to possible. By operating as a legal entity—not just code—we can sign contracts, hold assets, sue and be sued, and interact with the traditional financial system. Token holders have clear legal rights, not just cryptographic claims.</p>
            <p>Combined with the <a href="/knowledge/glossary/series-llc/">Series LLC</a> structure, this allows us to create isolated <a href="/knowledge/glossary/spv/">SPVs</a> for each offering while maintaining a single administrative umbrella. It's the best of both worlds: blockchain efficiency with legal certainty.</p>
