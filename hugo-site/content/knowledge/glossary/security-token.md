            <section class="term-section">
                <h2>Full Definition</h2>
                <p>Security tokens are blockchain-based digital assets that represent ownership in real-world securities such as company shares, bonds, real estate, or fund interests. Unlike utility tokens, security tokens are subject to securities regulations and must comply with laws like Reg D, Reg S, or Reg A+ in the US. They combine the benefits of traditional securities (legal rights, dividends, voting) with blockchain advantages (24/7 trading, fractional ownership, automated compliance).</p>
            </section>
            <section class="term-section">
                <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
                <p>Security tokens represent the evolution of capital markets infrastructure. By encoding compliance rules directly into <a href="smart-contract.html">smart contracts</a>, they eliminate the need for intermediaries while maintaining regulatory compliance. Investors benefit from increased liquidity through fractional ownership, while issuers gain access to global capital markets with reduced costs.</p>
