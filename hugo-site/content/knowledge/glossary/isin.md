                <h2>Full Definition</h2>
                <p>The International Securities Identification Number is the global standard for identifying securities, administered by national numbering agencies. When a security token receives an ISIN, it can be recognized by traditional financial infrastructure including custodian banks, clearinghouses, and securities depositories. This "TradFi bridge" enables investors to hold tokenized securities in their existing brokerage accounts.</p>
