            <section class="term-section">
                <h2>Full Definition</h2>
                <p>Know Your Customer (KYC) is a mandatory due diligence process that financial institutions and regulated platforms must perform to verify client identities. This typically involves collecting government-issued ID, proof of address, and source of funds documentation. For security token platforms, KYC ensures only eligible investors can participate in offerings and helps maintain compliance with securities regulations.</p>
            </section>
