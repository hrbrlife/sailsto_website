<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Full Definition</h2>
    <p>Secondary trading occurs when investors trade previously issued securities among themselves, rather than buying directly from the issuer. For tokenized securities, secondary markets can operate 24/7 on blockchain-based exchanges, with smart contracts automatically enforcing transfer restrictions and compliance. Sails.to's broker network facilitates compliant secondary trading, matching buyers with sellers while ensuring all parties meet eligibility requirements.</p>

    <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
