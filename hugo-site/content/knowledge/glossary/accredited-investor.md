<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Full Definition</h2>
    <p>Under SEC regulations, accredited investors are deemed financially sophisticated enough to understand and bear the risks of unregistered securities. Individuals qualify with annual income exceeding $200,000 ($300,000 joint) for the past two years, or net worth over $1 million excluding primary residence. Certain entities, licensed professionals, and company insiders also qualify. Most Reg D offerings are limited to accredited investors.</p>
</section>
<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Qualification Criteria</h2>
    <p><strong>Individuals qualify if they meet ANY of these:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>Annual income over $200,000 ($300,000 with spouse) for past 2 years with reasonable expectation of the same</li>
        <li>Net worth over $1 million (excluding primary residence)</li>
        <li>Holder of Series 7, 65, or 82 licenses</li>
        <li>Director, executive officer, or general partner of the issuer</li>
        <li>"Knowledgeable employees" of private funds</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>Entities qualify if they:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li>Have assets over $5 million (for trusts and other entities)</li>
        <li>Are banks, insurance companies, or registered investment companies</li>
        <li>Are family offices with $5M+ in assets</li>
        <li>Are entities owned entirely by accredited investors</li>
    </ul>
</section>
<section class="term-section">
    <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
    <p>US securities laws require <a href="/knowledge/glossary/reg-d/">Reg D 506(c)</a> offerings to verify that all investors are accredited. This isn't just a checkbox—it's a legal requirement with real consequences for issuers who get it wrong.</p>
    <p>Sails.to works with third-party verification services to confirm accredited status during <a href="/knowledge/glossary/kyc/">KYC</a>. Non-US investors may qualify under <a href="/knowledge/glossary/reg-s/">Reg S</a> without meeting US accreditation thresholds, instead qualifying as <a href="/knowledge/glossary/professional-investor/">professional investors</a> under their local regulations.</p>
</section>
